# Can a Balinese Cat Eat Grapes?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

## Why You Should Never Feed Grapes to Your Balinese
Regardless of the breed, **grapes and raisins are highly toxic to cats**, including the elegant Balinese. While some animals can process them, cats cannot. Ingesting even a small amount of grapes can lead to sudden and severe **acute kidney failure**.

The exact toxin responsible is not fully understood, but recent research suggests that **tartaric acid** in grapes is the primary culprit, which can cause rapid renal distress in felines.

## The Risks: What Happens if a Balinese Eats Grapes?
Because Balinese cats are active and curious, they may try to sneak a grape off the counter. The risks are severe:

*   **Kidney Failure:** The most critical risk; the kidneys may stop functioning entirely.
*   **Gastrointestinal Distress:** Vomiting and diarrhea are often the first signs of toxicity.
*   **Lethargy:** Your cat may become unusually tired or depressed.
*   **Loss of Appetite:** Refusing food or water.
*   **Dehydration:** Due to kidney dysfunction and vomiting.

## What to Do If Your Balinese Ate a Grape
If you suspect your cat has ingested a grape or raisin, **treat this as a medical emergency.** Do not wait for symptoms to appear, as early intervention is key to saving their kidney function.

1.  **Call Your Veterinarian Immediately:** Contact your vet or an emergency animal clinic right away.
2.  **Provide Details:** Tell the vet how many grapes were eaten, the variety (if known), and how long ago it happened.
3.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting:** Never attempt to make your cat vomit unless specifically instructed to do so by a professional.
4.  **Monitor Behavior:** Watch for signs of weakness or changes in urination patterns while you are en route to the clinic.

### Safe Alternatives for Your Balinese
If you want to give your Balinese a healthy treat, skip the fruit bowl and try these cat-safe options:
*   **Cooked Chicken or Turkey** (Plain, no seasoning)
*   **Small pieces of Steamed Pumpkin**
*   **Commercial Cat Treats** specifically formulated for feline nutrition
