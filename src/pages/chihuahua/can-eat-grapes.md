# Can a Chihuahua Eat Grapes? 🚫

**Verdict: NO.** 

**Chihuahuas should never eat grapes or raisins.** Regardless of the size of the dog or the type of grape, these fruits are highly toxic to canines.

---

## Why Are Grapes Dangerous for Chihuahuas?
While many fruits are healthy treats, grapes and raisins contain concentrated amounts of **tartaric acid**. In dogs, this compound can trigger sudden, acute **kidney failure**. 

Because Chihuahuas are toy-sized, their organs are smaller and can be overwhelmed more quickly by toxins than larger breeds, making this a critical emergency.

## Potential Risks & Symptoms
If a Chihuahua ingests even a single grape, they are at risk of severe renal distress. Watch for these warning signs:

*   **Vomiting and Diarrhea:** Often the first sign of toxicity.
*   **Lethargy:** Unusual tiredness or weakness.
*   **Loss of Appetite:** Refusing their favorite treats or meals.
*   **Dehydration:** Increased thirst or, conversely, a complete stop in urination (a sign of kidney failure).
*   **Muscle Tremors:** In severe cases of poisoning.

---

## What to Do If Your Chihuahua Ate a Grape
**Time is of the essence.** Kidney damage can happen rapidly, so immediate action is required.

1.  **Call Your Vet Immediately:** Contact your veterinarian or a local emergency animal clinic right away.
2.  **Contact Pet Poison Control:** If your vet is unavailable, call the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center.
3.  **Do NOT Induce Vomiting (Unless Instructed):** Never give your dog peroxide or try to make them vomit unless a professional tells you to do so over the phone.
4.  **Gather Information:** Try to determine exactly how many grapes were eaten and how long ago it happened.

### Safe, Chihuahua-Friendly Alternatives
If you want to give your tiny companion a fruity snack, try these safe, vet-approved options instead:
*   **Blueberries** (Great antioxidant boost)
*   **Apple Slices** (Seedless and skinless)
*   **Watermelon** (Seedless and in moderation)
*   **Banana** (Small pieces)
