# Can a St. Bernard Eat Macadamia Nuts?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

## Why Macadamia Nuts Are Dangerous for St. Bernards
Despite their massive size, St. Bernards are not immune to the toxins found in macadamia nuts. Macadamia nuts contain compounds that are **toxic to dogs**, affecting their nervous system and overall metabolic function. While a giant breed like a St. Bernard may require a larger quantity to show severe symptoms compared to a Chihuahua, the risks remain the same and can be life-threatening.

## Potential Risks & Symptoms
There are **zero nutritional benefits** for a dog to eat macadamia nuts; the risks far outweigh any potential gain. If your St. Bernard consumes these nuts, you may notice the following symptoms:

*   **Hind Leg Weakness:** Difficulty walking or "wobbly" movement.
*   **Vomiting & Diarrhea:** Gastrointestinal distress is common.
*   **Lethargy:** Extreme tiredness or depression.
*   **Hyperthermia:** An increase in body temperature.
*   **Tremors:** In severe cases, muscle tremors or seizures may occur.

## What to Do If Your St. Bernard Ate Macadamia Nuts
If you suspect your dog has ingested macadamia nuts, **do not wait for symptoms to appear.** Take the following steps immediately:

1.  **Contact Your Veterinarian:** Call your vet or an emergency animal clinic right away.
2.  **Call Pet Poison Helpline:** You can contact the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center for immediate guidance.
3.  **Identify the Amount:** Try to determine exactly how many nuts your dog ate and when it happened. This helps the vet determine the level of toxicity.
4.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting:** Unless specifically instructed to do so by a veterinary professional, do not attempt to make your dog throw up.

### Safe Alternatives for Your Giant Breed
If you want to give your St. Bernard a crunchy, healthy treat, opt for these dog-safe alternatives:
*   **Carrot sticks** (Low calorie and great for teeth)
*   **Apple slices** (Seedless and coreless)
*   **Plain cooked pumpkin** (Great for digestion)
*   **Blueberries** (Packed with antioxidants)
