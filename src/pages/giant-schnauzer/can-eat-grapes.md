# Can Giant Schnauzers Eat Grapes? 🍇

**Verdict: NO ❌**

Giant Schnauzers, like all dog breeds, **must never eat grapes or raisins.** 

---

### Why Grapes Are Dangerous for Giant Schnauzers
While many fruits are healthy treats, grapes and raisins contain a specific toxin (recently identified as tartaric acid) that is highly toxic to dogs. Even though the Giant Schnauzer is a large breed, toxicity is not always dose-dependent; **even a single grape can trigger acute kidney failure** in some dogs.

### Potential Risks & Symptoms
Feeding your Giant Schnauzer grapes can lead to rapid organ failure. Keep a close eye on your dog for these warning signs:

*   **Gastrointestinal Distress:** Sudden vomiting or diarrhea.
*   **Lethargy:** Unusual tiredness or weakness.
*   **Loss of Appetite:** Refusing their favorite meals.
*   **Dehydration:** Increased thirst or, conversely, a complete stop in urine production (a sign of kidney failure).

### What to Do If Your Giant Schnauzer Ate Grapes
If your dog has ingested a grape or raisin, **treat this as a medical emergency.** Do not wait for symptoms to appear, as kidney damage can happen quickly.

1.  **Call Your Vet Immediately:** Contact your primary veterinarian or the nearest emergency animal clinic.
2.  **Contact Pet Poison Control:** Call the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center or a similar hotline.
3.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting Unless Instructed:** Only induce vomiting if your vet specifically tells you to do so over the phone.
4.  **Gather Information:** Try to determine exactly how many grapes were eaten and how long ago it happened.

### Safe Alternatives for Your Giant Schnauzer
If you want to give your Giant Schnauzer a fruity treat, try these dog-safe and nutritionist-approved options instead:
*   **Blueberries** (Rich in antioxidants)
*   **Apple Slices** (Seedless and skinless)
*   **Watermelon** (Seedless and rind-free)
*   **Banana** (In moderation)
