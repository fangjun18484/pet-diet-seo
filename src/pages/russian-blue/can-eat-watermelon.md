# Can a Russian Blue Eat Watermelon?

**Verdict: YES** ✅ (In moderation and prepared correctly)

## Why Watermelon is Safe for Russian Blues
Watermelon is non-toxic to cats, including the elegant Russian Blue. Since it consists of about 92% water, it is a refreshing, low-calorie snack that can provide a bit of variety to your cat's diet. While cats are obligate carnivores and don't *need* fruit, a small piece of watermelon is perfectly safe.

---

## Potential Benefits & Risks

### The Benefits 🌟
*   **Hydration Boost:** Because of its high water content, it's a great treat for cats who struggle to drink enough water.
*   **Vitamin Intake:** It contains small amounts of vitamins A and C.
*   **Low Calorie:** It is a lighter alternative to processed commercial treats.

### The Risks ⚠️
*   **High Sugar Content:** Watermelon contains natural sugars. Overfeeding can lead to weight gain or spike blood glucose levels, which is dangerous for cats with **diabetes**.
*   **Digestive Upset:** Some cats may experience diarrhea or an upset stomach if they eat too much fruit.
*   **Choking Hazards:** Seeds and the tough rind are not digestible and can cause intestinal blockages.

---

## How to Safely Serve Watermelon to Your Cat

If you want to share a slice of summer with your Russian Blue, follow these strict preparation rules:

1.  **Remove All Seeds:** Ensure every single seed (black or white) is removed to prevent choking or intestinal obstruction.
2.  **Discard the Rind:** Only feed the red, fleshy part. The green rind is too hard to digest and can cause gastrointestinal distress.
3.  **Cut into Tiny Cubes:** Serve small, bite-sized pieces (about the size of a blueberry) to avoid choking.
4.  **Moderation is Key:** Treat watermelon as a **rare snack**, not a meal. One or two small cubes once or twice a week is plenty.

**Pro Tip:** If your Russian Blue is picky, try freezing the watermelon cubes for a refreshing, cooling treat during the summer months!
