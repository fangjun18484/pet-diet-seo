# Can Oriental Shorthairs Eat Grapes? The Essential Safety Guide

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

Oriental Shorthairs, like all cat breeds, **must never eat grapes or raisins.**

---

## Why Grapes Are Dangerous for Cats
While some fruits are safe treats, grapes and raisins are highly toxic to felines. Recent veterinary research suggests that **tartaric acid** found in grapes is the primary culprit. 

Because cats have a unique metabolic system, they cannot process this compound, which can lead to sudden and severe **acute kidney failure**. This toxicity occurs regardless of the amount consumed; even a single grape can be dangerous for a small cat.

---

## Potential Risks & Symptoms
There are no benefits to feeding grapes to an Oriental Shorthair. The risks are severe and potentially fatal. If your cat ingests a grape, watch for these warning signs:

*   **Vomiting and Diarrhea:** Often the first sign of distress.
*   **Lethargy:** Unusual tiredness or weakness.
*   **Loss of Appetite:** Refusing their favorite treats or meals.
*   **Changes in Urination:** Decreased urine output (a sign of kidney failure).
*   **Dehydration:** Dry gums and skin.

---

## What to Do If Your Cat Ate a Grape
If your Oriental Shorthair has accidentally consumed a grape or raisin, **treat this as a medical emergency.**

1.  **Call Your Vet Immediately:** Contact your primary veterinarian or the nearest emergency animal clinic.
2.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting:** Never attempt to make your cat vomit unless specifically instructed to do so by a professional.
3.  **Provide Information:** Tell the vet exactly how many grapes were eaten and how long ago it happened.
4.  **Monitor Closely:** Keep a close eye on their behavior and hydration levels until you reach the clinic.

### Safe Alternatives for Your Oriental Shorthair
If you want to give your curious Oriental Shorthair a healthy snack, try these cat-safe options in moderation:
*   **Blueberries** (Small amounts)
*   **Cooked Pumpkin** (Plain, no sugar/spices)
*   **Watermelon** (Seedless and rind-free)
*   **Commercial Cat Treats** (Vet-approved)
