# Can a Burmese Cat Eat Grapes?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

---

### Why Burmese Cats Cannot Eat Grapes
Regardless of the breed, **grapes and raisins are highly toxic to cats**, including Burmese cats. While the exact toxin is not fully understood, research suggests that **tartaric acid** found in grapes can cause sudden and severe kidney failure in feline companions. 

Because Burmese cats are known for their curious and active personalities, it is crucial to keep these fruits completely out of their reach.

---

### The Risks: What Happens if They Eat Grapes?
There are **no nutritional benefits** to feeding grapes to a cat; the risks far outweigh any perceived gain. Ingesting even a small amount of a grape or raisin can lead to:

*   **Acute Renal Failure:** The primary and most dangerous risk is the sudden shutdown of the kidneys.
*   **Gastrointestinal Distress:** Immediate vomiting and diarrhea.
*   **Lethargy:** A sudden drop in energy levels and unusual tiredness.
*   **Loss of Appetite:** Refusing food and water.
*   **Dehydration:** Due to the kidneys' inability to process fluids correctly.

---

### What to Do if Your Burmese Cat Ate a Grape
If you suspect your cat has ingested a grape or raisin, **treat this as a veterinary emergency.** Do not wait for symptoms to appear, as kidney damage can happen rapidly.

1.  **Call Your Vet Immediately:** Contact your primary veterinarian or the nearest emergency animal hospital.
2.  **Provide Details:** Tell the vet approximately how many grapes were eaten and how long ago the incident occurred.
3.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting:** Never attempt to make your cat vomit unless specifically instructed to do so by a medical professional.
4.  **Monitor for Symptoms:** Keep a close eye on their behavior and hydration levels while transporting them to the clinic.

### Safe Alternatives for Your Burmese
If you want to give your Burmese cat a healthy treat, skip the vineyard and try these cat-safe options:
*   **Blueberries** (in moderation)
*   **Cooked Pumpkin** (plain, no sugar)
*   **Small pieces of cooked chicken** (unseasoned)
