# Can Boston Terriers Eat Grapes? 🍇

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

Boston Terriers, like all dog breeds, **should never eat grapes or raisins.** Even a small amount can be highly toxic and potentially fatal.

---

## Why Are Grapes Dangerous for Boston Terriers?
Grapes and raisins contain substances that can cause **acute kidney failure** in dogs. While the exact toxin was long debated, recent research points to **tartaric acid** as the primary culprit. 

Because Boston Terriers are small-to-medium-sized dogs, their bodies can be overwhelmed by these toxins more quickly than larger breeds, making the risk even more critical.

---

## The Risks: What Happens if They Eat Grapes?
There are no health benefits to feeding grapes to your dog—only severe risks. If a Boston Terrier consumes grapes, they may experience:

*   **Kidney Failure:** The most severe risk, where the kidneys stop filtering waste from the blood.
*   **Gastrointestinal Distress:** Sudden vomiting and diarrhea.
*   **Lethargy:** Extreme tiredness or weakness.
*   **Loss of Appetite:** Refusing food or water.
*   **Dehydration:** Due to the inability of the kidneys to regulate fluids.

---

## 🚨 What to Do If Your Boston Terrier Ate Grapes
If your dog has ingested a grape or raisin, **treat this as a medical emergency.** Do not wait for symptoms to appear, as kidney damage can happen rapidly.

1.  **Call Your Vet Immediately:** Contact your primary veterinarian or the nearest emergency animal clinic.
2.  **Contact Poison Control:** You can call the *ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center* or the *Pet Poison Helpline*.
3.  **Provide Details:** Be ready to tell the vet how many grapes were eaten, the approximate time of ingestion, and your dog's weight.
4.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting** unless specifically instructed to do so by a veterinary professional.

### Safe Alternatives for Your Boston Terrier
If you want to give your Boston Terrier a fruity treat, try these dog-safe options instead:
*   **Blueberries** (Rich in antioxidants)
*   **Apple slices** (Seedless and skinless)
*   **Watermelon** (Seedless and rind-free)
*   **Banana slices** (In moderation)
