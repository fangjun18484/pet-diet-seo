# Can a Boston Terrier Eat Macadamia Nuts?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

## Why Macadamia Nuts Are Dangerous for Boston Terriers
You should **never** feed macadamia nuts to your Boston Terrier. Unlike some nuts that are simply hard to digest, macadamia nuts contain a toxin that is specifically harmful to dogs. 

Regardless of the breed's size or health status, macadamia nuts can cause a systemic reaction that affects the nervous system, leading to a condition known as **macadamia nut toxicosis**.

## The Risks: What Happens if They Eat One?
Even a small amount of macadamia nuts can be dangerous for a small-to-medium breed like the Boston Terrier. Because of their size, they are more susceptible to higher concentrations of the toxin per pound of body weight.

### Potential Symptoms include:
*   **Lethargy:** Extreme tiredness or weakness.
*   **Hind Leg Weakness:** Difficulty walking or "drunk" movement (ataxia).
*   **Vomiting:** Sudden nausea and stomach upset.
*   **Hyperthermia:** An increase in body temperature.
*   **Tremors:** In severe cases, muscle tremors or seizures may occur.

**Note:** Symptoms typically appear within 12 hours of ingestion.

## What to Do if Your Boston Terrier Ate Macadamia Nuts
If you suspect your dog has consumed macadamia nuts, **treat it as a medical emergency.**

1.  **Remove Access:** Immediately take away any remaining nuts to prevent further ingestion.
2.  **Call Your Vet:** Contact your veterinarian or an emergency animal clinic immediately.
3.  **Provide Details:** Be ready to tell the vet how many nuts were eaten, the approximate time of ingestion, and your dog's current weight.
4.  **Monitor:** Keep a close eye on their mobility and energy levels until you reach the clinic.

## Safe, Crunchy Alternatives for Your Boston Terrier
Since your Boston Terrier can't have macadamia nuts, try these dog-safe, healthy treats instead:
*   **Carrot Sticks:** Low calorie and great for chewing.
*   **Apple Slices:** (Ensure seeds and core are removed).
*   **Plain Cucumber:** A hydrating, crunchy snack.
*   **Blueberries:** Packed with antioxidants and safe in moderation.
