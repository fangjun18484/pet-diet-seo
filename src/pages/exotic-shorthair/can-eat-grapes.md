# Can Exotic Shorthairs Eat Grapes?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

## Why You Should Never Feed Grapes to Your Exotic Shorthair
Regardless of the breed, **grapes and raisins are highly toxic to all cats**, including the Exotic Shorthair. 

While the exact toxin is still being studied, recent research suggests that **tartaric acid** in grapes can cause sudden and acute kidney failure in felines. Because Exotic Shorthairs are a brachycephalic (flat-faced) breed, they may already have specific health sensitivities; adding a nephrotoxic (kidney-damaging) food to their diet is extremely dangerous.

---

## The Risks: What Happens if They Eat Grapes?
There is no "safe" amount of grapes for a cat. Even a single grape or raisin can trigger a severe reaction. The primary risks include:

*   **Acute Kidney Failure:** The most severe risk, which can be fatal if not treated immediately.
*   **Gastrointestinal Distress:** Severe vomiting and diarrhea.
*   **Lethargy:** Sudden loss of energy and depression.
*   **Loss of Appetite:** Refusing food and water.
*   **Dehydration:** Due to the kidneys' inability to process fluids.

---

## What to Do If Your Exotic Shorthair Ate a Grape
If you suspect your cat has ingested a grape or raisin, **treat this as a medical emergency.** Do not wait for symptoms to appear, as kidney damage can happen quickly.

1.  **Call Your Veterinarian Immediately:** Or contact a pet poison control center.
2.  **Note the Quantity:** Try to determine how many grapes were eaten and how long ago.
3.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting:** Never attempt to make your cat vomit unless explicitly instructed to do so by a licensed veterinarian.
4.  **Seek Emergency Care:** Your vet may administer activated charcoal or IV fluids to flush the toxins from the system.

---

## Safe, Cat-Friendly Alternatives
If you want to give your Exotic Shorthair a healthy treat, skip the vineyard and try these vet-approved options:

*   **Blueberries:** A safe, antioxidant-rich alternative (in moderation).
*   **Cooked Pumpkin:** Great for digestion (plain, no sugar/spices).
*   **Freeze-Dried Chicken:** A high-protein treat they will love.
*   **Catnip or Silvervine:** A safe way to provide mental stimulation.
