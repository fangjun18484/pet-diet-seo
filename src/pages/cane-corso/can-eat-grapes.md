# Can a Cane Corso Eat Grapes?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

A Cane Corso, regardless of its size or strength, **cannot and should not eat grapes.** Grapes and raisins are highly toxic to all dog breeds.

---

### Why Grapes Are Dangerous for Cane Corsos
While many fruits are healthy treats, grapes (and their concentrated form, raisins) contain chemicals—specifically **tartaric acid**—that can trigger sudden and severe **acute kidney failure** in dogs. 

Unlike some toxins that are dose-dependent based on weight, grapes can affect dogs differently; some may eat a large amount with no reaction, while others may experience kidney failure from just one or two grapes. You cannot risk it with your Cane Corso.

---

### Potential Risks & Symptoms
If your Cane Corso consumes grapes, the primary risk is **renal failure**, which can be fatal if left untreated. Watch for these warning signs:

*   **Vomiting and Diarrhea:** Often occurs within a few hours of ingestion.
*   **Lethargy:** Unusual tiredness or weakness.
*   **Loss of Appetite:** Refusing their favorite meals.
*   **Changes in Urination:** Either a significant decrease in urine output or excessive urination.
*   **Dehydration:** Dry gums and skin.

---

### What to Do If Your Cane Corso Ate Grapes
Since grape toxicity is a medical emergency, speed is critical. Follow these steps immediately:

1.  **Call Your Veterinarian or an Emergency Pet Clinic:** Do not wait for symptoms to appear.
2.  **Contact Pet Poison Helpline:** If your vet is unavailable, call a dedicated animal poison control center.
3.  **Provide Details:** Have the approximate number of grapes consumed and the time of ingestion ready for the vet.
4.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting** unless explicitly told to do so by a professional, as this can sometimes cause further complications.

### Safe Alternatives for Your Cane Corso
If you want to give your dog a fruity treat, try these vet-approved options instead:
*   **Blueberries** (Rich in antioxidants)
*   **Apple Slices** (Remove seeds and core)
*   **Watermelon** (Remove seeds and rind)
*   **Banana** (In moderation)
