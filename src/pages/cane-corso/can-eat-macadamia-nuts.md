# Can a Cane Corso Eat Macadamia Nuts?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

***

## Why Macadamia Nuts Are Dangerous for Cane Corsos
Regardless of their size and strength, **Cane Corsos cannot eat macadamia nuts.** These nuts contain a toxin that is specifically harmful to dogs, leading to a condition known as *macadamia nut toxicosis*. 

While a large breed like the Cane Corso may require a larger quantity of nuts to show severe symptoms compared to a small dog, the risk is too high to ignore. The toxins target the nervous system, potentially leading to debilitating physical reactions.

## Potential Risks and Symptoms
There are **no nutritional benefits** for a dog to eat macadamia nuts; the risks far outweigh any perceived protein or fat gains. If your Cane Corso ingests macadamia nuts, look for these warning signs:

*   **Lethargy:** Sudden extreme tiredness or weakness.
*   **Hind Leg Weakness:** Difficulty walking or "stumbling."
*   **Vomiting:** Nausea and gastrointestinal distress.
*   **Hyperthermia:** An increase in body temperature (fever).
*   **Tremors:** In severe cases, muscle tremors or disorientation.

## What to Do If Your Cane Corso Ate Macadamia Nuts
If you suspect your dog has consumed macadamia nuts (either plain or as part of a cookie or dessert), take the following steps immediately:

1.  **Remove Access:** Ensure no more nuts are available.
2.  **Call Your Veterinarian:** Contact your vet or an emergency animal clinic immediately.
3.  **Contact Pet Poison Control:** Call the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center or a similar emergency line.
4.  **Monitor Closely:** Keep a close eye on their movement and energy levels while waiting for professional medical advice.
5.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting:** Unless specifically instructed to do so by a veterinarian, do not attempt to make your dog vomit.

### Safe Alternatives for Your Cane Corso
If you want to give your Cane Corso a crunchy, healthy treat, try these dog-safe options instead:
*   **Carrot sticks** (Great for chewing)
*   **Apple slices** (Without seeds)
*   **Plain cooked pumpkin**
*   **Blueberries**
