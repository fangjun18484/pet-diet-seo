# Can English Springer Spaniels Eat Grapes?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

English Springer Spaniels, like all dog breeds, **should never eat grapes or raisins.** Whether fresh, dried, or seedless, grapes are highly toxic to dogs and can lead to severe health complications.

---

### Why Are Grapes Dangerous for Dogs?
While the exact compound causing the toxicity was long debated, recent research suggests that **tartaric acid** found in grapes and raisins is the culprit. 

Unlike some foods that are toxic in large quantities, grapes can cause **acute kidney failure** in dogs, and some dogs are more sensitive than others. Even a single grape can be enough to trigger a life-threatening reaction in some pets.

### Potential Risks & Symptoms
If your English Springer Spaniel consumes grapes, they are at risk of sudden renal failure. Watch for these warning signs:

*   **Vomiting and Diarrhea:** Usually occurs shortly after ingestion.
*   **Lethargy:** Unusual tiredness or lack of energy.
*   **Loss of Appetite:** Refusing their favorite treats or meals.
*   **Dehydration:** Increased thirst or, conversely, a decrease in urine output.
*   **Joint Pain or Weakness:** In advanced stages of kidney failure.

---

### What to Do If Your Dog Ate Grapes
If your English Springer Spaniel has accidentally ingested a grape or raisin, **act quickly.** This is a veterinary emergency.

1.  **Call Your Vet Immediately:** Contact your primary veterinarian or the nearest emergency animal clinic.
2.  **Contact Pet Poison Control:** You can call the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center or a similar service for immediate guidance.
3.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting Unless Instructed:** Only induce vomiting if your vet specifically tells you to do so over the phone.
4.  **Gather Information:** Try to determine exactly how many grapes were eaten and how long ago it happened.

### Safe, Healthy Alternatives
Since English Springer Spaniels are active, food-motivated dogs, try these grape-safe alternatives for a healthy snack:
*   **Blueberries:** Packed with antioxidants and safe in moderation.
*   **Apple Slices:** (Remove the seeds and core first).
*   **Watermelon:** (Remove the seeds and rind).
*   **Carrot Sticks:** Great for chewing and low in calories.
