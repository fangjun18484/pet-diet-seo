# Can Yorkshire Terriers Eat Peanut Butter?

**Verdict: YES (But with caution!)**

## The Quick Answer
Yes, Yorkshire Terriers can eat peanut butter, but it must be offered in moderation and be free of specific toxic additives. While peanut butter is a great high-value treat for training your Yorkie, their small size makes them more susceptible to weight gain and digestive upset.

---

## Why Peanut Butter is Okay (and When it Isn't)
Most peanut butters are safe for dogs because they provide protein and healthy fats. However, because Yorkies are a toy breed, a single tablespoon of peanut butter represents a much larger percentage of their daily caloric intake than it would for a Golden Retriever.

### 🌟 Potential Benefits
*   **High-Value Reward:** Perfect for hiding pills or as a motivator during training.
*   **Healthy Fats:** Contains omega-3 and omega-6 fatty acids which can support a shiny Yorkie coat.
*   **Protein Boost:** A source of plant-based protein.

### ⚠️ Potential Risks
*   **Xylitol Toxicity:** Some "low sugar" or "diet" peanut butters contain **Xylitol (birch sugar)**, an artificial sweetener that is **lethal** to dogs.
*   **Pancreatitis:** The high fat content can trigger pancreatitis in sensitive dogs, especially small breeds like Yorkies.
*   **Obesity:** Excessive calories can lead to rapid weight gain.
*   **Excess Sodium:** Salted peanut butters can strain a small dog's kidneys.

---

## How to Safely Feed Peanut Butter to Your Yorkie

If you want to treat your Yorkie, follow these strict safety guidelines:

### 1. Read the Ingredient Label (Crucial!)
Check the label for **Xylitol** (sometimes listed as *birch sugar*). If you see it, **do not feed it to your dog.** The safest choice is a natural peanut butter where the only ingredient is "peanuts."

### 2. Avoid Added Sugars and Salts
Choose unsalted and unsweetened varieties to prevent sodium overload and unnecessary weight gain.

### 3. Control the Portion Size
Because Yorkies are tiny, stick to a **small smear (about 1/2 to 1 teaspoon)**. Treat it as a snack, not a meal.

### 4. Monitor for Allergies
Like humans, some dogs are allergic to peanuts. Give a tiny amount first and watch for itching, swelling, or hives.
