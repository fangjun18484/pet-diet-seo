# Can Yorkshire Terriers Eat Blueberries? 🫐

**Verdict: YES** ✅

Yorkshire Terriers can safely eat blueberries. These tiny berries are non-toxic and can be a healthy, nutrient-dense addition to your Yorkie's diet.

---

## Why Blueberries Are Safe for Yorkies
Blueberries are packed with vitamins and minerals that are beneficial for dogs. Because they are low in calories and high in water content, they make an excellent low-calorie treat for small breeds like Yorkies, who can be prone to weight gain.

## Potential Benefits & Risks

### 🌟 The Benefits
*   **Antioxidants:** Rich in anthocyanins, which help fight free radicals and support cognitive function.
*   **Immune Support:** Contains Vitamin C and K to boost the immune system and support bone health.
*   **Fiber:** Helps maintain a healthy digestive tract.
*   **Low Calorie:** A great alternative to processed dog treats.

### ⚠️ The Risks
*   **Sugar Content:** While natural, blueberries contain sugar. Overfeeding can lead to weight gain or spike blood glucose levels in diabetic dogs.
*   **Choking Hazard:** Because Yorkies have very small throats, a whole blueberry could potentially cause choking if swallowed too quickly.
*   **Digestive Upset:** Introducing too many berries too quickly may cause loose stools in sensitive dogs.

---

## How to Safely Feed Blueberries to Your Yorkie

To ensure your Yorkie enjoys their treat safely, follow these expert guidelines:

1.  **Wash Thoroughly:** Always rinse blueberries to remove any pesticides or contaminants.
2.  **Portion Control:** Treat blueberries as a *treat*, not a meal. For a small breed like a Yorkie, **2–3 blueberries per day** is typically sufficient.
3.  **Cut Them in Half:** To prevent choking, consider slicing the berries in half for the smallest Yorkies.
4.  **Avoid Additives:** Only feed fresh or frozen blueberries. **Never** feed your dog blueberries from a pie, jam, or dried versions containing added sugars or xylitol (a toxic sweetener).

### 💡 Pro Tip: The Frozen Treat
On a hot day, freeze a few blueberries and give them to your Yorkie. It provides a refreshing, crunchy texture that helps keep them hydrated and occupied!
