# Can a Yorkshire Terrier Eat Apples?

**Verdict: ✅ YES**

Yorkshire Terriers can safely enjoy apples as a healthy, crunchy snack. However, because Yorkies are a toy breed with small throats and sensitive stomachs, preparation is key to avoiding choking hazards and digestive upset.

---

## Why Apples are Safe for Yorkies
Apples are non-toxic to dogs and provide a refreshing alternative to processed store-bought treats. They contain essential vitamins and minerals that support a dog's overall health without adding excessive calories to their diet.

## The Benefits & Risks

### 🌟 The Benefits
*   **Rich in Vitamins:** Apples provide Vitamin A and Vitamin C, which support the immune system.
*   **Fiber Boost:** The fiber in apples aids in healthy digestion.
*   **Low Calorie:** A great treat for Yorkies prone to weight gain.
*   **Dental Health:** The crunch can help reduce tartar buildup on the teeth.

### ⚠️ The Risks
*   **Cyanide Toxicity:** Apple seeds contain amygdalin, which releases cyanide when chewed. While a few seeds may not harm a large dog, they are more dangerous for a tiny Yorkie.
*   **Choking Hazard:** The hard core and large chunks can easily lodge in a Yorkie's small throat.
*   **Sugar Content:** Apples contain natural sugars. Overfeeding can lead to weight gain or upset the stomach of dogs with diabetes.

---

## How to Safely Feed Apples to Your Yorkie

To ensure your Yorkie enjoys their treat without any danger, follow these preparation steps:

1.  **Wash Thoroughly:** Remove any pesticides or wax from the skin.
2.  **Core and De-seed:** Completely remove the core and every single seed.
3.  **Cut into Tiny Pieces:** Because Yorkies are small, slice the apple into **small, bite-sized cubes** to prevent choking.
4.  **Practice Moderation:** Apples should be treated as a snack, not a meal. Limit treats to **10% of their daily caloric intake**.
5.  **Avoid Apple Products:** Never feed your dog apple pie, apple crisps with added sugar, or processed apple snacks containing Xylitol (a toxic sweetener).

**Pro Tip:** Try freezing apple slices for a refreshing, long-lasting treat during the summer months!
