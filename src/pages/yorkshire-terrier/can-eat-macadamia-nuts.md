# Can Yorkshire Terriers Eat Macadamia Nuts?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

## Why Macadamia Nuts are Dangerous for Yorkies
You should **never** feed macadamia nuts to your Yorkshire Terrier. Unlike some nuts that are simply hard to digest, macadamia nuts contain a specific toxin (though the exact compound is not fully understood) that is poisonous to all dogs, regardless of breed.

Because Yorkies are toy breeds with small body masses, even a single macadamia nut can trigger a toxic reaction more quickly than it would in a larger dog.

## The Risks: Symptoms of Macadamia Nut Poisoning
If your Yorkie ingests macadamia nuts, they may experience "macadamia nut toxicosis." Symptoms typically appear within 12 hours and include:

*   **Lethargy:** Unusual tiredness or weakness.
*   **Hind Leg Weakness:** Difficulty walking or "wobbly" back legs.
*   **Vomiting:** Sudden gastrointestinal distress.
*   **Hyperthermia:** An increase in body temperature.
*   **Tremors:** In severe cases, muscle tremors or disorientation.

**Note:** There are no nutritional benefits to macadamia nuts for dogs; the risks far outweigh any potential calorie or fat gain.

## What to Do If Your Yorkie Ate Macadamia Nuts
If you suspect your dog has eaten macadamia nuts, **act quickly**. Follow these steps:

1.  **Remove Access:** Immediately take any remaining nuts away from your dog.
2.  **Call Your Vet:** Contact your veterinarian or an emergency animal clinic right away.
3.  **Provide Details:** Be ready to tell the vet how many nuts were eaten, the approximate time of ingestion, and your dog's current weight.
4.  **Monitor Closely:** Watch for the symptoms listed above while you are en route to the clinic.

### Safe Alternatives for Your Yorkie
If you want to give your Yorkie a crunchy, healthy treat, try these dog-safe options instead:
*   **Blueberries** (Rich in antioxidants)
*   **Carrot sticks** (Great for dental health)
*   **Apple slices** (Seedless and skinless)
*   **Plain cooked pumpkin** (Great for digestion)
