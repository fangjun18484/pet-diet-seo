# Can a Yorkshire Terrier Eat Cheese?

**Verdict: YES (In Moderation)**

## The Quick Answer
Yes, Yorkshire Terriers can eat cheese, but it should be treated as an **occasional treat** rather than a staple of their diet. While cheese is not toxic to dogs, Yorkies are small breeds with sensitive stomachs, meaning a small amount of cheese for them is a large amount relative to their body weight.

---

## Benefits and Risks

### 🌟 Potential Benefits
When given in tiny portions, certain cheeses provide:
*   **Protein:** Supports muscle maintenance.
*   **Calcium:** Promotes strong bones and teeth.
*   **Palatability:** Great for hiding pills or rewarding training.

### ⚠️ Potential Risks
*   **Lactose Intolerance:** Many dogs lack the enzyme (lactase) to digest dairy, which can lead to gas, bloating, and diarrhea.
*   **High Caloric Density:** Yorkies are prone to obesity. Too much cheese can lead to rapid weight gain.
*   **Excess Sodium:** High salt content in some cheeses can strain a small dog's kidneys and heart.
*   **Pancreatitis:** The high fat content in rich cheeses can trigger pancreatitis in sensitive dogs.

---

## How to Safely Feed Cheese to Your Yorkie

If you want to share a snack with your Yorkie, follow these professional guidelines to ensure their safety:

### 1. Choose the Right Type
*   **Best Choices:** Hard, aged cheeses (like **Cheddar, Swiss, or Parmesan**) generally have less lactose and are easier to digest.
*   **Avoid:** Blue cheese, Brie, or any cheese containing **garlic or onion powder**, as these are toxic to dogs.

### 2. Control the Portion
Stick to the "10% Rule." Treats should never make up more than 10% of your dog's daily caloric intake. For a Yorkie, a piece the size of a **small sugar cube** is plenty.

### 3. Start Slow
Introduce cheese in a very small amount first to check for signs of lactose intolerance. Watch for:
*   Loose stools
*   Excessive gas
*   Vomiting

### 4. Opt for Low-Sodium
Always choose plain, unsalted varieties to protect your dog's cardiovascular health.

**Pro Tip:** If your Yorkie is overweight or has a history of pancreatitis, consult your veterinarian before introducing dairy into their diet.
