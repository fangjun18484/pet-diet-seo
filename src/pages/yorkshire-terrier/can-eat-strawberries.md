# Can Yorkshire Terriers Eat Strawberries? 🍓

**Verdict: YES**

## Why Strawberries are Safe for Yorkies
Strawberries are non-toxic and generally safe for dogs. For a small breed like the Yorkshire Terrier, strawberries can be a refreshing, low-calorie treat that provides essential nutrients without adding excessive bulk to their diet.

## The Benefits & Risks

### ✅ The Benefits
*   **Antioxidants:** Strawberries are rich in antioxidants, which help protect your Yorkie's cells from damage.
*   **Vitamin C:** Supports a healthy immune system.
*   **Fiber:** Aids in healthy digestion and gut health.
*   **Low Calorie:** A great alternative to processed dog treats for weight management.

### ⚠️ The Risks
*   **Natural Sugars:** While healthy, strawberries contain fructose. Too many can lead to weight gain or upset the stomach of dogs with diabetes.
*   **Choking Hazard:** Because Yorkies are toy breeds with small throats, whole strawberries are a significant choking risk.
*   **Digestive Upset:** Introducing any new food too quickly can cause diarrhea or vomiting in sensitive Yorkies.

## How to Safely Feed Strawberries to Your Yorkie

To ensure your Yorkie enjoys their treat safely, follow these preparation steps:

1.  **Wash Thoroughly:** Remove any pesticides or dirt from the skin.
2.  **Remove the Greens:** Cut off the stem and leaves, as these offer no nutritional value and can be hard to digest.
3.  **Cut into Tiny Pieces:** **Crucial for Yorkies!** Slice the strawberry into small, bite-sized cubes to prevent choking.
4.  **Moderation is Key:** Strawberries should be treated as a "treat," not a meal. Limit them to **1–2 small pieces** a few times a week.
5.  **The "First Time" Rule:** Give your dog one tiny piece first and monitor them for 24 hours to ensure they don't have an allergic reaction or stomach sensitivity.

***Disclaimer:** Always consult with your veterinarian before introducing new foods into your pet's diet, especially if your dog has pre-existing health conditions like diabetes.*
