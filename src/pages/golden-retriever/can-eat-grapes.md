# Can Golden Retrievers Eat Grapes? The Essential Safety Guide

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

**Golden Retrievers cannot eat grapes. Grapes and raisins are highly toxic to dogs and can lead to severe health complications.**

---

## Why Are Grapes Dangerous for Golden Retrievers?
While many fruits are healthy treats, grapes and raisins contain substances that are toxic to a dog's renal system. Recent research suggests that **tartaric acid** in grapes is the primary culprit, causing sudden and acute kidney failure in dogs.

Unlike some foods that are only toxic in large quantities, grapes can cause a reaction in some dogs regardless of the amount consumed. Because Golden Retrievers are often "food-motivated" and prone to scavenging, they are at a higher risk of accidental ingestion.

---

## Potential Risks & Symptoms
If your Golden Retriever eats a grape or raisin, they are at risk of **Acute Kidney Injury (AKI)**. Watch for these warning signs:

*   **Vomiting and Diarrhea:** Often occurring shortly after ingestion.
*   **Lethargy:** Unusual tiredness or lack of energy.
*   **Loss of Appetite:** Refusing their favorite treats or meals.
*   **Dehydration:** Increased thirst or, conversely, a decrease in urination.
*   **Coordination Issues:** Stumbling or disorientation.

---

## What to Do If Your Dog Ate Grapes
If you suspect your Golden Retriever has consumed grapes or raisins, **treat this as a medical emergency.**

1.  **Contact Your Vet Immediately:** Call your veterinarian or an emergency animal clinic right away.
2.  **Provide Details:** Tell the vet how many grapes were eaten, the variety (if known), and approximately how long ago it happened.
3.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting Unless Instructed:** Only induce vomiting if your vet specifically tells you to do so over the phone.
4.  **Monitor Closely:** Even if your dog seems fine initially, kidney failure can progress silently.
