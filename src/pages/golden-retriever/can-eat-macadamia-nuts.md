# Can Golden Retrievers Eat Macadamia Nuts? 🚫

**Verdict: NO.** 

**Golden Retrievers cannot eat macadamia nuts. They are highly toxic to dogs and should be kept far away from your pet.**

---

### Why Macadamia Nuts are Dangerous
While many nuts are safe in moderation, macadamia nuts contain a specific toxin (though the exact compound is not fully understood) that affects a dog's nervous system. Because Golden Retrievers are notorious "scavengers" who will eat almost anything they find on the floor or counter, it is crucial to keep these nuts in airtight containers and out of reach.

### Potential Risks & Symptoms
Ingesting even a small amount of macadamia nuts can lead to **macadamia nut toxicosis**. Symptoms typically appear within 12 hours of consumption and can include:

*   **Lethargy:** Extreme tiredness or weakness.
*   **Hind-Leg Weakness:** Difficulty walking or "wobbly" back legs.
*   **Vomiting:** Nausea and gastrointestinal distress.
*   **Hyperthermia:** An increase in body temperature.
*   **Tremors:** In severe cases, muscle tremors or seizures may occur.

---

### What to Do If Your Golden Retriever Ate Macadamia Nuts
If you suspect your dog has eaten macadamia nuts—whether they were raw, roasted, or part of a cookie—**treat this as a medical emergency.**

1.  **Call Your Vet Immediately:** Contact your primary veterinarian or the nearest emergency animal clinic.
2.  **Contact Pet Poison Control:** You can call the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center at **(888) 420-9000**.
3.  **Gather Information:** Try to determine how many nuts were consumed and how long ago it happened. Bring the packaging to the vet if possible.
4.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting:** Never induce vomiting unless specifically instructed to do so by a licensed veterinary professional.

### Safe Alternatives for Your Golden
If you want to give your Golden Retriever a crunchy, healthy treat, try these dog-safe options instead:
*   **Plain Carrots:** Great for dental health and low in calories.
*   **Apple Slices:** (Ensure there are no seeds or core).
*   **Blueberries:** Packed with antioxidants.
*   **Plain Pumpkin:** Excellent for digestion.
