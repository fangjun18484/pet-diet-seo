# Can a Cairn Terrier Eat Tomatoes? 🍅

**Verdict: YES (But only if they are fully ripe!)**

While a ripe, red tomato is generally safe for your Cairn Terrier, the green parts of the plant can be toxic. Because Cairn Terriers are smaller dogs, it is crucial to monitor the quantity and quality of the tomatoes you provide.

---

### Why the Caution?
The primary concern with tomatoes is a compound called **solanine**. Solanine is a natural toxin found in the **green stems, leaves, and unripe green tomatoes**. 

While the red, ripe flesh of the tomato contains very little solanine and is safe for most dogs, consuming the green portions of the plant can lead to solanine poisoning.

---

### Potential Benefits & Risks

#### ✅ The Benefits (Ripe Tomatoes Only)
When fed in moderation, ripe tomatoes offer several nutritional perks:
*   **Lycopene:** A powerful antioxidant that supports heart health and cellular repair.
*   **Vitamins:** Rich in Vitamin C, Vitamin K, and Potassium.
*   **Hydration:** High water content helps keep your active terrier hydrated.

#### ⚠️ The Risks
*   **Solanine Toxicity:** If your dog chews on a tomato vine or eats a green tomato, they may experience lethargy, gastrointestinal distress, or weakness.
*   **Acidity:** Tomatoes are acidic. Some dogs with sensitive stomachs may experience heartburn or diarrhea.
*   **Added Ingredients:** Many human tomato products (ketchup, pasta sauce) contain **onion or garlic powder**, both of which are toxic to dogs.

---

### How to Safely Feed Tomatoes to Your Cairn Terrier

If you want to give your pup a taste of this garden vegetable, follow these golden rules:

1.  **Ripe Red Only:** Never feed your dog green tomatoes or any part of the tomato plant (leaves/stems).
2.  **Fresh is Best:** Serve plain, raw chunks of tomato. Avoid any canned versions containing salt, sugar, or preservatives.
3.  **Portion Control:** Cairn Terriers are small. A few small cubes are plenty; treats should never exceed 10% of their daily caloric intake.
4.  **Remove the Guts:** If your dog has a sensitive stomach, remove the seeds and the gel surrounding them.

**🚨 What to do if your dog eats a green tomato or plant:**
If you suspect your Cairn Terrier has ingested a green tomato or tomato leaves, contact your veterinarian immediately or call a pet poison control center. Watch for signs of vomiting, diarrhea, or lack of coordination.
