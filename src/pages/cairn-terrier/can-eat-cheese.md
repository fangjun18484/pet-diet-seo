# Can a Cairn Terrier Eat Cheese? 🧀

**Verdict: YES (In Moderation)**

While Cairn Terriers can safely eat cheese, it should be treated as an occasional snack rather than a dietary staple. Not all cheeses are created equal, and your dog's individual tolerance plays a huge role.

---

## Why the "Yes" Comes With a Warning
Most dogs are lactose intolerant to some degree. While they can digest the protein and fats in cheese, the lactose (milk sugar) can cause digestive upset for some Cairn Terriers. Because Cairn Terriers are energetic and generally hardy, a small cube of cheese is usually fine, but overfeeding can lead to weight gain or tummy troubles.

## Benefits vs. Risks

### Potential Benefits ✅
*   **High-Value Reward:** Due to the strong scent and taste, cheese is an excellent motivator for training a spirited Cairn Terrier.
*   **Protein & Calcium:** In very small quantities, cheese provides a boost of these essential nutrients.

### Potential Risks ⚠️
*   **Lactose Intolerance:** May cause gas, bloating, or diarrhea.
*   **High Caloric Density:** Excessive cheese can lead to obesity.
*   **Pancreatitis:** High-fat cheeses can trigger inflammation of the pancreas, especially in dogs prone to digestive issues.
*   **Sodium Content:** Too much salt can be harmful to a dog's kidneys and heart.
*   **Toxic Additives:** Some specialty cheeses contain garlic or onion powder, which are **toxic** to dogs.

---

## How to Safely Feed Cheese to Your Cairn Terrier

If you want to share a treat with your pup, follow these professional guidelines:

### 1. Choose the Right Type
*   **Best Choices:** Low-sodium cottage cheese, mozzarella, or mild cheddar.
*   **Avoid:** Blue cheese, Brie, or any cheese seasoned with herbs, garlic, or onions.

### 2. Portion Control
Stick to the **"10% Rule"**: Treats should never make up more than 10% of your dog's daily caloric intake. For a small breed like the Cairn Terrier, a piece the size of a postage stamp is plenty.

### 3. Start Slow
Introduce cheese in a tiny amount first. Monitor your dog for 24 hours for any signs of gastrointestinal distress.

### 4. Check the Ingredients
Always read the label. Ensure there are no artificial sweeteners (like Xylitol) or salt-heavy preservatives.

**Pro Tip:** If your Cairn Terrier has a sensitive stomach, consider **lactose-free cheese** or plain Greek yogurt as a safer alternative!
