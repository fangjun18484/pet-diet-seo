# Can a Cairn Terrier Eat Onions?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

## Why Onions Are Dangerous for Cairn Terriers
Regardless of their spunky personality and hardy nature, Cairn Terriers—like all dogs—cannot safely eat onions. 

Onions belong to the *Allium* family. They contain organic compounds called **N-propyl disulfides**, which are toxic to dogs. These compounds cause oxidative damage to a dog's red blood cells, leading to a serious condition called **hemolytic anemia**, where the body destroys its own red blood cells faster than it can produce them.

## The Potential Risks
Feeding your Cairn Terrier onions (whether raw, cooked, or powdered) can lead to severe health complications. Because Cairn Terriers are small, even a relatively small amount of onion can be dangerous.

### Short-Term Symptoms:
*   **Gastrointestinal Distress:** Vomiting, diarrhea, and abdominal pain.
*   **Lethargy:** Unusual tiredness or lack of energy.
*   **Loss of Appetite:** Refusing their favorite treats or meals.

### Long-Term/Severe Risks:
*   **Hemolytic Anemia:** The breakdown of red blood cells.
*   **Pale Gums:** A sign of decreased oxygen in the blood.
*   **Dark-Colored Urine:** Often reddish or brown, indicating hemoglobinuria (blood in the urine).

## What to Do If Your Cairn Terrier Ate Onions
If your dog accidentally ingested onions, garlic, or onion powder, **treat it as a medical emergency.**

1.  **Contact Your Vet Immediately:** Call your veterinarian or a pet poison control center right away.
2.  **Identify the Amount:** Try to determine how much onion was consumed and whether it was raw, cooked, or concentrated (like onion powder).
3.  **Note the Timing:** Tell the vet exactly when the ingestion happened.
4.  **Monitor for Symptoms:** Watch for pale gums or extreme lethargy, but remember that **symptoms may not appear for several days** after ingestion.

### Safe Alternatives for Your Pup
If you want to give your Cairn Terrier a crunchy, healthy snack, skip the onions and try these dog-safe vegetables instead:
*   **Carrots** (Great for chewing!)
*   **Green Beans** (Low calorie and healthy)
*   **Cucumber** (Hydrating and refreshing)
