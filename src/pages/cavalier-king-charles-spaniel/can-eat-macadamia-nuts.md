# Can a Cavalier King Charles Spaniel Eat Macadamia Nuts?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

Cavalier King Charles Spaniels, like all dog breeds, **cannot** eat macadamia nuts. These nuts are highly toxic to dogs and can lead to severe health complications.

---

### Why Macadamia Nuts Are Dangerous
While we don't fully understand the exact chemical compound responsible for the toxicity, macadamia nuts act as a neurotoxin in dogs. Even a small amount can trigger a reaction that affects the central nervous system, leading to rapid physical decline.

### Potential Risks & Symptoms
Because Cavalier King Charles Spaniels are small-to-medium-sized dogs, they are particularly vulnerable to toxicity as a single nut represents a larger dose relative to their body weight.

**If your dog consumes macadamia nuts, watch for these symptoms:**
*   **Hind Leg Weakness:** Difficulty walking or "wobbly" back legs.
*   **Vomiting & Diarrhea:** Gastrointestinal distress.
*   **Lethargy:** Extreme tiredness or depression.
*   **Hyperthermia:** An increase in body temperature.
*   **Tremors:** In severe cases, muscle tremors or seizures.

---

### What to Do If Your Cavalier Ate Macadamia Nuts
If you suspect your dog has ingested macadamia nuts (including those found in cookies, cakes, or granola), **take immediate action**:

1.  **Contact Your Vet Immediately:** Call your veterinarian or an emergency animal clinic right away.
2.  **Call Pet Poison Control:** If your vet is unavailable, contact a pet poison hotline.
3.  **Identify the Quantity:** Try to determine exactly how many nuts were eaten and when. This helps the vet determine the severity of the toxicity.
4.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting:** Unless specifically instructed to do so by a professional, do not try to make your dog vomit at home.

### Safe Alternatives for Your Cavalier
If you want to give your Cavalier King Charles Spaniel a healthy, crunchy treat, opt for these dog-safe alternatives:
*   **Fresh Blueberries:** Packed with antioxidants.
*   **Apple Slices:** (Ensure seeds and core are removed).
*   **Carrot Sticks:** Great for dental health and low in calories.
*   **Plain Pumpkin:** Great for digestion.
