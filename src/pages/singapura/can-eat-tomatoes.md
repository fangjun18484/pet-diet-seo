# Can a Singapura Cat Eat Tomatoes? 🍅

**Verdict: YES (But only if they are fully ripe!)**

While your Singapura is a curious and energetic little explorer, not everything in the kitchen is safe. When it comes to tomatoes, the answer is a cautious **yes**, provided you follow strict safety guidelines.

---

### Why the Caution?
The primary concern with tomatoes is a compound called **solanine**. 

*   **Ripe Tomatoes:** The red, fleshy part of a fully ripened tomato is generally non-toxic to cats.
*   **Green Tomatoes & Plants:** The stems, leaves, and unripened green tomatoes contain solanine, which is **toxic** to cats and can cause gastrointestinal distress or neurological issues.

---

### Potential Benefits & Risks

#### 🟢 The Benefits (Minimal)
While cats are obligate carnivores and don't *need* vegetables, a tiny piece of ripe tomato provides:
*   **Lycopene:** A powerful antioxidant.
*   **Vitamins A and C:** General health boosters.
*   **Hydration:** High water content.

#### 🔴 The Risks (Significant)
*   **Solanine Poisoning:** If they chew on the plant or eat a green tomato, symptoms include lethargy, vomiting, and diarrhea.
*   **Acidity:** Tomatoes are acidic. Some Singapuras may have sensitive stomachs, leading to acid reflux or upset tummies.
*   **Added Ingredients:** Many store-bought tomato products contain garlic or onion powder, both of which are **highly toxic** to cats.

---

### How to Safely Feed Tomatoes to Your Singapura

Because Singapuras are one of the smallest cat breeds, their systems are more sensitive to small amounts of toxins or acidity. Follow these rules:

1.  **Strictly Ripe:** Only offer bright red, fully ripe tomatoes.
2.  **Remove the Greenery:** Ensure there are absolutely no leaves, stems, or green cores attached.
3.  **Plain Only:** Never feed your cat ketchup, pasta sauce, or salsa. These contain salt, sugar, and toxic aromatics (onion/garlic).
4.  **Bite-Sized Portions:** Treat it as a rare snack, not a meal. A piece the size of a pea is plenty.

### ⚠️ What to do if your cat ate a green tomato or plant
If your Singapura has ingested a tomato plant or a green tomato, contact your veterinarian immediately. Watch for these warning signs:
*   Excessive drooling
*   Vomiting or diarrhea
*   Lack of coordination (ataxia)
*   Extreme lethargy
