# Can a Singapura Cat Eat Apples?

**Verdict: YES (In Moderation)**

While Singapura cats are obligate carnivores and don't *need* fruit in their diet, small amounts of apple are safe and non-toxic for them to consume.

---

### Why Apples Are Safe for Singapura Cats
Apples are not toxic to cats. Most Singapuras can tolerate a small piece of apple as an occasional treat. However, because cats lack the taste receptors for sweetness, they may not be as interested in apples as humans are.

### Potential Benefits & Risks

#### ✅ The Benefits
*   **Fiber:** A tiny amount of apple can provide a boost of fiber, which may help some cats with digestion.
*   **Vitamin C:** While cats synthesize their own Vitamin C, a small amount doesn't cause harm.
*   **Hydration:** Apples have a high water content, contributing to overall hydration.

#### ⚠️ The Risks
*   **Cyanide Toxicity:** Apple **seeds and the core** contain amygdalin, which releases cyanide when chewed. This is highly toxic to cats.
*   **Sugar Content:** Apples contain natural sugars (fructose). Overfeeding can lead to weight gain or spike blood glucose levels in diabetic cats.
*   **Digestive Upset:** Some cats may experience diarrhea or an upset stomach if they eat too much fruit.

---

### How to Safely Feed Apples to Your Singapura

If you want to share a slice of apple with your cat, follow these strict safety guidelines:

1.  **Remove All Seeds and the Core:** This is the most important step. Ensure no seeds are attached to the piece you provide.
2.  **Peel the Skin (Optional):** Some cats find the skin hard to digest; peeling it can prevent choking or tummy issues.
3.  **Cut into Tiny Pieces:** Provide a small, bite-sized cube (about the size of a blueberry) to prevent choking.
4.  **Avoid Added Sugars:** Never feed your cat apple pie, apple sauce with added sugar, or canned apples in syrup.
5.  **The "10% Rule":** Treats should never make up more than 10% of your Singapura's daily caloric intake.

**Pro Tip:** Since Singapura cats are known for being high-energy and curious, always supervise them while they eat to ensure they don't try to scavenge the rest of the apple from your countertop!
