# Can a Singapura Eat Grapes? 🍇

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

---

## Why You Should Never Feed Grapes to Your Singapura
While your Singapura is curious and loves to explore, grapes (and raisins) are **highly toxic** to cats. Regardless of the breed, cats cannot process certain compounds in grapes, which can lead to sudden and severe health complications.

## The Risks: What Happens?
Feeding grapes to a Singapura isn't just a matter of an upset stomach; it is a medical emergency. The primary risk is **Acute Kidney Failure**.

### Potential Symptoms of Grape Toxicity:
*   **Vomiting:** Often occurring shortly after ingestion.
*   **Lethargy:** Unusual tiredness or weakness.
*   **Loss of Appetite:** Refusing their favorite treats or wet food.
*   **Dehydration:** Dry gums or skin.
*   **Decreased Urination:** A sign that the kidneys are failing to filter waste.

**Note:** There is no "safe" amount of grapes. Even a single grape or raisin can trigger toxicity in some cats.

## What to Do If Your Singapura Ate a Grape
If you suspect your cat has ingested a grape or raisin, **do not wait for symptoms to appear.** Kidney damage can happen quickly.

1.  **Call Your Vet Immediately:** Contact your primary veterinarian or the nearest emergency animal clinic.
2.  **Provide Details:** Tell the vet exactly how many grapes were eaten and how long ago it happened.
3.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting:** Never attempt to make your cat vomit at home unless specifically instructed to do so by a professional.
4.  **Monitor Closely:** Keep a close eye on their behavior and hydration levels while transporting them to the clinic.

## Safe, Cat-Friendly Alternatives
If you want to give your Singapura a healthy snack, skip the fruit bowl and try these vet-approved treats instead:
*   **Cooked Chicken:** Plain, unseasoned breast meat.
*   **Cat Grass:** A safe way for them to chew on greenery.
*   **Small Pieces of Steamed Pumpkin:** Great for digestion.
*   **Commercial Cat Treats:** Specifically formulated for feline nutritional needs.
