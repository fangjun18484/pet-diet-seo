# Can a Singapura Eat Onions?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

---

## Why Onions Are Dangerous for Singapura Cats
Regardless of their breed, all cats—including the petite Singapura—must avoid onions. Onions belong to the *Allium* family, which contains compounds known as **N-propyl disulfide**. 

Cats lack the enzyme necessary to process these compounds. When ingested, these toxins cause oxidative damage to the red blood cells, leading to a serious condition called **hemolytic anemia**, where the body destroys its own red blood cells faster than it can produce them.

---

## Potential Risks & Symptoms
Feeding onions to your Singapura (whether raw, cooked, powdered, or dehydrated) can lead to severe health complications. Because Singapura cats are small, even a small amount of onion can have a significant impact on their system.

**Watch for these signs of onion toxicity:**
*   **Lethargy:** Unusual tiredness or weakness.
*   **Pale Gums:** A sign of decreased red blood cell count (anemia).
*   **Dark-Colored Urine:** Often reddish or brown due to hemoglobinuria.
*   **Loss of Appetite:** Refusing food or water.
*   **Rapid Heart Rate:** The heart works harder to pump oxygen through fewer red blood cells.

---

## What to Do If Your Singapura Ate Onions
If you suspect your cat has ingested onions or onion powder, **treat it as a medical emergency.** Toxicity may not appear immediately; it can take several days for the full effects of anemia to manifest.

### Immediate Action Plan:
1.  **Call Your Veterinarian:** Contact your vet or an emergency animal clinic immediately.
2.  **Remove the Source:** Ensure your cat no longer has access to the food.
3.  **Identify the Amount:** Try to determine how much onion was consumed and whether it was raw or concentrated (like onion powder).
4.  **Monitor Closely:** Keep a close eye on their breathing and gum color while transporting them to the clinic.

**Pro Tip:** Avoid giving "human" treats like tuna salads or soups, as these frequently contain hidden onion or garlic powders. Stick to cat-specific treats to keep your Singapura healthy and happy!
