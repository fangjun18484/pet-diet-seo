# Can a Boxer Eat Macadamia Nuts?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

## Why Macadamia Nuts are Dangerous for Boxers
Regardless of their breed, **macadamia nuts are toxic to dogs**, including Boxers. While the exact toxin responsible has not been fully identified, these nuts cause a reaction known as *macadamia nut toxicosis*, which affects the nervous system and metabolism.

Even a small amount of macadamia nuts can cause a significant adverse reaction in your dog.

## The Risks: Symptoms of Macadamia Nut Poisoning
If your Boxer ingests macadamia nuts, symptoms typically appear within **12 hours**. Because Boxers are high-energy dogs, you may notice a sudden, alarming drop in their activity levels.

**Watch for these warning signs:**
*   **Lethargy:** Extreme tiredness or weakness in the hind legs.
*   **Vomiting:** Frequent nausea or stomach upset.
*   **Hyperthermia:** An increase in body temperature (fever).
*   **Tremors:** Muscle shaking or lack of coordination (ataxia).
*   **Depression:** A noticeable change in mood or responsiveness.

## What to Do If Your Boxer Ate Macadamia Nuts
If you suspect your Boxer has consumed macadamia nuts, **treat this as a medical emergency.**

1.  **Contact Your Vet Immediately:** Call your veterinarian or a local emergency pet clinic right away.
2.  **Call Pet Poison Helpline:** If your vet is unavailable, contact the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center.
3.  **Gather Information:** Try to determine how many nuts were eaten and how long ago the ingestion occurred.
4.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting:** Never induce vomiting unless specifically instructed to do so by a veterinary professional.
5.  **Monitor Closely:** Keep your dog quiet and calm while waiting for professional medical guidance.

### Safe Alternatives for Your Boxer
If you want to give your Boxer a healthy, crunchy treat, swap macadamia nuts for these dog-safe options:
*   **Carrot sticks** (Great for chewing)
*   **Apple slices** (No seeds or core)
*   **Plain blueberries**
*   **Cucumber slices**
