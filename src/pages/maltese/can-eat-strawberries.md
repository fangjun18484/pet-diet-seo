# Can a Maltese Eat Strawberries? Safety & Feeding Guide

**Verdict: ✅ YES**

Maltese dogs can safely enjoy strawberries! These vibrant berries are non-toxic and can be a healthy, low-calorie treat for your small companion when fed in moderation.

---

### Why Strawberries are Safe for Maltese
Strawberries are naturally free from the toxins found in other fruits (like grapes or raisins) that are harmful to dogs. For a small breed like the Maltese, strawberries provide a burst of flavor and essential nutrients without adding excessive calories to their diet.

### Potential Benefits & Risks

#### 🍓 The Benefits
*   **High in Vitamin C:** Supports a healthy immune system.
*   **Antioxidants:** Helps fight free radicals and supports overall cellular health.
*   **Fiber:** Promotes healthy digestion.
*   **Hydration:** High water content helps keep your dog hydrated.

#### ⚠️ The Risks
*   **Natural Sugars:** While healthy, the sugar in strawberries can be an issue for dogs with diabetes or those prone to obesity.
*   **Digestive Upset:** Introducing too many berries too quickly may cause diarrhea or an upset stomach.
*   **Choking Hazard:** Because Maltese are tiny, whole strawberries can be a choking risk.

---

### How to Safely Serve Strawberries to Your Maltese

To ensure your Maltese enjoys their treat safely, follow these preparation steps:

1.  **Wash Thoroughly:** Rinse the berries under cold water to remove any pesticides or dirt.
2.  **Remove the Stem:** Cut off the green leafy top and the stem.
3.  **Slice into Small Pieces:** Since Maltese have small mouths, dice the strawberry into tiny, bite-sized cubes to prevent choking.
4.  **Practice Moderation:** Treat strawberries as a *treat*, not a meal. **1 to 2 small pieces** are plenty for a Maltese.
5.  **Avoid Additives:** Never feed your dog strawberries that have been canned in syrup, sweetened, or mixed with chocolate/xylitol.

**Pro Tip:** Try freezing strawberry slices for a refreshing, cooling treat during the summer months!
