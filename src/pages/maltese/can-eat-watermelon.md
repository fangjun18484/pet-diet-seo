# Can a Maltese Eat Watermelon?

**Verdict: ✅ YES**

Maltese dogs can safely enjoy watermelon as an occasional treat. It is non-toxic, hydrating, and generally well-tolerated by small breeds.

---

### Why Watermelon is Safe for Maltese
Watermelon is primarily composed of water (about 92%), making it an excellent low-calorie snack. Since Maltese are prone to obesity if overfed, watermelon provides a refreshing alternative to high-calorie commercial treats.

### 🍉 Potential Benefits & Risks

#### The Benefits
*   **Hydration:** Perfect for keeping your Maltese hydrated during hot summer months.
*   **Nutrient-Rich:** Contains essential vitamins like **A, B6, and C**, as well as **potassium**.
*   **Low Calorie:** A great way to reward your dog without adding excessive weight.

#### The Risks
*   **High Sugar Content:** While natural, the sugar in watermelon can be an issue for dogs with diabetes.
*   **The Rind:** The hard outer skin is difficult to digest and can cause **gastrointestinal blockage**, especially in a small breed like the Maltese.
*   **The Seeds:** Seeds can cause choking or lead to intestinal obstructions if consumed in large quantities.

---

### 🛠 How to Safely Serve Watermelon to Your Maltese

Because Maltese are tiny, preparation is key to preventing choking and tummy upsets. Follow these steps:

1.  **Remove the Rind:** Serve only the red, fleshy part of the fruit. Discard the green skin.
2.  **Remove All Seeds:** Ensure every seed (both black and white) is removed before feeding.
3.  **Cut into Tiny Cubes:** Slice the fruit into small, bite-sized pieces (roughly the size of a blueberry) to prevent choking.
4.  **Moderation is Key:** Treat watermelon as a snack, not a meal. Stick to a few small cubes once or twice a week.

**Pro Tip:** Try freezing watermelon cubes for a cooling, refreshing summer treat that keeps your Maltese occupied!
