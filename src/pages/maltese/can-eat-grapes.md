# Can a Maltese Eat Grapes? 🍇

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

**Absolutely not. You should never feed grapes or raisins to a Maltese, or any other dog breed.**

---

## Why Grapes Are Dangerous for Maltese Dogs
While grapes may seem like a healthy snack for humans, they are **highly toxic** to dogs. Recent research suggests that tartaric acid in grapes is the primary culprit, leading to sudden and severe kidney failure.

Because Maltese are small-breed dogs, their lower body mass means that even a small amount of grapes can trigger a toxic reaction more quickly than in a larger dog.

## The Risks: What Happens if a Maltese Eats Grapes?
There is no "safe" amount of grapes. Some dogs may react strongly to a single grape, while others may tolerate more—but the risk of **acute kidney failure** is too high to gamble with.

### Watch for these warning signs:
*   **Vomiting and Diarrhea:** Often the first sign of distress.
*   **Lethargy:** Unusual tiredness or weakness.
*   **Loss of Appetite:** Refusing their favorite treats or meals.
*   **Changes in Urination:** A significant decrease in urine output (a sign of kidney failure).
*   **Dehydration:** Dry gums and skin.

---

## 🚨 What to Do if Your Maltese Ate a Grape
If your Maltese accidentally ingested a grape or raisin, **treat this as a medical emergency.** Do not wait for symptoms to appear, as kidney damage can happen rapidly.

1.  **Call Your Vet Immediately:** Contact your veterinarian or the nearest emergency animal clinic.
2.  **Provide Details:** Tell the vet how many grapes were eaten and how long ago it happened.
3.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting** unless explicitly instructed to do so by a professional.
4.  **Seek Immediate Care:** Your vet may induce vomiting or administer activated charcoal to prevent the toxin from entering the bloodstream.

---

## Safe, Healthy Alternatives for Your Maltese
If you want to give your Maltese a fruity treat, stick to these dog-safe options:
*   **Blueberries:** Packed with antioxidants and small enough for a Maltese's mouth.
*   **Apple Slices:** (Remove all seeds and the core).
*   **Watermelon:** (Remove the seeds and rind).
*   **Banana:** A great source of potassium in moderation.
