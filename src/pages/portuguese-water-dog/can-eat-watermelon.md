# Can Portuguese Water Dogs Eat Watermelon?

**Verdict: ✅ YES**

## Why Watermelon is Safe for Portuguese Water Dogs
Watermelon is a refreshing, low-calorie snack that is perfectly safe for Portuguese Water Dogs. Since this breed is active and often spends time in the water or outdoors, the high water content in watermelon makes it an excellent hydrating treat.

## The Benefits and Risks

### 🌟 The Benefits
*   **Hydration:** Watermelon is approximately 92% water, helping your dog stay hydrated during summer walks.
*   **Essential Nutrients:** It provides a boost of **Vitamin A, Vitamin C, and Potassium**.
*   **Low Calorie:** It is a great alternative to processed treats for dogs prone to weight gain.

### ⚠️ The Risks
While the flesh is safe, two parts of the watermelon can be dangerous:
*   **The Seeds:** Seeds can cause intestinal blockages, especially in smaller dogs or those with sensitive stomachs.
*   **The Rind:** The hard outer skin is difficult to digest and can lead to gastrointestinal distress or blockage if swallowed in large chunks.
*   **Sugar Content:** While natural, the sugar in watermelon should be given in moderation to avoid obesity or spikes in blood glucose (especially for diabetic dogs).

## How to Safely Serve Watermelon to Your PWD

To ensure your Portuguese Water Dog enjoys their treat safely, follow these preparation steps:

1.  **Remove All Seeds:** Ensure all black and white seeds are removed before serving.
2.  **Discard the Rind:** Cut away the green skin and the white pith; feed only the red, juicy flesh.
3.  **Bite-Sized Pieces:** Cut the fruit into small, manageable cubes to prevent choking.
4.  **Moderation is Key:** Treat watermelon as a snack, not a meal. It should make up no more than **10% of your dog's daily caloric intake**.
5.  **Freeze for Fun:** For an extra-engaging treat, freeze the watermelon cubes to help your dog cool down on a hot day!
