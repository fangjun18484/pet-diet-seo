# Can a Portuguese Water Dog Eat Grapes?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

***

## Why Grapes Are Dangerous for Portuguese Water Dogs
Regardless of the breed, **grapes and raisins are highly toxic to dogs.** While the exact toxin was long debated, recent research points to **tartaric acid** as the cause of toxicity. 

Portuguese Water Dogs, despite their athletic nature and hardy constitutions, are just as susceptible to grape poisoning as any other dog. Even a small amount can trigger a severe reaction in some dogs, while others may be more resilient; however, the risk is far too high to ever attempt feeding them.

## The Risks: What Happens?
Feeding grapes to your dog can lead to **acute kidney failure**. This is a medical emergency that can progress rapidly.

### Warning Signs of Grape Toxicity:
*   **Vomiting and Diarrhea:** Usually occurs within a few hours of ingestion.
*   **Lethargy:** Unusual tiredness or weakness.
*   **Loss of Appetite:** Refusing favorite treats or meals.
*   **Dehydration:** Dry gums or skin.
*   **Decreased Urination:** A critical sign that the kidneys are shutting down.

## What to Do If Your Dog Ate Grapes
If your Portuguese Water Dog has accidentally ingested a grape or a raisin, **time is of the essence.** Follow these steps immediately:

1.  **Call Your Veterinarian or Pet Poison Control:** Do not wait for symptoms to appear. Early intervention is the key to preventing permanent kidney damage.
2.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting Unless Instructed:** Only induce vomiting if your vet specifically tells you to do so over the phone.
3.  **Identify the Amount:** Try to determine exactly how many grapes were eaten and how long ago it happened. This helps your vet determine the urgency of the treatment.
4.  **Monitor for Symptoms:** While on the way to the clinic, keep a close eye on their behavior and breathing.

### Safe Alternatives for Your Portie
If you want to give your Portuguese Water Dog a fruity treat, skip the grapes and try these kidney-safe options:
*   **Blueberries** (Rich in antioxidants)
*   **Apple Slices** (Seedless and core-free)
*   **Watermelon** (Seedless and rind-free)
*   **Banana** (In moderation)
