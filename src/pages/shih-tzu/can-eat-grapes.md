# Can a Shih Tzu Eat Grapes? 🍇

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

**Do not feed grapes or raisins to your Shih Tzu.** Grapes and raisins are highly toxic to dogs, regardless of the breed or the amount consumed.

---

### Why Are Grapes Dangerous for Shih Tzus?
Grapes and raisins contain a natural toxin (recently identified as tartaric acid) that can cause **acute kidney failure** in dogs. 

Because Shih Tzus are small-breed dogs, their bodies are more sensitive to toxins. Even a single grape or a small raisin can trigger a severe reaction, leading to rapid organ failure if not treated immediately.

---

### The Risks: What Happens if They Eat Grapes?
There are no "benefits" to feeding grapes to a dog; the risks are severe and potentially fatal.

**Common symptoms of grape poisoning include:**
*   **Vomiting and Diarrhea:** Usually occurs within a few hours of ingestion.
*   **Lethargy:** Your dog may seem unusually tired or weak.
*   **Loss of Appetite:** Refusing food or treats.
*   **Dehydration:** Dry gums and skin.
*   **Decreased Urination:** A primary sign that the kidneys are beginning to fail.

---

### 🚨 What to Do if Your Shih Tzu Ate a Grape
If your Shih Tzu has accidentally consumed a grape or raisin, **treat this as a medical emergency.**

1.  **Call Your Vet Immediately:** Do not wait for symptoms to appear. Early intervention is the key to saving your dog's kidney function.
2.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting Unless Instructed:** Only induce vomiting if your veterinarian explicitly tells you to do so over the phone.
3.  **Provide Information:** Be ready to tell the vet how many grapes were eaten and approximately how long ago it happened.
4.  **Visit the ER:** If your regular vet is closed, head to the nearest emergency animal clinic.
