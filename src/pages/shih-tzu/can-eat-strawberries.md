# Can a Shih Tzu Eat Strawberries?

**Verdict: ✅ YES**

Shih Tzus can safely enjoy strawberries! These vibrant berries are non-toxic for dogs and can be a healthy, refreshing treat when given in moderation.

---

### Why Strawberries Are Safe for Shih Tzus
Strawberries are packed with essential nutrients and are generally well-tolerated by dogs. Because Shih Tzus are a small breed, they require treats that are low in calories but high in nutrition, making strawberries an excellent choice compared to processed dog treats.

### Potential Benefits & Risks

#### 🌟 The Benefits
*   **Antioxidants:** Rich in anthocyanins, which help fight free radicals and support overall cellular health.
*   **Vitamin C:** Supports a strong immune system and promotes healthy skin and coat.
*   **Fiber:** Aids in digestion and helps keep your Shih Tzu feeling full.
*   **Low Calorie:** A great way to treat a small dog without contributing to obesity.

#### ⚠️ The Risks
*   **Natural Sugars:** While healthy, strawberries contain fructose. Too many can lead to weight gain or upset the blood sugar levels of dogs with diabetes.
*   **Choking Hazard:** Because Shih Tzus have small throats, whole strawberries can be a choking risk.
*   **Digestive Upset:** Introducing too many berries too quickly may cause diarrhea or an upset stomach in some sensitive dogs.

---

### How to Safely Feed Strawberries to Your Shih Tzu

To ensure your pup enjoys their treat safely, follow these preparation steps:

1.  **Wash Thoroughly:** Rinse the berries under cold water to remove any pesticides or dirt.
2.  **Remove the Greens:** Pull off the green leafy stems and hulls, as these provide no nutritional value and can be hard to digest.
3.  **Cut Into Tiny Pieces:** Slice the strawberry into small, bite-sized chunks. This prevents choking and makes it easier for a small Shih Tzu to chew.
4.  **Moderation is Key:** Follow the **"10% Rule"**—treats should never make up more than 10% of your dog's daily caloric intake. For a Shih Tzu, 1–2 small pieces are usually plenty.

**Pro Tip:** Try freezing strawberry slices for a cooling summer treat that keeps your dog occupied!
