# Can a Shih Tzu Eat Apples? 🍎

**Verdict: ✅ YES**

Shih Tzus can safely eat apples! Apples are a crunchy, low-calorie treat that can fit perfectly into your small dog's diet, provided they are served correctly.

---

## Why Apples Are Safe for Shih Tzus
Apples are non-toxic to dogs and provide a refreshing alternative to processed store-bought treats. Because Shih Tzus are prone to weight gain and certain dental issues, the natural crunch and nutrients in apples make them an excellent choice for a healthy snack.

## The Benefits & Risks

### 🌟 The Benefits
*   **Vitamin Boost:** Apples are rich in Vitamin A and Vitamin C, supporting overall immune health.
*   **Digestive Health:** The fiber found in apples helps regulate digestion.
*   **Dental Health:** Chewing on apple slices can help clean teeth and freshen breath.
*   **Low Calorie:** A great option for Shih Tzus who need to maintain a healthy weight.

### ⚠️ The Risks
*   **Apple Seeds:** These contain small amounts of **cyanide**, which is toxic to dogs if consumed in large quantities.
*   **The Core:** The tough center of the apple is a major **choking hazard**, especially for a small breed like the Shih Tzu.
*   **Sugar Content:** While natural, the sugar in apples can be an issue for dogs with diabetes.

---

## How to Safely Serve Apples to Your Shih Tzu

To ensure your pup enjoys their treat without any danger, follow these simple preparation steps:

1.  **Wash Thoroughly:** Remove any pesticides or wax from the skin.
2.  **Remove the Core and Seeds:** Completely discard the center and all seeds.
3.  **Cut into Small Pieces:** Because Shih Tzus have small mouths and throats, cut the apple into **bite-sized cubes or thin slices** to prevent choking.
4.  **Moderation is Key:** Treat apples as a *treat*, not a meal. Limit them to a few slices a day to avoid digestive upset (diarrhea) from too much fiber/sugar.

**Pro Tip:** Try freezing apple slices for a refreshing, cooling treat during the summer months!
