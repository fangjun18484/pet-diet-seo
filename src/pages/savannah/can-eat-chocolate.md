# Can a Savannah Cat Eat Chocolate?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

## Why Chocolate is Dangerous for Savannah Cats
Despite their wild ancestry and large size, Savannah cats have the same biological sensitivities as domestic cats. Chocolate contains **methylxanthines**, specifically **theobromine** and **caffeine**. 

While humans can easily metabolize these compounds, cats process them very slowly. This leads to a toxic buildup in their system, which overstimulates the central nervous system and the heart.

## Potential Risks & Toxicity
Chocolate is not just a "stomach ache" risk; it is a systemic poison for felines. The darker the chocolate (higher cocoa content), the higher the risk of severe toxicity.

### Common Symptoms of Chocolate Poisoning:
*   **Digestive Distress:** Vomiting and diarrhea.
*   **Hyperactivity:** Extreme restlessness and pacing.
*   **Cardiac Issues:** Rapid heart rate (tachycardia) or irregular heartbeats.
*   **Neurological Signs:** Muscle tremors, agitation, or seizures.
*   **Severe Cases:** Coma or death if left untreated.

## What to Do If Your Savannah Cat Ate Chocolate
If your cat has ingested chocolate—whether it was a small piece of a milk chocolate bar or a lick of dark chocolate—**treat it as a medical emergency.**

### Immediate Action Steps:
1.  **Call Your Vet Immediately:** Contact your primary veterinarian or the nearest emergency animal hospital.
2.  **Identify the Source:** Determine exactly what type of chocolate was eaten (e.g., Dark, Milk, White) and estimate the amount consumed.
3.  **Save the Packaging:** Bring the wrapper to the clinic so the vet can calculate the exact dose of theobromine ingested.
4.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting:** Never attempt to make your cat vomit unless explicitly instructed to do so by a licensed professional, as this can cause aspiration pneumonia.

**Pro Tip:** Keep all chocolate, cocoa powder, and chocolate-flavored desserts in sealed containers and high cabinets to prevent your curious Savannah from investigating them.
