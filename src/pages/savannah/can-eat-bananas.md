# Can a Savannah Cat Eat Bananas?

**Verdict: YES (In Moderation)**

## The Quick Explanation
Bananas are non-toxic to cats, including the Savannah breed. While they are safe to ingest, it is important to remember that Savannahs—like all cats—are **obligate carnivores**. This means their bodies are designed to derive nutrients from animal proteins, not plant-based sugars. A small piece of banana is a safe treat, but it should not be a staple of their diet.

---

## Benefits and Risks

### Potential Benefits
*   **Potassium:** Supports heart and kidney function.
*   **Magnesium:** Aids in nerve and muscle function.
*   **Fiber:** In very small amounts, it can help with digestion.

### Potential Risks
*   **High Sugar Content:** Bananas are rich in natural sugars. Excessive intake can lead to weight gain or increase the risk of diabetes.
*   **Digestive Upset:** Some cats may experience diarrhea or stomach upset if they are sensitive to fruit.
*   **Lack of Nutritional Necessity:** Bananas provide no essential nutrients that a Savannah cannot get from a high-quality, meat-based diet.

---

## How to Safely Feed Bananas to Your Savannah

If your Savannah is curious about your snack, follow these safety guidelines:

1.  **Remove the Peel:** Never feed the peel to your cat; it is difficult to digest and can cause intestinal blockages.
2.  **Keep Portions Tiny:** Offer a small slice or a piece the size of a blueberry. A treat should never exceed 10% of their daily caloric intake.
3.  **Avoid Added Sugars:** Only feed plain, fresh bananas. Never give your cat banana bread, dried bananas with added sugar, or flavored banana treats.
4.  **Monitor the Reaction:** The first time you offer a banana, watch for any signs of allergic reaction or digestive distress.

**Pro Tip:** If your Savannah has pre-existing health issues, such as diabetes or kidney disease, consult your veterinarian before introducing any fruit into their diet.
