# Can Savannah Cats Eat Avocado?

**Verdict: NO** ❌

While you might want to share your healthy snacks with your high-energy Savannah cat, **avocados are not recommended for feline consumption.**

---

### Why You Should Avoid Feeding Avocado to Your Savannah
The primary concern is a toxin called **persin**. Persin is a fungicidal toxin found in the leaves, peel, and pit of the avocado plant. 

While cats are generally more resistant to persin than birds or horses, it can still cause gastrointestinal distress. Furthermore, the high fat content in avocados can trigger other serious health issues in cats.

---

### Potential Risks & Side Effects

Feeding avocado to your Savannah cat can lead to the following complications:

*   **Gastrointestinal Upset:** The most common reaction to persin is vomiting and diarrhea.
*   **Pancreatitis:** Avocados are incredibly rich in fats. For a cat, this high fat load can lead to inflammation of the pancreas (pancreatitis), a painful and serious condition.
*   **Choking Hazard:** The large avocado pit is a significant choking hazard and can cause an intestinal blockage if swallowed.
*   **Added Ingredients:** Many human avocado preparations (like guacamole) contain **garlic and onions**, both of which are highly toxic to cats and can cause hemolytic anemia.

---

### What to Do If Your Savannah Ate Avocado

If your cat sneaked a bite of your avocado toast, don't panic, but do take action:

1.  **Assess the Amount:** A tiny lick of the flesh is unlikely to be fatal, but larger quantities or ingestion of the skin/pit are more dangerous.
2.  **Monitor for Symptoms:** Watch for lethargy, repeated vomiting, or diarrhea over the next 24 hours.
3.  **Check the Ingredients:** If they ate guacamole, check for garlic or onion powder. If these were present, **call your vet immediately.**
4.  **Contact Your Vet:** If your cat is showing signs of distress or has eaten a significant portion of the fruit, call your veterinarian or an animal poison control center.

**Pro Tip:** If you're looking for a safe, healthy treat for your Savannah, stick to small pieces of cooked chicken, plain tuna, or vet-approved cat treats!
