# Can a Savannah Cat Eat Grapes?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

## Why Savannah Cats Cannot Eat Grapes
Despite their wild ancestry and hardy nature, Savannah cats are still felines, and **grapes (and raisins) are highly toxic to cats.** 

While the exact toxin in grapes is not fully understood, ingestion can lead to **acute kidney failure**. Because Savannahs are high-energy and curious, they may be more prone to "counter-surfing" or stealing snacks, making it crucial for owners to keep these fruits completely out of reach.

## The Risks of Grape Consumption
There are no health benefits to feeding grapes to a Savannah cat; there are only severe risks. Even a single grape or raisin can be dangerous depending on the cat's size and sensitivity.

**Potential symptoms of grape poisoning include:**
*   **Vomiting and Diarrhea:** Often the first sign of distress.
*   **Lethargy:** Unusual tiredness or weakness.
*   **Loss of Appetite:** Refusing their favorite wet food or treats.
*   **Dehydration:** Increased thirst or a decrease in urine output (a sign of kidney failure).
*   **Neurological Issues:** In severe cases, disorientation or seizures.

## What to Do If Your Savannah Cat Ate a Grape
If you suspect your cat has ingested a grape or raisin, **treat this as a medical emergency.** Do not wait for symptoms to appear, as kidney damage can happen quickly.

1.  **Call Your Vet Immediately:** Contact your primary veterinarian or the nearest emergency animal clinic.
2.  **Provide Details:** Tell the vet approximately how many grapes were eaten and how long ago the incident occurred.
3.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting:** Never attempt to make your cat vomit unless explicitly instructed to do so by a licensed professional.
4.  **Monitor Closely:** Keep a close eye on their behavior and hydration levels until you reach the clinic.

### Safe Alternatives for Your Savannah
If your Savannah cat enjoys fruit, opt for these **cat-safe** alternatives in small quantities:
*   **Blueberries** (Rich in antioxidants)
*   **Watermelon** (Seedless and rind-free)
*   **Cantaloupe** (Fresh and seedless)
