# Can Savannah Cats Eat Macadamia Nuts?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

## Why You Should Avoid Macadamia Nuts
Regardless of their wild ancestry, Savannah cats—like all domestic cats—should **never** eat macadamia nuts. These nuts contain compounds that are toxic to pets, potentially leading to severe neurological and gastrointestinal distress. While much of the research focuses on dogs, the risks are too high to ignore for cats.

## Potential Risks & Symptoms
Macadamia nuts offer zero nutritional benefit to your Savannah cat and instead pose significant health risks. If ingested, your cat may experience:

*   **Digestive Upset:** Severe vomiting and diarrhea.
*   **Neurological Issues:** Lack of coordination (ataxia), muscle weakness, or tremors.
*   **Lethargy:** Unusual tiredness or a sudden drop in energy levels.
*   **Hyperthermia:** In rare, severe cases, an increase in body temperature.

## What to Do If Your Savannah Cat Ate a Macadamia Nut
If you suspect your cat has ingested macadamia nuts, **do not wait for symptoms to appear.** Take the following steps immediately:

1.  **Remove the Source:** Ensure no more nuts are accessible to your cat.
2.  **Call Your Vet:** Contact your veterinarian or a pet poison control center immediately.
3.  **Provide Information:** Be ready to tell your vet how many nuts were eaten, the approximate time of ingestion, and your cat's current weight.
4.  **Monitor Closely:** Watch for any signs of weakness, disorientation, or vomiting while you are en route to the clinic.

### Safe Alternatives for Your Savannah
If you want to give your active Savannah cat a healthy treat, opt for vet-approved options like:
*   **Small pieces of cooked chicken or turkey** (unseasoned).
*   **Freeze-dried minnows or salmon.**
*   **Commercial cat treats** formulated for high-energy breeds.
