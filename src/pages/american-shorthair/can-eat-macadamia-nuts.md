# Can American Shorthairs Eat Macadamia Nuts?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

## Why You Should Avoid Macadamia Nuts
While American Shorthairs are hardy and curious cats, **macadamia nuts are toxic to cats.** These nuts contain compounds that can cause systemic distress and are far too rich for a feline's digestive system. Unlike some nuts that are simply "unhealthy," macadamia nuts can trigger a toxic reaction in pets.

## The Risks: What Happens If They Eat Them?
Feeding macadamia nuts to your American Shorthair poses several significant health risks:

*   **Macadamia Toxicity:** While more documented in dogs, cats can experience similar toxic reactions, including disorientation and lack of coordination.
*   **Pancreatitis:** Macadamia nuts are incredibly high in fats. This can cause inflammation of the pancreas (pancreatitis), leading to severe abdominal pain and vomiting.
*   **Digestive Upset:** Even a small amount can lead to gastrointestinal distress, including diarrhea and nausea.
*   **Choking Hazard:** Due to their size and density, these nuts can pose a choking risk or cause an intestinal blockage.

## What to Do If Your Cat Ate Macadamia Nuts
If your American Shorthair has ingested a macadamia nut, do not wait for symptoms to appear. Take the following steps immediately:

1.  **Remove Access:** Ensure no more nuts are within reach of your cat.
2.  **Identify the Amount:** Try to determine how many nuts were eaten and when.
3.  **Contact Your Vet:** Call your veterinarian or an emergency pet clinic immediately. 
4.  **Monitor for Symptoms:** Watch for signs of toxicity, such as:
    *   Vomiting
    *   Lethargy or weakness
    *   Tremors or unsteady walking
    *   Loss of appetite

**Pro Tip:** If you're looking for a safe, crunchy treat for your American Shorthair, stick to veterinarian-approved cat treats or small pieces of cooked, unseasoned chicken.
