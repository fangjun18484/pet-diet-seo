# Can Cocker Spaniels Eat Macadamia Nuts? 🚫

**The Verdict: NO.**

Cocker Spaniels, like all dog breeds, **cannot** eat macadamia nuts. These nuts are highly toxic to dogs and can lead to severe health complications.

---

## Why Macadamia Nuts Are Dangerous for Dogs
While the exact toxic compound in macadamia nuts isn't fully understood, they contain substances that trigger a systemic reaction in dogs. Because Cocker Spaniels are medium-sized dogs, even a small amount of these nuts can cause a significant toxic reaction.

## Potential Risks & Symptoms
Ingesting macadamia nuts can lead to **macadamia nut toxicosis**. Symptoms usually appear within 12 hours of consumption and may include:

*   **Lethargy:** Unusual tiredness or weakness.
*   **Hind Leg Weakness:** Difficulty walking or "wobbly" back legs.
*   **Vomiting & Diarrhea:** Gastrointestinal distress.
*   **Hyperthermia:** An increase in body temperature.
*   **Tremors:** In severe cases, muscle shaking or seizures.

**Note:** There are no nutritional benefits to macadamia nuts for dogs; the risks far outweigh any perceived "healthy fats."

---

## What to Do If Your Cocker Spaniel Ate Macadamia Nuts
If you suspect your dog has eaten macadamia nuts (or a product containing them, like cookies or granola), **take immediate action**:

1.  **Remove Access:** Ensure your dog cannot eat any more of the nuts.
2.  **Call Your Vet Immediately:** Contact your veterinarian or an emergency animal clinic right away.
3.  **Contact Pet Poison Control:** If your vet is unavailable, call the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center.
4.  **Monitor Symptoms:** Be prepared to tell the vet how many nuts were eaten and how long ago the ingestion occurred.

### Safe Alternatives for Your Cocker Spaniel
If you want to give your Cocker Spaniel a crunchy, healthy treat, try these dog-safe options instead:
*   **Carrot sticks** (Low calorie and great for teeth)
*   **Apple slices** (Seedless and skinless)
*   **Plain cooked pumpkin** (Great for digestion)
*   **Blueberries** (Rich in antioxidants)
