# Can a Cocker Spaniel Eat Grapes?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

## Why Cocker Spaniels Cannot Eat Grapes
Regardless of the breed, **grapes and raisins are highly toxic to dogs**, including Cocker Spaniels. While some dogs may seem fine after eating one, others can suffer severe reactions even from a small amount. 

Recent research suggests that **tartaric acid** found in grapes is the primary cause of toxicity, which can lead to sudden, acute kidney failure.

## The Risks: What Happens?
There are no benefits to feeding grapes to your dog; the risks are severe and potentially fatal. If a Cocker Spaniel consumes grapes, they may experience:

*   **Acute Kidney Failure:** The most dangerous risk, which can lead to permanent organ damage or death.
*   **Gastrointestinal Distress:** Vomiting, diarrhea, and loss of appetite.
*   **Lethargy:** Unusual tiredness or weakness.
*   **Dehydration:** Due to the kidneys' inability to process fluids properly.

## What to Do If Your Cocker Spaniel Ate Grapes
If your dog has ingested a grape or raisin, **this is a veterinary emergency.** Do not "wait and see" if symptoms appear.

1.  **Call Your Vet Immediately:** Contact your primary veterinarian or the nearest emergency animal clinic.
2.  **Provide Details:** Tell the vet how many grapes were eaten, the approximate time of ingestion, and your dog's weight.
3.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting Unless Instructed:** Only induce vomiting if specifically told to do so by a professional.
4.  **Monitor for Symptoms:** Watch for changes in urination patterns or sudden lethargy.

### Safe Fruit Alternatives for Cocker Spaniels
If you want to give your Cocker Spaniel a healthy treat, try these grape-safe alternatives:
*   **Blueberries** (Rich in antioxidants)
*   **Apple Slices** (Seedless and core-free)
*   **Watermelon** (Seedless and rind-free)
*   **Banana** (In moderation)
