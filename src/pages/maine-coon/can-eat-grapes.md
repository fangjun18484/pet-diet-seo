# Can Maine Coons Eat Grapes? 🍇

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

Maine Coons, like all cats, **should never eat grapes or raisins.** 

---

## Why Grapes Are Dangerous for Maine Coons
While Maine Coons are the "gentle giants" of the cat world, their internal organs are just as susceptible to toxins as any other breed. Grapes and raisins contain tartaric acid, which is highly toxic to cats. 

Unlike some foods that cause mild stomach upset, grapes can trigger **acute kidney failure**, which can be fatal if not treated immediately.

## Potential Risks & Symptoms
There are **no nutritional benefits** to feeding grapes to a cat; there are only severe risks. If your Maine Coon ingests a grape, watch for these warning signs:

*   **Vomiting and Diarrhea:** Often the first sign of toxicity.
*   **Lethargy:** Unusual tiredness or weakness.
*   **Loss of Appetite:** Refusing their favorite treats or wet food.
*   **Changes in Urination:** A significant decrease in urine output (a sign of kidney failure).
*   **Dehydration:** Dry gums or skin.

## What to Do If Your Maine Coon Ate a Grape
If you suspect your cat has eaten a grape or raisin, **treat this as a medical emergency.** Do not wait for symptoms to appear.

1.  **Call Your Veterinarian Immediately:** Contact your vet or the nearest emergency animal hospital.
2.  **Prevent Further Access:** Remove the grapes from the area to ensure they don't eat more.
3.  **Provide Information:** Be ready to tell the vet how many grapes were eaten and approximately how long ago the ingestion occurred.
4.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting:** Never attempt to make your cat vomit unless explicitly instructed to do so by a licensed veterinary professional.

### Safe Alternatives for Your Maine Coon
If you want to give your Maine Coon a healthy snack, try these cat-safe options instead:
*   **Blueberries** (in moderation)
*   **Cooked Pumpkin** (plain, no spices)
*   **Small pieces of cooked chicken** (unseasoned)
