# Can a Tonkinese Eat Macadamia Nuts?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

## Why You Should Avoid Macadamia Nuts
Regardless of the breed, **Tonkinese cats cannot eat macadamia nuts.** These nuts contain naturally occurring toxins that can cause severe reactions in cats. While many nuts are simply indigestible or high in fat, macadamia nuts are specifically toxic to the feline nervous system.

## The Risks: What Happens if a Tonkinese Eats One?
Macadamia nuts can lead to a condition known as macadamia nut toxicosis. Because Tonkinese cats are active and curious, they may be tempted to snack on a dropped nut, but the risks far outweigh any curiosity.

**Potential symptoms include:**
*   **Lethargy:** Sudden, extreme tiredness or weakness.
*   **Vomiting:** Gastrointestinal distress and nausea.
*   **Hyperthermia:** An increase in body temperature.
*   **Loss of Coordination:** Unsteady walking or tremors (ataxia).
*   **Depression:** A noticeable drop in their usual playful energy.

## What to Do If Your Cat Ate a Macadamia Nut
If you suspect your Tonkinese has ingested a macadamia nut, **do not wait for symptoms to appear.** Act quickly to prevent the toxins from being fully absorbed into the bloodstream.

1.  **Remove the Source:** Ensure no more nuts are accessible to your cat.
2.  **Call Your Vet Immediately:** Contact your veterinarian or an emergency animal clinic.
3.  **Provide Details:** Tell the vet approximately how many nuts were eaten and how long ago the ingestion occurred.
4.  **Monitor Closely:** Keep a close eye on their movement and breathing until you reach the clinic.

### Safe Alternatives for Your Tonkinese
If you want to give your Tonkinese a healthy treat, skip the nuts and try these cat-safe options:
*   **Freeze-dried chicken or salmon**
*   **Small pieces of cooked pumpkin** (plain)
*   **Commercial cat treats** formulated for feline nutrition
