# Can a Basset Hound Eat Grapes?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

**Basset Hounds cannot eat grapes.** Whether they are red, green, seedless, or organic, grapes are highly toxic to all dog breeds, including Basset Hounds.

---

### Why Grapes are Dangerous
Grapes and raisins contain a specific compound (recently identified as **tartaric acid**) that can cause sudden, acute **kidney failure** in dogs. 

Unlike some foods that are toxic only in large quantities, grapes can trigger a severe reaction in some dogs even in small amounts. Because Basset Hounds are food-motivated "scavengers," they are at a higher risk of accidentally consuming these fruits if left on a counter or table.

---

### Potential Risks & Symptoms
If your Basset Hound ingests grapes, they are at risk of severe renal distress. Watch for these warning signs:

*   **Gastrointestinal Upset:** Vomiting, diarrhea, or loss of appetite.
*   **Lethargy:** Unusual tiredness or weakness.
*   **Dehydration:** Increased thirst or, conversely, a complete stop in urination (a sign of kidney failure).
*   **Neurological Issues:** Disorientation or lack of coordination.

---

### What to Do If Your Dog Ate a Grape
If your Basset Hound has consumed a grape or raisin, **treat this as a medical emergency.**

1.  **Call Your Vet Immediately:** Contact your veterinarian or a local emergency pet clinic right away.
2.  **Contact Pet Poison Control:** You can also call the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center.
3.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting:** Unless explicitly told to do so by a professional, do not try to make your dog vomit, as this can sometimes cause further complications.
4.  **Provide Information:** Be ready to tell the vet how many grapes were eaten, the approximate time of ingestion, and your dog's weight.

### Safe Fruit Alternatives for Basset Hounds
Want to give your Basset Hound a healthy treat instead? Try these dog-safe options:
*   **Blueberries** (Rich in antioxidants)
*   **Apple Slices** (Remove the seeds and core)
*   **Watermelon** (Seedless and rind-free)
*   **Banana** (In moderation)
