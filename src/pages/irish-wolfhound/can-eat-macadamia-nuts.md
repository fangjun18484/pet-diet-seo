# Can an Irish Wolfhound Eat Macadamia Nuts?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

Do not feed macadamia nuts to your Irish Wolfhound. Regardless of their massive size, macadamia nuts are **toxic to all dogs**, and giving them to your pet can lead to a serious medical emergency.

---

### Why Macadamia Nuts Are Dangerous
While the exact toxin in macadamia nuts hasn't been fully identified, they contain compounds that trigger a systemic reaction in dogs. Because Irish Wolfhounds are giant breeds, they may be able to ingest a slightly larger quantity before showing severe symptoms compared to a Chihuahua, but the toxicity remains the same. 

**Never assume your dog's size makes them immune to toxins.**

---

### Potential Risks & Symptoms
If an Irish Wolfhound consumes macadamia nuts, you may notice the following symptoms, typically appearing within 12 hours:

*   **Lethargy:** Unusual tiredness or weakness in the hind legs.
*   **Vomiting:** Frequent stomach upset.
*   **Hyperthermia:** An increase in body temperature (fever).
*   **Tremors:** In severe cases, muscle tremors or disorientation.
*   **Loss of Appetite:** Refusal to eat their regular meals.

---

### What to Do If Your Dog Ate Macadamia Nuts
If your Irish Wolfhound has accidentally ingested macadamia nuts, take the following steps immediately:

1.  **Contact Your Vet Immediately:** Call your veterinarian or a Pet Poison Helpline.
2.  **Identify the Quantity:** Try to determine how many nuts were eaten and if they were salted or flavored (which can add further complications).
3.  **Monitor Symptoms:** Keep a close eye on their movement and energy levels.
4.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting:** Unless specifically instructed to do so by a licensed veterinary professional.

### Safe Alternatives for Your Giant Breed
If you want to give your Irish Wolfhound a healthy, crunchable treat, try these dog-safe options instead:
*   **Carrot sticks** (Low calorie and great for dental health).
*   **Apple slices** (Seedless and core-free).
*   **Plain blueberries** (Packed with antioxidants).
*   **Cucumber slices** (Hydrating and refreshing).
