# Can a Papillon Eat Strawberries? 🍓

**Verdict: YES** ✅

## Why Strawberries are Safe for Papillons
Strawberries are non-toxic and safe for dogs of all sizes, including the petite Papillon. They are a low-calorie treat that provides a burst of flavor without the harmful additives found in many store-bought dog treats.

## Potential Benefits & Risks

### The Benefits 🌟
*   **Antioxidants:** Strawberries are rich in antioxidants, which help protect your dog's cells from damage.
*   **Vitamin C:** Supports a healthy immune system.
*   **Fiber:** Promotes healthy digestion and gut health.
*   **Low Calorie:** A great alternative for Papillons prone to weight gain.

### The Risks ⚠️
*   **Natural Sugars:** While safe, strawberries contain fructose. Overfeeding can lead to weight gain or upset the stomach of dogs with diabetes.
*   **Digestive Upset:** Some dogs may experience mild diarrhea if they are introduced to new fruits too quickly.
*   **Choking Hazard:** Because Papillons have small mouths and throats, large chunks of fruit can be a choking risk.

## How to Safely Feed Strawberries to Your Papillon

To ensure your Papillon enjoys their treat safely, follow these preparation steps:

1.  **Wash Thoroughly:** Remove any pesticides or dirt by rinsing the berries under cold water.
2.  **Remove the Greenery:** Strip away the stems and leaves (the hull), as these are not beneficial and can be hard to digest.
3.  **Slice Into Tiny Pieces:** Since Papillons are a toy breed, cut the strawberry into small, bite-sized cubes to prevent choking.
4.  **Practice Moderation:** Treat strawberries as a *treat*, not a meal. Limit them to **1–2 small pieces** a day.

**Pro Tip:** Try freezing strawberry slices for a refreshing, cooling snack during the summer months!
