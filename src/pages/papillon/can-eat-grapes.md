# Can a Papillon Eat Grapes? 🍇

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

**Absolutely not. You should never feed grapes or raisins to a Papillon, regardless of the variety or amount.**

---

## Why Grapes are Dangerous for Papillons
While some fruits are healthy treats, grapes and raisins are highly toxic to dogs. Recent veterinary research suggests that **tartaric acid** found in grapes is the primary cause of toxicity. 

Because Papillons are a small breed, their lower body mass means that even a single grape or a few raisins can trigger a severe toxic reaction.

## The Risks: What Happens?
Feeding a grape to your Papillon can lead to **acute kidney failure**. This is a medical emergency that can progress rapidly. 

**Watch for these warning signs:**
*   **Vomiting and Diarrhea:** Often occurs shortly after ingestion.
*   **Lethargy:** Unusual tiredness or weakness.
*   **Loss of Appetite:** Refusing their favorite treats or meals.
*   **Changes in Urination:** A significant decrease in urine output (a sign of kidney failure).
*   **Dehydration:** Dry gums and skin.

## What to Do If Your Papillon Ate a Grape
If your dog accidentally ingested a grape or raisin, **do not wait for symptoms to appear.** Once symptoms show, kidney damage may already be advanced.

### Immediate Action Steps:
1.  **Call Your Vet Immediately:** Contact your veterinarian or the nearest emergency animal clinic.
2.  **Contact Poison Control:** You can call the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center at **(888) 420-9000**.
3.  **Provide Details:** Be ready to tell the vet how many grapes were eaten, the approximate time of ingestion, and your dog's weight.
4.  **Follow Medical Advice:** The vet may induce vomiting or administer activated charcoal to prevent the toxin from entering the bloodstream.

## Safe Fruit Alternatives for Papillons
If you want to give your Papillon a sweet, healthy snack, try these dog-safe options instead:
*   **Blueberries:** Packed with antioxidants.
*   **Apple Slices:** (Remove all seeds and the core).
*   **Watermelon:** (Remove the seeds and rind).
*   **Banana:** In small, moderate portions.
