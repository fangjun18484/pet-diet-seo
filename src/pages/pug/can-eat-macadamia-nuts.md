# Can Pugs Eat Macadamia Nuts? 🚫

**Verdict: NO.** 

Pugs, like all dogs, **should never eat macadamia nuts.** These nuts are highly toxic to canines and can lead to severe health complications.

---

### Why are Macadamia Nuts Dangerous for Pugs?
While humans enjoy them as a healthy snack, macadamia nuts contain a compound (though the exact toxin is still being studied) that affects a dog's nervous system. Because Pugs are small, even a small amount of these nuts can trigger a toxic reaction.

### Potential Risks & Symptoms
If your Pug ingests macadamia nuts, you may notice symptoms appearing within **3 to 12 hours**. Be on the lookout for:

*   **Lethargy:** Unusual tiredness or weakness in the hind legs.
*   **Vomiting:** Frequent upset stomach or nausea.
*   **Hyperthermia:** An increase in body temperature (fever).
*   **Tremors:** Muscle shaking or loss of coordination (ataxia).
*   **Depression:** A noticeable drop in their usual bubbly Pug personality.

---

### What to Do If Your Pug Ate Macadamia Nuts
If you suspect your Pug has eaten macadamia nuts—whether raw, roasted, or as part of a cookie—**treat this as a medical emergency.**

1.  **Call Your Vet Immediately:** Contact your veterinarian or an emergency pet clinic right away.
2.  **Identify the Amount:** Try to determine how many nuts were consumed and when.
3.  **Monitor Behavior:** Keep a close eye on your dog for any of the symptoms listed above.
4.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting** unless specifically instructed to do so by a professional.

### Safe, Healthy Alternatives for Your Pug
Want to give your Pug a crunchy treat? Skip the nuts and try these dog-safe options instead:
*   **Fresh Carrots:** Low calorie and great for dental health.
*   **Blueberries:** Packed with antioxidants.
*   **Apple Slices:** (Remove the seeds and core first!)
*   **Plain Cucumber:** A hydrating, low-calorie snack.
