# Can Shetland Sheepdogs Eat Blueberries?

**Verdict: ✅ YES**

Shetland Sheepdogs (Shelties) can safely eat blueberries. These tiny, nutrient-packed berries make an excellent low-calorie treat for your active herding dog.

---

## Why Blueberries Are Safe for Shelties
Blueberries are non-toxic to dogs and are naturally low in sugar compared to many other fruits. Because Shelties can be prone to weight gain if their activity levels drop, blueberries provide a healthy alternative to processed, high-calorie dog treats.

## Potential Health Benefits
Adding a few blueberries to your Sheltie's diet can provide several nutritional boosts:

*   **Rich in Antioxidants:** Blueberries are loaded with anthocyanins, which help combat oxidative stress and support cognitive function as your dog ages.
*   **Immune Support:** High levels of Vitamin C and K help strengthen the immune system.
*   **Digestive Health:** The natural fiber found in berries promotes healthy digestion and regular bowel movements.
*   **Low Calorie:** They are a great way to reward your dog during training without adding unnecessary weight.

## Potential Risks to Consider
While blueberries are safe, keep these three precautions in mind:

1.  **Sugar Content:** While lower than some fruits, blueberries still contain natural sugars. Overfeeding can lead to weight gain or upset the stomach of dogs with diabetes.
2.  **Choking Hazard:** While rare for a medium-sized dog like a Sheltie, always supervise your dog while they eat whole berries.
3.  **Processed Products:** **Never** feed your dog blueberry muffins, jams, or dried blueberries containing **Xylitol** (a toxic artificial sweetener) or added refined sugars.

## How to Safely Serve Blueberries to Your Sheltie

To ensure your Sheltie enjoys their treat safely, follow these simple guidelines:

*   **Fresh & Raw:** Wash the berries thoroughly to remove any pesticides or dirt.
*   **Frozen:** Frozen blueberries are a fantastic cooling treat during the summer months.
*   **Moderation is Key:** Follow the **"10% Rule"**—treats (including fruit) should make up no more than 10% of your dog's daily caloric intake. For a Sheltie, a small handful (5–10 berries) is usually plenty.
*   **Introduce Slowly:** If it's their first time eating blueberries, give them one or two and monitor for any signs of digestive upset.
