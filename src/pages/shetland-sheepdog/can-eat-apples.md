# Can Shetland Sheepdogs Eat Apples? 🍎

**Verdict: YES**

Shetland Sheepdogs (Shelties) can safely enjoy apples as a treat. Apples are non-toxic and provide a refreshing, crunchy snack that fits well into a balanced canine diet.

---

## Why Apples Are Safe for Shelties
Apples are packed with essential nutrients that support a dog's overall health. Since Shelties are active, intelligent dogs, a low-calorie treat like apple is an excellent alternative to processed dog biscuits.

### 🌟 The Benefits
*   **Vitamin A & C:** Supports a strong immune system and healthy skin.
*   **Fiber:** Aids in healthy digestion and helps regulate bowel movements.
*   **Low Calorie:** A great option for keeping your Sheltie lean and agile.
*   **Dental Health:** The crunch of a fresh apple slice can help reduce plaque buildup.

### ⚠️ The Risks
While the flesh of the apple is safe, certain parts are dangerous:
*   **Apple Seeds:** Contain small amounts of **cyanide**, which can be toxic if consumed in large quantities.
*   **The Core:** This is a significant choking hazard and can cause intestinal blockages.
*   **Sugar Content:** Apples contain natural sugars. While safe, too many can lead to weight gain or spike blood sugar in diabetic dogs.

---

## How to Safely Feed Apples to Your Sheltie

To ensure your Sheltie enjoys their treat without risk, follow these simple preparation steps:

1.  **Wash Thoroughly:** Remove any pesticides or waxes from the skin.
2.  **Core and De-seed:** Completely remove the center and all seeds.
3.  **Slice into Bite-Sized Pieces:** Shelties have medium-sized mouths, but small slices prevent choking.
4.  **Practice Moderation:** Apples should be treated as a snack, not a meal. Follow the **"10% Rule"**—treats should never make up more than 10% of your dog's daily caloric intake.
