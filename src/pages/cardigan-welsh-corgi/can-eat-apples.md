# Can a Cardigan Welsh Corgi Eat Apples?

**Verdict: ✅ YES**

Cardigan Welsh Corgis can safely eat apples. They make a fantastic, low-calorie treat that provides a satisfying crunch for your dog.

---

### Why Apples are Safe for Corgis
Apples are non-toxic to dogs and are packed with essential nutrients. While Corgis are prone to weight gain (which can put pressure on their long spines), apples are a healthier alternative to processed store-bought treats.

### Potential Benefits & Risks

#### 🌟 The Benefits
*   **Vitamin Boost:** Apples are rich in Vitamin A and Vitamin C.
*   **Digestive Health:** The fiber in apples helps regulate bowel movements.
*   **Dental Hygiene:** Chewing on a crisp slice of apple can help clean teeth and freshen breath.
*   **Low Calorie:** A great option for Corgis needing to maintain a lean weight.

#### ⚠️ The Risks
*   **Apple Seeds:** These contain a small amount of **cyanide**. While a few seeds likely won't hurt, they should always be avoided.
*   **The Core:** The tough center of the apple is a choking hazard and can cause gastrointestinal blockages.
*   **Sugar Content:** Apples contain natural sugars. Overfeeding can lead to weight gain or cause issues for dogs with diabetes.

---

### How to Safely Prepare Apples for Your Corgi

To ensure your Cardigan Welsh Corgi enjoys their snack safely, follow these three steps:

1.  **Wash Thoroughly:** Rinse the apple under cold water to remove any pesticides or wax.
2.  **Remove the Core and Seeds:** Slice the apple and carefully remove the center and all seeds.
3.  **Cut into Bite-Sized Pieces:** Corgis can be enthusiastic eaters; cutting the apple into small, manageable cubes prevents choking.

**Pro Tip:** Keep apple treats to a minimum—no more than **one or two slices a day**. Treats should never exceed 10% of your dog's daily caloric intake.
