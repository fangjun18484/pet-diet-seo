# Can a Cardigan Welsh Corgi Eat Garlic?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

## Why You Should Avoid Feeding Garlic to Your Corgi
While garlic is a staple in human kitchens, it is toxic to dogs, including the Cardigan Welsh Corgi. Garlic belongs to the **Allium family** (along with onions, shallots, and chives). 

These plants contain compounds called **thiosulfates**. While humans can digest these easily, dogs lack the enzyme necessary to process them. When ingested, thiosulfates cause oxidative damage to the red blood cells, leading them to rupture—a condition known as **hemolytic anemia**.

## Potential Risks and Symptoms
The toxicity of garlic is cumulative, meaning small amounts over a long period can be just as dangerous as one large dose. Because Corgis are smaller dogs, they may reach toxic thresholds faster than larger breeds.

**Watch for these warning signs of garlic poisoning:**
*   **Lethargy:** Unusual tiredness or weakness.
*   **Pale Gums:** A sign of decreased red blood cell count (anemia).
*   **Loss of Appetite:** Refusing food or treats.
*   **Rapid Heart Rate:** Difficulty breathing or panting.
*   **Dark-Colored Urine:** A result of hemoglobin being released into the bloodstream.

## What to Do If Your Corgi Ate Garlic
If your Cardigan Welsh Corgi accidentally ingested garlic powder, raw garlic, or a dish containing garlic, take the following steps immediately:

1.  **Remove the Source:** Ensure they cannot eat any more of the food.
2.  **Assess the Amount:** Determine how much was eaten and whether it was concentrated (garlic powder is significantly more potent than fresh garlic).
3.  **Call Your Veterinarian:** Contact your vet or an emergency pet poison helpline immediately. 
4.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting** unless specifically instructed to do so by a medical professional.
5.  **Monitor Behavior:** Keep a close eye on their energy levels and gum color until you can get professional medical advice.

### Safe Alternatives for Flavor
If you want to spice up your Corgi's meal safely, try these dog-friendly options:
*   **Fresh Parsley:** Great for breath and safe in moderation.
*   **Blueberries:** A healthy, antioxidant-rich treat.
*   **Plain Pumpkin:** Excellent for digestion and flavor.
