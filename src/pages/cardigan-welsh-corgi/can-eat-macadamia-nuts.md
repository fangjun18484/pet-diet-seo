# Can a Cardigan Welsh Corgi Eat Macadamia Nuts?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

## Why Macadamia Nuts Are Dangerous for Corgis
Regardless of their breed, **Cardigan Welsh Corgis cannot eat macadamia nuts.** These nuts contain a specific toxin (though the exact compound is not fully understood) that affects a dog's nervous system. Even a small amount can lead to toxicity, and because Corgis are smaller dogs, they are particularly susceptible to adverse reactions.

## Potential Risks & Symptoms
Feeding macadamia nuts to your Corgi can lead to **Macadamia Nut Toxicosis**. Symptoms typically appear within 12 hours of ingestion and may include:

*   **Lethargy:** Unusual tiredness or weakness in the hind legs.
*   **Vomiting:** Sudden nausea and gastric distress.
*   **Hyperthermia:** An increase in body temperature (fever).
*   **Tremors:** In severe cases, muscle tremors or disorientation.
*   **Loss of Appetite:** Refusal to eat their normal meals.

## What to Do If Your Corgi Ate Macadamia Nuts
If you suspect your Cardigan Welsh Corgi has ingested macadamia nuts, **treat this as a medical emergency.** Follow these steps immediately:

1.  **Remove Access:** Ensure your dog cannot eat any more of the nuts.
2.  **Call Your Vet:** Contact your veterinarian or a local emergency animal clinic immediately.
3.  **Contact Poison Control:** You can call the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center at **(888) 426-4435**.
4.  **Gather Information:** Note the approximate number of nuts eaten and how long ago it happened to help your vet determine the treatment plan.
5.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting:** Never induce vomiting unless specifically instructed to do so by a licensed veterinary professional.

### Safe Alternatives for Your Corgi
If you want to give your Corgi a crunchy, healthy treat, try these dog-safe options instead:
*   **Carrot sticks** (Low calorie and great for teeth)
*   **Apple slices** (Seedless and core-free)
*   **Plain blueberries** (Packed with antioxidants)
