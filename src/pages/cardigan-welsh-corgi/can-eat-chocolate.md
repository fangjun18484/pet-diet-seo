# Can a Cardigan Welsh Corgi Eat Chocolate?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

---

### Why Chocolate is Dangerous for Corgis
Chocolate contains **theobromine** and **caffeine**, both of which are methylxanthines. While humans can metabolize these compounds easily, dogs—including Cardigan Welsh Corgis—process them very slowly. 

This leads to a dangerous buildup in their system, which can overstimulate the central nervous system and the heart, potentially leading to toxicity.

### The Risks: What Happens if They Eat It?
There are no benefits to feeding chocolate to a Corgi. The risks are severe and depend on the type of chocolate (dark and baking chocolate are the most toxic) and the size of the dog.

**Potential symptoms of chocolate poisoning include:**
*   **Hyperactivity:** Unusual restlessness or pacing.
*   **Digestive Distress:** Vomiting and diarrhea.
*   **Cardiac Issues:** Rapid heart rate or irregular heartbeat.
*   **Neurological Problems:** Muscle tremors or seizures.
*   **Severe Cases:** Coma or death.

### What to Do if Your Corgi Ate Chocolate
If your Cardigan Welsh Corgi has ingested chocolate, **do not wait for symptoms to appear.** Take the following steps immediately:

1.  **Identify the Source:** Determine what kind of chocolate was eaten (e.g., milk chocolate vs. 90% dark chocolate) and estimate the amount.
2.  **Call Your Vet Immediately:** Contact your veterinarian or an emergency animal clinic right away.
3.  **Call Pet Poison Helpline:** If your vet is unavailable, call the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center.
4.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting Unless Instructed:** Only induce vomiting if a licensed veterinary professional tells you to do so over the phone.

### Safe, Dog-Friendly Alternatives
Want to give your Corgi a special treat? Skip the cocoa and try these dog-safe options:
*   **Fresh Blueberries:** Packed with antioxidants.
*   **Apple Slices:** (Remove the seeds and core first).
*   **Carrot Sticks:** Great for chewing and low in calories.
*   **Plain Pumpkin:** Pureed pumpkin (not pie filling) is great for digestion.
