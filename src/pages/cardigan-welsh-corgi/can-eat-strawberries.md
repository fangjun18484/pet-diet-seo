# Can a Cardigan Welsh Corgi Eat Strawberries?

**Verdict: ✅ YES**

### Why are Strawberries Safe for Cardigan Welsh Corgis?
Strawberries are non-toxic and generally safe for dogs, including the Cardigan Welsh Corgi. They are low in calories and high in essential nutrients, making them a healthy alternative to processed store-bought treats.

---

### 🍓 Potential Benefits & Risks

#### The Benefits
*   **Rich in Antioxidants:** Strawberries contain anthocyanins and Vitamin C, which help support your Corgi's immune system.
*   **Fiber Content:** Great for digestive health and keeping their coat shiny.
*   **Low Calorie:** Since Corgis are prone to obesity, strawberries provide a sweet flavor without adding excessive weight.
*   **Dental Health:** The texture can help gently clean teeth and freshen breath.

#### The Risks
*   **Natural Sugars:** While healthy, strawberries contain fructose. Too many can lead to weight gain or blood sugar spikes in dogs with diabetes.
*   **Digestive Upset:** Some dogs may experience mild stomach upset if they are introduced to new fruits too quickly.
*   **Choking Hazard:** Whole strawberries or stems can pose a choking risk for smaller dogs.

---

### 🛒 How to Safely Feed Strawberries to Your Corgi

To ensure your Cardigan Welsh Corgi enjoys their treat safely, follow these preparation steps:

1.  **Wash Thoroughly:** Rinse the berries to remove any pesticides or dirt.
2.  **Remove the Greenery:** Always cut off the stem and leaves (the hulls), as these can be difficult to digest.
3.  **Slice into Bite-Sized Pieces:** Cut the strawberry into small, manageable chunks to prevent choking.
4.  **Practice Moderation:** Treat strawberries as a **treat, not a meal**. Follow the "10% Rule"—treats should make up no more than 10% of your dog's daily caloric intake.
5.  **Avoid Added Sugars:** Never feed your Corgi strawberries from a jam, syrup, or dessert, as these often contain Xylitol (toxic) or excessive sugar.

**Pro Tip:** Try freezing strawberry slices for a refreshing, cooling treat during the summer months!
