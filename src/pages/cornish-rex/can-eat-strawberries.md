# Can a Cornish Rex Eat Strawberries?

**Verdict: YES (In Moderation)**

### Why Strawberries Are Safe for Cornish Rex
Strawberries are non-toxic to cats, including the Cornish Rex. While cats are **obligate carnivores**—meaning their primary diet must consist of meat—they can safely consume small amounts of certain fruits as occasional treats. Strawberries do not contain the harmful compounds found in grapes or raisins, making them a safe, occasional snack.

---

### Potential Benefits & Risks

#### 🍓 The Benefits
While your cat won't crave strawberries like a human would, a tiny piece provides:
*   **Antioxidants:** Helps combat free radicals in the body.
*   **Vitamin C:** Supports overall cellular health.
*   **Hydration:** High water content helps maintain fluid intake.
*   **Fiber:** A small amount can aid in digestion.

#### ⚠️ The Risks
Despite being safe, there are a few precautions to keep in mind:
*   **Sugar Content:** Strawberries contain natural sugars. Overfeeding can lead to weight gain or exacerbate issues for cats with **diabetes**.
*   **Digestive Upset:** Some cats have sensitive stomachs; introducing new foods too quickly can cause diarrhea or vomiting.
*   **Low Nutritional Necessity:** Cats cannot taste "sweetness," so they gain no nutritional requirement from fruit.

---

### How to Safely Feed Strawberries to Your Cornish Rex

If you want to share a strawberry with your curly-coated companion, follow these safety steps:

1.  **Wash Thoroughly:** Remove all pesticides and dirt by rinsing the berry under cold water.
2.  **Remove the Greenery:** Cut off the stem and leaves (the hulls), as these are unnecessary and harder to digest.
3.  **Cut into Tiny Pieces:** Offer a small, bite-sized cube (about the size of a pea) to prevent choking.
4.  **Moderation is Key:** One small piece once or twice a week is plenty. Treats should never exceed **10% of your cat's daily caloric intake**.
5.  **Avoid Processed Versions:** Never feed your cat strawberry jam, syrups, or desserts, as these contain added sugars and artificial sweeteners (like Xylitol) that are **toxic** to cats.

**Pro Tip:** If this is your Cornish Rex's first time trying strawberries, monitor them for 24 hours to ensure they don't have an adverse digestive reaction.
