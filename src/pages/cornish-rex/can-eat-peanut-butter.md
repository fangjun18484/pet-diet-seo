# Can a Cornish Rex Eat Peanut Butter? 🥜

**Verdict: YES (In Moderation)**

While peanut butter is not a natural part of a cat's diet, it is generally safe for Cornish Rex cats to eat in small quantities, provided the ingredients are clean.

---

### Why is Peanut Butter Safe (But Not Necessary)?
Cornish Rex cats, like all cats, are **obligate carnivores**, meaning their primary nutritional needs are met through animal proteins. They do not require nuts, seeds, or legumes to thrive. However, a small lick of peanut butter is non-toxic and can serve as an occasional high-value treat.

---

### Potential Benefits & Risks

#### ✅ The Benefits
*   **High Protein:** Peanuts provide a concentrated source of protein.
*   **Healthy Fats:** Contains omega-6 fatty acids which can support skin health (important for the unique coat of a Cornish Rex).
*   **Palatability:** It can be used to hide pills or encourage a picky eater.

#### ❌ The Risks
*   **Xylitol Toxicity:** **CRITICAL.** Some "low sugar" or "diet" peanut butters contain Xylitol (birch sugar), which is extremely toxic to cats and can be fatal.
*   **High Calorie Count:** Cornish Rexes are active, but too many calories can lead to obesity and diabetes.
*   **Pancreatitis:** The high fat content can trigger pancreatitis in sensitive cats.
*   **Excess Sodium:** Many commercial brands add salt, which can strain a cat's kidneys over time.

---

### How to Safely Feed Peanut Butter to Your Cornish Rex

If you want to give your cat a taste, follow these safety guidelines:

1.  **Read the Label First:** Ensure the only ingredients are **peanuts** and perhaps a bit of salt. **Avoid any product containing Xylitol, sugar, or chocolate.**
2.  **Keep it Tiny:** Treat peanut butter as a "once-in-a-while" snack. A portion the size of a **small pea** is sufficient.
3.  **Watch for Allergies:** While rare, some cats are allergic to peanuts. Monitor your cat for itching, swelling, or vomiting after the first taste.
4.  **Avoid "Reduced Fat" Versions:** These often replace fats with artificial sweeteners (like Xylitol) which are dangerous.

**Pro Tip:** If you are using peanut butter to hide medication, ensure you rinse your cat's mouth or provide water afterward to prevent the sticky residue from trapping food debris against their teeth.
