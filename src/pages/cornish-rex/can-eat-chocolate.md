# Can a Cornish Rex Eat Chocolate?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

---

## Why is Chocolate Dangerous for Cornish Rex Cats?
Like all cats, the Cornish Rex cannot metabolize **theobromine** and **caffeine**, two methylxanthines found in cocoa beans. While humans digest these compounds easily, cats process them very slowly. This leads to a toxic buildup in their system that can overstimulate the central nervous system and the heart.

Regardless of the breed's unique curly coat or lean physique, their internal biology is the same as any other domestic cat—**chocolate is toxic.**

---

## The Risks: What Happens if They Eat It?

There are no benefits to feeding chocolate to a cat; only significant health risks. The severity of the toxicity depends on the type of chocolate (darker = more dangerous) and the amount consumed.

### Common Symptoms of Chocolate Poisoning:
*   **Gastrointestinal Distress:** Vomiting and diarrhea.
*   **Hyperactivity:** Extreme restlessness, pacing, or "zoomies" that seem abnormal.
*   **Cardiovascular Stress:** Rapid heart rate (tachycardia) or irregular heart rhythms.
*   **Neurological Issues:** Muscle tremors, stiffness, or seizures.
*   **Severe Cases:** Coma or death.

---

## What to Do if Your Cornish Rex Ate Chocolate

If you suspect your cat has ingested chocolate, **treat it as a medical emergency.** Do not wait for symptoms to appear.

### Immediate Action Plan:
1.  **Remove the Source:** Take the chocolate away immediately to prevent further ingestion.
2.  **Identify the Product:** Note the **type of chocolate** (e.g., baking chocolate, milk chocolate, white chocolate) and the **approximate amount** eaten.
3.  **Call Your Vet:** Contact your veterinarian or an emergency pet clinic immediately.
4.  **Provide Details:** Tell the vet your cat's weight and when the ingestion occurred.
5.  **Do NOT Induce Vomiting:** Never attempt to make your cat vomit unless specifically instructed to do so by a licensed veterinarian, as this can cause aspiration pneumonia.

### Safe Alternatives for Treats
If you want to spoil your Cornish Rex, stick to cat-safe treats such as:
*   Small pieces of cooked chicken or turkey.
*   Commercial cat treats designed for feline nutrition.
*   A small pinch of catnip.
