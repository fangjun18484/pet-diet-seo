# Can a Cornish Rex Eat Bread?

**Verdict: YES (In moderation)**

While bread is not toxic to cats, it provides **zero nutritional value** for a Cornish Rex. It should be treated as an occasional treat rather than a part of their regular diet.

---

## Why Bread is "Okay" but Not Ideal
Cornish Rex cats, like all felines, are **obligate carnivores**. This means their bodies are biologically designed to derive nutrients from animal proteins and fats, not carbohydrates. 

Bread consists primarily of processed flour and sugar. Since cats lack the enzymes to efficiently process large amounts of carbs, bread acts as "empty calories" that can lead to health complications if fed frequently.

---

## Potential Benefits & Risks

### ❌ The Risks
*   **Weight Gain & Diabetes:** Excessive carbs can lead to obesity and insulin resistance, especially in less active indoor cats.
*   **Digestive Upset:** Some Cornish Rex cats have sensitive stomachs; gluten or yeast in bread can cause bloating, gas, or diarrhea.
*   **Toxic Ingredients:** Many breads contain additives that are **dangerous** to cats, such as:
    *   **Garlic and Onion powder** (can cause anemia).
    *   **Raisins or Grapes** (can cause kidney failure).
    *   **Xylitol** (an artificial sweetener found in some specialty breads that is highly toxic).

### ✅ The "Benefits"
*   **Psychological Reward:** For some cats, a tiny piece of plain bread is a tasty novelty or a bonding experience with their owner.

---

## How to Safely Feed Bread to Your Cornish Rex

If you want to give your cat a small taste, follow these strict safety guidelines:

1.  **Keep it Plain:** Only offer plain white or whole-wheat bread. Avoid sourdough (which can be too acidic) or flavored breads.
2.  **Size Matters:** A piece the size of a **fingernail** is plenty. Do not let it become a meal replacement.
3.  **Toast it:** Lightly toasting the bread can sometimes make it easier for cats to digest and less likely to cause stomach upset.
4.  **Check the Label:** Ensure there are no seeds, nuts, or artificial sweeteners.

### ⚠️ What to do if they ate "Forbidden" Bread
If your Cornish Rex accidentally ate bread containing **garlic, onions, or raisins**, contact your veterinarian or an animal poison control center immediately. These ingredients can cause serious internal damage regardless of the amount consumed.
