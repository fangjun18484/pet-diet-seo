# Can a Cornish Rex Eat Macadamia Nuts?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

## Why Macadamia Nuts are Dangerous for Cornish Rex Cats
While your Cornish Rex might be curious and adventurous, **macadamia nuts are toxic to cats.** These nuts contain compounds that can cause neurological distress and metabolic upset. Regardless of the breed, cats lack the enzymes to process the high fat and specific oils found in macadamia nuts, making them a dangerous addition to their diet.

## Potential Risks and Side Effects
Feeding macadamia nuts to a Cornish Rex can lead to several serious health complications:

*   **Pancreatitis:** Due to the extremely high fat content, macadamia nuts can trigger inflammation of the pancreas, which is painful and potentially life-threatening.
*   **Neurological Issues:** Some cats may experience lethargy, disorientation, or muscle weakness.
*   **Gastrointestinal Distress:** Expect symptoms such as vomiting, diarrhea, and abdominal pain.
*   **Weight Gain & Metabolic Stress:** Cornish Rex cats have a unique metabolism; introducing calorie-dense, toxic fats can disrupt their health balance.

## What to Do If Your Cat Ate a Macadamia Nut
If your Cornish Rex has accidentally ingested a macadamia nut, do not panic, but **act quickly.**

1.  **Remove Access:** Immediately take the nuts away from your cat to prevent further consumption.
2.  **Contact Your Vet:** Call your veterinarian or an emergency pet poison control center immediately.
3.  **Monitor Symptoms:** Keep a close eye on your cat for signs of vomiting, weakness, or tremors.
4.  **Provide Fresh Water:** Ensure they have plenty of water to help their system, but do not attempt to induce vomiting unless specifically instructed by a professional.

### Safe Alternatives for Your Cornish Rex
If you want to give your cat a healthy treat, skip the nuts and try these vet-approved options:
*   **Small pieces of cooked chicken** (plain, no seasoning).
*   **Commercial cat treats** formulated for their specific nutritional needs.
*   **A small piece of steamed fish** (salmon or tuna in water).
