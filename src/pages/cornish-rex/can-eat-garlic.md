# Can a Cornish Rex Eat Garlic?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

---

### Why Garlic is Dangerous for Cornish Rex Cats
While your Cornish Rex may be curious and adventurous, garlic is **toxic** to all cats, regardless of breed. Garlic belongs to the *Allium* family (which includes onions, leeks, and chives). 

These plants contain compounds called **thiosulfates**. While humans can digest these easily, cats lack the enzyme necessary to process them. When a cat ingest garlic, the thiosulfates cause oxidative damage to their red blood cells, leading to a serious condition called **hemolytic anemia**.

---

### Potential Risks & Symptoms
Because the damage happens at a cellular level, you might not see a reaction immediately. The risks include:

*   **Hemolytic Anemia:** The destruction of red blood cells, reducing the blood's ability to carry oxygen.
*   **Lethargy:** Extreme tiredness or weakness due to lack of oxygen in the blood.
*   **Pale Gums:** A sign that the cat is becoming anemic.
*   **Rapid Heart Rate:** The heart works harder to pump the remaining healthy blood cells.
*   **Digestive Distress:** Vomiting or diarrhea.

**Note:** Garlic powder is more concentrated than fresh garlic, making it even more dangerous in smaller amounts.

---

### What to Do If Your Cat Ate Garlic
If your Cornish Rex has ingested garlic or a food containing garlic powder, follow these steps immediately:

1.  **Remove the Source:** Ensure they cannot eat any more of the food.
2.  **Call Your Vet:** Contact your veterinarian or a Pet Poison Helpline immediately. Be ready to tell them **how much** was eaten and **when**.
3.  **Monitor for Symptoms:** Keep a close eye on their breathing and gum color.
4.  **Do NOT Induce Vomiting:** Never attempt to make your cat vomit unless specifically instructed to do so by a licensed veterinary professional.

**Safe Alternatives:** If you want to give your cat a special treat, stick to cat-safe greens like a tiny piece of steamed broccoli or a pinch of organic catnip.
