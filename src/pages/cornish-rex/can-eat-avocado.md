# Can a Cornish Rex Eat Avocado? 🥑

**Verdict: NO**

While your Cornish Rex might be curious about your toast, you should **avoid feeding avocado to your cat.** 

---

## Why Avocado is Risky for Cats
The primary concern with avocados is a fungicidal toxin called **persin**. While cats are generally more resistant to persin than birds or horses, it can still cause digestive upset in some felines. 

Beyond the toxin, avocados are extremely high in fat. While the Cornish Rex is an active breed, their digestive systems are not designed to process concentrated amounts of plant-based fats, which can lead to more serious health complications.

---

## Potential Risks & Side Effects

If your Cornish Rex consumes avocado, you should watch for the following risks:

*   **Pancreatitis:** The high fat content can trigger inflammation of the pancreas, a painful and potentially life-threatening condition.
*   **Gastrointestinal Distress:** Vomiting, diarrhea, and abdominal pain are common reactions.
*   **Choking Hazard:** The large avocado pit is a significant choking risk and can cause intestinal blockages if swallowed.
*   **Toxicity:** Though rare, some cats may react poorly to persin, leading to lethargy or respiratory distress.

---

## What to Do If Your Cat Ate Avocado

If your Cornish Rex sneaked a bite of your guacamole or a piece of avocado, don't panic, but do take action:

1.  **Assess the Amount:** A tiny lick of a ripe avocado is unlikely to be fatal, but larger quantities are a cause for concern.
2.  **Monitor for Symptoms:** Keep a close eye on your cat for the next 24 hours. Look for vomiting, lethargy, or a loss of appetite.
3.  **Check for Pit Ingestion:** If you suspect your cat swallowed the pit, **contact a veterinarian immediately**, as this is a surgical emergency.
4.  **Call the Vet:** When in doubt, call your vet or a Pet Poison Helpline. Provide them with your cat's weight and the approximate amount of avocado consumed.

### Safe Alternatives for Your Cornish Rex
If you want to give your cat a healthy treat, skip the avocado and try:
*   **Cooked Salmon** (plain, no seasoning)
*   **Small pieces of steamed Broccoli**
*   **Commercial Cat Treats** formulated for feline nutrition
