# Can a Pembroke Welsh Corgi Eat Grapes?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

## Why Grapes Are Dangerous for Corgis
You should **never** feed grapes or raisins to a Pembroke Welsh Corgi. Unlike some foods that are only toxic in large quantities, grapes and raisins can cause **acute kidney failure** in dogs, regardless of the breed or the amount consumed.

While the exact toxin is still being studied, recent research points to **tartaric acid** as the primary cause of toxicity, which can lead to rapid organ failure.

## The Risks: What Happens if They Eat Grapes?
Because Corgis are food-motivated and opportunistic eaters, they may sneak a grape off the counter. The risks are severe and can manifest quickly:

*   **Kidney Failure:** The most critical risk; toxins attack the kidneys, preventing them from filtering waste from the blood.
*   **Gastrointestinal Distress:** Sudden vomiting and diarrhea.
*   **Lethargy:** A noticeable drop in energy levels and depression.
*   **Loss of Appetite:** Refusing food or water.
*   **Dehydration:** Due to vomiting and the kidneys' inability to regulate fluids.

## What to Do if Your Corgi Ate Grapes
If your Pembroke Welsh Corgi has ingested a grape or raisin, **this is a veterinary emergency.** Do not wait for symptoms to appear.

1.  **Call Your Vet Immediately:** Contact your primary veterinarian or the nearest emergency animal hospital.
2.  **Provide Details:** Tell the vet approximately how many grapes were eaten, the type of grape, and how long ago it happened.
3.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting Unless Instructed:** Only induce vomiting if your veterinarian explicitly tells you to do so over the phone.
4.  **Monitor closely:** Watch for any signs of distress while transporting them to the clinic.

## Safe Fruit Alternatives for Corgis
If you want to give your Corgi a healthy, sweet treat, swap the grapes for these dog-safe options:

*   **Blueberries:** Packed with antioxidants and a similar size to grapes.
*   **Apple Slices:** (Remove seeds and the core first).
*   **Watermelon:** (Remove seeds and rind).
*   **Banana:** In moderation, as a tasty treat.
