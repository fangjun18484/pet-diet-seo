# Can a Pembroke Welsh Corgi Eat Strawberries?

**Verdict: ✅ YES**

## Why Strawberries are Safe for Corgis
Strawberries are non-toxic and safe for dogs, including Pembroke Welsh Corgis. They are a low-calorie treat that provides a burst of flavor and essential nutrients without the risks associated with other fruits (like grapes or raisins).

## The Benefits and Risks

### 🌟 The Benefits
Adding a few strawberries to your Corgi's diet can provide:
*   **Antioxidants:** Rich in anthocyanins, which help combat free radicals.
*   **Vitamin C:** Supports a healthy immune system.
*   **Fiber:** Aids in healthy digestion.
*   **Hydration:** High water content helps keep your pup hydrated.

### ⚠️ The Potential Risks
While safe, there are a few things to keep in mind:
*   **Natural Sugars:** Strawberries contain fructose. Because Corgis are prone to obesity, too many strawberries can lead to weight gain.
*   **Digestive Upset:** Introducing any new food too quickly can cause stomach upset or diarrhea in sensitive dogs.
*   **Choking Hazard:** Large chunks can be a choking risk for smaller breeds.

## How to Safely Feed Strawberries to Your Corgi

To ensure your Pembroke Welsh Corgi enjoys their treat safely, follow these preparation steps:

1.  **Wash Thoroughly:** Rinse the berries to remove any pesticides or dirt.
2.  **Remove the Greens:** Cut off the stem and leaves (the hull), as these can be difficult to digest.
3.  **Slice into Bite-Sized Pieces:** Cut the berry into small, manageable pieces to prevent choking.
4.  **Moderation is Key:** Treat strawberries as a **treat, not a meal**. They should make up no more than 10% of your dog's daily caloric intake.
5.  **Avoid Added Sugars:** Never feed your dog strawberries from a syrup, jam, or sweetened dessert, as these often contain xylitol or excessive sugar.

**Pro Tip:** Try freezing strawberry slices for a refreshing, low-calorie summer snack for your Corgi!
