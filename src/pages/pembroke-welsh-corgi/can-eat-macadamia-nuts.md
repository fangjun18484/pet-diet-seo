# Can a Pembroke Welsh Corgi Eat Macadamia Nuts?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

---

## Why You Should Avoid Macadamia Nuts
**Macadamia nuts are toxic to all dogs, including Pembroke Welsh Corgis.** 

Unlike some foods that are simply "unhealthy," macadamia nuts contain a compound (though not fully identified by science) that acts as a neurotoxin in canines. Even a small amount can cause a significant adverse reaction in your Corgi's nervous system, leading to a state of toxicity.

## Potential Risks & Symptoms
Because Corgis are smaller dogs, they may reach toxic thresholds faster than larger breeds. If your Corgi consumes macadamia nuts, watch for these symptoms:

*   **Lethargy and Weakness:** Your normally energetic Corgi may suddenly seem exhausted.
*   **Hind-End Weakness:** Difficulty walking or "wobbly" back legs.
*   **Vomiting:** A common immediate reaction to the toxin.
*   **Hyperthermia:** An increase in body temperature.
*   **Tremors:** In severe cases, muscle shaking or seizures may occur.

**Are there any benefits?** No. There are no nutritional benefits to macadamia nuts that outweigh the severe risk of toxicity.

## What to Do If Your Corgi Ate Macadamia Nuts
If you suspect your Corgi has ingested macadamia nuts—whether raw, roasted, or as part of a cookie or candy—**treat this as a medical emergency.**

1.  **Contact Your Vet Immediately:** Call your veterinarian or a local emergency pet clinic right away.
2.  **Call Pet Poison Helpline:** You can also contact the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center.
3.  **Identify the Quantity:** Try to determine exactly how many nuts were eaten and when.
4.  **Do NOT Induce Vomiting:** Never attempt to make your dog vomit unless specifically instructed to do so by a licensed veterinary professional.
5.  **Monitor Behavior:** Keep your dog quiet and calm while transporting them to the clinic to avoid overstimulating their nervous system.

### Safe Alternatives for Your Corgi
If you want to give your Corgi a crunchy, healthy treat, try these dog-safe options instead:
*   **Blueberries** (Antioxidant-rich)
*   **Carrot slices** (Low calorie and great for teeth)
*   **Apple slices** (Seedless and skinless)
*   **Plain cooked pumpkin** (Great for digestion)
