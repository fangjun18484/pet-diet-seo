# Can a Munchkin Cat Eat Grapes?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

## Why You Should Never Feed Grapes to Your Munchkin Cat
Regardless of the breed, **grapes and raisins are highly toxic to cats**, including Munchkin cats. While the exact toxin has not been fully identified, ingestion can lead to sudden and severe **acute kidney failure**.

Because Munchkin cats are smaller than average due to their genetic chondrodysplasia, their bodies may be even more sensitive to toxins, making it critical to keep these fruits far out of their reach.

## The Risks of Grape Consumption
There are no nutritional benefits to feeding grapes to a cat; there are only severe health risks. Potential symptoms of grape poisoning include:

*   **Vomiting and Diarrhea:** Often the first sign of distress.
*   **Lethargy:** Extreme tiredness or weakness.
*   **Loss of Appetite:** Refusing their favorite treats or wet food.
*   **Dehydration:** Increased thirst or a complete lack of urination (indicating kidney failure).
*   **Neurological Issues:** In severe cases, disorientation or seizures.

## What to Do If Your Munchkin Cat Ate a Grape
If your cat has ingested a grape or raisin, **treat this as a medical emergency**. Do not wait for symptoms to appear, as kidney damage can happen rapidly.

1.  **Call Your Veterinarian Immediately:** Or contact a pet poison control center.
2.  **Remove Access:** Ensure no more grapes are available in the environment.
3.  **Note the Quantity:** Try to determine exactly how many grapes (and what type) were eaten to help your vet determine the treatment plan.
4.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting:** Never attempt to make your cat vomit unless specifically instructed to do so by a licensed professional.

### Safe Alternatives for Your Munchkin Cat
If you want to give your cat a fruity treat, stick to small, cat-safe options (in moderation):
*   **Blueberries** (in very small amounts)
*   **Cranberries**
*   **Watermelon** (seedless and rind-free)
