# Can Old English Sheepdogs Eat Strawberries?

**Verdict: ✅ YES**

## Why Strawberries Are Safe for Old English Sheepdogs
Strawberries are non-toxic and generally safe for dogs of all breeds, including the Old English Sheepdog. They are low in calories and packed with essential nutrients, making them an excellent alternative to processed dog treats.

## The Benefits and Risks

### 🍓 Potential Benefits
*   **Vitamin C:** Supports a healthy immune system and skin health.
*   **Antioxidants:** Helps fight free radicals and reduces inflammation.
*   **Fiber:** Promotes healthy digestion and bowel regularity.
*   **Hydration:** High water content helps keep your dog hydrated.

### ⚠️ Potential Risks
*   **Natural Sugars:** While healthy, strawberries contain fructose. Overfeeding can lead to weight gain or spike blood sugar levels in dogs with diabetes.
*   **Digestive Upset:** Introducing too many berries too quickly can cause diarrhea or an upset stomach in sensitive dogs.
*   **Choking Hazard:** While rare with soft berries, very large chunks could potentially be a risk for smaller dogs (though less likely for the larger Old English Sheepdog).

## How to Safely Feed Strawberries to Your Dog

To ensure your Old English Sheepdog enjoys this treat safely, follow these preparation steps:

1.  **Wash Thoroughly:** Always rinse strawberries under cold water to remove pesticides and dirt.
2.  **Remove the Greenery:** Strip off the leaves and the stem (the hull), as these provide no nutritional value and can be harder to digest.
3.  **Cut into Bite-Sized Pieces:** Slice the berry into small, manageable chunks to prevent choking and make them easier to chew.
4.  **Practice Moderation:** Strawberries should be treated as a **treat, not a meal**. Follow the "10% Rule": treats should make up no more than 10% of your dog's daily caloric intake.
5.  **Avoid Added Sugars:** Never feed your dog strawberries from a syrup, jam, or dessert, as these contain xylitol or excessive sugar which can be toxic or harmful.

**Pro Tip:** Try freezing strawberry chunks for a refreshing, low-calorie summer snack that keeps your fluffy Old English Sheepdog cool!
