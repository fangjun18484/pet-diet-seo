# Can Old English Sheepdogs Eat Macadamia Nuts?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

**Old English Sheepdogs cannot eat macadamia nuts.** These nuts are highly toxic to dogs, regardless of the breed, size, or age. You should never feed them to your dog as a treat or allow them access to them in your home.

---

### Why Macadamia Nuts are Dangerous
While the exact toxin in macadamia nuts is not fully understood, they contain compounds that trigger a reaction in a dog's nervous system. Because Old English Sheepdogs are large dogs, they may require a larger quantity to show severe symptoms compared to a small breed, but the toxicity level is the same—**they are unsafe for all canines.**

---

### Potential Risks & Symptoms
Feeding macadamia nuts to your Old English Sheepdog can lead to **macadamia nut toxicosis**. Symptoms typically appear within 12 hours of ingestion and include:

*   **Hind Leg Weakness:** Difficulty walking or "wobbly" back legs.
*   **Lethargy:** Unusual tiredness or lack of energy.
*   **Vomiting:** Sudden gastrointestinal distress.
*   **Hyperthermia:** An increase in body temperature (fever).
*   **Tremors:** In severe cases, muscle tremors or disorientation.

---

### What to Do If Your Dog Ate Macadamia Nuts
If you suspect your Old English Sheepdog has ingested macadamia nuts, **act quickly**. Follow these steps:

1.  **Remove Access:** Immediately take the nuts away from your dog.
2.  **Call Your Vet:** Contact your veterinarian or an emergency animal clinic right away.
3.  **Contact Poison Control:** You can call the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center at **(888) 426-4435**.
4.  **Monitor Behavior:** Note exactly how many nuts were eaten and when it happened to help your vet determine the treatment plan.
5.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting:** Unless specifically instructed to do so by a professional, do not try to make your dog vomit at home.

### Safe Alternatives for Your Sheepdog
If you want to give your Old English Sheepdog a crunchy, healthy snack, try these dog-safe options instead:
*   **Carrot sticks** (Great for chewing)
*   **Apple slices** (No seeds or core)
*   **Plain blueberries**
*   **Cucumber slices**
