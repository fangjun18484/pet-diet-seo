# Can Old English Sheepdogs Eat Grapes? 🍇

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

---

### Why Grapes Are Dangerous for Your Old English Sheepdog
Regardless of the breed, **grapes and raisins are highly toxic to dogs.** While some dogs may appear fine after eating one, others can experience severe reactions. 

The exact compound causing the toxicity was recently identified as **tartaric acid**. Old English Sheepdogs, like all canines, cannot process this substance, which can lead to sudden, acute kidney failure.

---

### The Risks: What Happens if They Eat Grapes?
There are no nutritional benefits to feeding grapes to your dog; the risks far outweigh any perceived "vitamin" intake. 

**Potential symptoms of grape poisoning include:**
*   **Vomiting and Diarrhea:** Often occurring within a few hours of ingestion.
*   **Lethargy:** Unusual tiredness or weakness.
*   **Loss of Appetite:** Refusing their favorite treats or meals.
*   **Dehydration:** Increased thirst or, conversely, a decrease in urine output.
*   **Kidney Failure:** The most severe risk, which can be fatal if left untreated.

---

### 🚨 What to Do if Your Dog Ate Grapes
If your Old English Sheepdog has ingested a grape or raisin, **treat this as a medical emergency.** Do not wait for symptoms to appear, as kidney damage can happen quickly.

1.  **Call Your Veterinarian Immediately:** Contact your vet or the nearest emergency animal clinic.
2.  **Provide Details:** Tell the vet approximately how many grapes were eaten and how long ago it happened.
3.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting Unless Instructed:** Only induce vomiting if your vet explicitly tells you to do so over the phone.
4.  **Monitor Output:** Keep a close eye on whether your dog is still urinating.

---

### Safe, Dog-Friendly Alternatives
If you want to give your Old English Sheepdog a fruity treat, skip the grapes and try these vet-approved options instead:
*   **Blueberries:** Packed with antioxidants and safe in moderation.
*   **Apple Slices:** (Remove the seeds and core first).
*   **Watermelon:** (Remove the seeds and rind).
*   **Banana:** Great for a quick, healthy snack.
