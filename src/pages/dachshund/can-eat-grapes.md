# Can Dachshunds Eat Grapes? 🍇

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

**Dachshunds cannot eat grapes.** Whether they are red, green, seedless, or organic, grapes and raisins are highly toxic to dogs.

---

### Why are Grapes Dangerous for Dachshunds?
Grapes and raisins contain a specific compound (recently identified as tartaric acid) that can cause **acute kidney failure** in dogs. 

While some dogs may tolerate a single grape, others are hypersensitive. Because Dachshunds are small-breed dogs, their lower body mass means that even a small amount of toxin can have a devastating effect on their renal system.

### The Risks: What Happens if They Eat One?
There is no "safe" dose of grapes. If your Dachshund ingests grapes, they are at risk for the following:

*   **Acute Renal Failure:** The kidneys stop filtering waste from the blood.
*   **Gastrointestinal Distress:** Severe vomiting and diarrhea.
*   **Lethargy:** Sudden loss of energy and weakness.
*   **Loss of Appetite:** Refusal to eat or drink.
*   **Dehydration:** Due to the inability of the kidneys to regulate fluids.

---

### 🚨 What to Do if Your Dachshund Ate Grapes
If you suspect your dog has eaten a grape or raisin, **treat this as a medical emergency.** 

1.  **Call Your Vet Immediately:** Do not wait for symptoms to appear. Early intervention (such as inducing vomiting) is critical.
2.  **Contact Pet Poison Control:** If your vet is unavailable, call a pet toxicity hotline.
3.  **Monitor Symptoms:** Note exactly how many grapes were eaten and when it happened to help your vet determine the treatment plan.
4.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting** unless specifically instructed to do so by a veterinary professional.

---

### Safe, Dog-Friendly Alternatives
If you want to give your Dachshund a sweet, juicy treat, swap grapes for these vet-approved options:

*   **Blueberries:** Packed with antioxidants and safe in moderation.
*   **Apple Slices:** (Remove the seeds and core first).
