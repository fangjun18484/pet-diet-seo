# Can a Whippet Eat Grapes? 🍇

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

**No, a Whippet cannot eat grapes.** Grapes and raisins are highly toxic to dogs, regardless of their breed, size, or individual tolerance.

---

### Why Grapes Are Dangerous for Whippets
While many fruits are healthy treats, grapes and raisins contain substances that can cause **acute kidney failure** in dogs. 

Recent research suggests that **tartaric acid** found in grapes is the likely culprit. Because Whippets are lean, athletic dogs with efficient metabolisms, they are not "immune" to this toxin; even a single grape or raisin can trigger a life-threatening reaction in some dogs.

---

### Potential Risks & Symptoms
Feeding a grape to your Whippet can lead to rapid organ failure. Watch for these warning signs:

*   **Vomiting and Diarrhea:** Often the first sign of distress.
*   **Lethargy:** Unusual tiredness or weakness.
*   **Loss of Appetite:** Refusing food or water.
*   **Dehydration:** Dry gums or skin.
*   **Decreased Urination:** A critical sign that the kidneys are shutting down.

---

### What to Do If Your Whippet Ate a Grape
If your dog has ingested a grape or raisin, **treat it as a medical emergency.** Do not wait for symptoms to appear, as kidney damage can happen quickly.

1.  **Call Your Vet Immediately:** Contact your veterinarian or the nearest emergency animal clinic.
2.  **Provide Details:** Tell the vet how many grapes were eaten and approximately how long ago.
3.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting** unless specifically instructed to do so by a professional.
4.  **Monitor:** Keep your dog calm and quiet until you reach the clinic.

---

### Safe, Healthy Alternatives for Your Whippet
If you want to give your Whippet a fruity snack, try these vet-approved, kidney-safe options instead:

*   **Blueberries:** Packed with antioxidants.
*   **Apple Slices:** (Remove all seeds and the core).
*   **Watermelon:** (Remove the seeds and rind).
*   **Banana:** Great in moderation for a quick energy boost.
