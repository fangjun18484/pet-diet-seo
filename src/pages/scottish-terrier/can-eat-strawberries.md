# Can a Scottish Terrier Eat Strawberries?

**Verdict: ✅ YES**

Scottish Terriers can safely enjoy strawberries as an occasional treat. They are non-toxic and provide a burst of flavor and nutrition that most "Scotties" love.

---

## Why Strawberries Are Safe for Scottish Terriers
Strawberries are naturally low in calories and free from the toxic compounds found in other fruits (like grapes or raisins). While Scottish Terriers are known for their hardy nature, their digestive systems can typically handle the natural sugars and fiber found in fresh strawberries without issue.

## Potential Benefits & Risks

### 🌟 The Benefits
*   **Antioxidants:** Rich in anthocyanins, which help combat oxidative stress.
*   **Vitamin C:** Supports a healthy immune system and skin health.
*   **Fiber:** Aids in digestion and promotes gut health.
*   **Low Calorie:** A great alternative to processed, high-calorie dog treats.

### ⚠️ The Risks
*   **Natural Sugars:** While safe, too many strawberries can lead to weight gain or spike blood sugar levels in dogs with diabetes.
*   **Digestive Upset:** Introducing too many new fruits too quickly may cause mild diarrhea or an upset stomach.
*   **Allergies:** Though rare, some dogs may have an allergic reaction to strawberries.

---

## How to Safely Feed Strawberries to Your Scottie

To ensure your Scottish Terrier enjoys their treat safely, follow these preparation guidelines:

1.  **Wash Thoroughly:** Always rinse berries to remove pesticides and dirt.
2.  **Remove the Greens:** Strip away the green stems and leaves, as these can be a choking hazard or cause stomach irritation.
3.  **Slice Small:** Scottish Terriers are small-to-medium dogs. Slice the strawberries into bite-sized pieces to prevent choking.
4.  **Moderation is Key:** Strawberries should be treats, not meals. Limit them to **2–3 slices a day**.
5.  **Avoid Added Sugars:** Never feed your dog strawberries from a syrup, jam, or dessert recipe, as these often contain Xylitol (toxic) or excessive sugar.

**Pro Tip:** Try freezing strawberry slices for a refreshing, cooling treat during the summer months!
