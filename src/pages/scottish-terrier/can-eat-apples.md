# Can a Scottish Terrier Eat Apples?

**Verdict: ✅ YES**

Scottish Terriers can safely enjoy apples as an occasional treat. Apples are non-toxic and provide a crunchy, refreshing snack that fits well into a balanced diet for your "Scottie."

---

### Why Apples Are Safe for Scottish Terriers
Apples are packed with essential nutrients and are generally well-tolerated by dogs. Since Scottish Terriers are active, spirited dogs, the natural sugars in apples provide a quick energy boost, while the fiber supports their digestive health.

### Benefits vs. Risks

| **Potential Benefits** ✅ | **Potential Risks** ⚠️ |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Vitamin A & C:** Supports immune function and skin health. | **Cyanide Toxicity:** Apple seeds contain amygdalin, which releases cyanide when chewed. |
| **Fiber:** Aids in digestion and promotes regular bowel movements. | **Choking Hazard:** The hard core can cause obstructions or choking. |
| **Low Calorie:** A great alternative to processed, high-calorie dog treats. | **Sugar Content:** Too many apples can lead to weight gain or blood sugar spikes. |

---

### How to Safely Serve Apples to Your Scottie

To ensure your Scottish Terrier enjoys their treat without any danger, follow these preparation steps:

1.  **Wash Thoroughly:** Rinse the apple to remove any pesticides or wax residues.
2.  **Remove the Core and Seeds:** This is the most critical step. **Never** feed your dog the center of the apple.
3.  **Slice into Bite-Sized Pieces:** Cut the flesh into small, manageable cubes to prevent choking.
4.  **Avoid Added Sugars:** Never feed your dog apple pie filling, sweetened applesauce, or candied apples, as these contain dangerous levels of sugar and potentially Xylitol (an artificial sweetener toxic to dogs).
5.  **Moderation is Key:** Apples should be treated as a snack, not a meal. Limit them to a few slices a day to avoid upsetting your dog's stomach.

**Pro Tip:** If your Scottie has a sensitive stomach or is diabetic, consult your veterinarian before introducing new fruits into their diet.
