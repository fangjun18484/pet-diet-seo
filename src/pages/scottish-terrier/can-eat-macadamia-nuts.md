# Can a Scottish Terrier Eat Macadamia Nuts?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

### Why Macadamia Nuts Are Dangerous for Scottish Terriers
Regardless of the breed, macadamia nuts are **toxic to all dogs**, including Scottish Terriers. These nuts contain a specific toxin (though the exact chemical agent is still being studied) that affects a dog's nervous system. 

Because Scottish Terriers are small to medium-sized dogs, even a small amount of macadamia nuts can lead to a concentrated dose of toxins, making them particularly vulnerable to adverse reactions.

---

### Potential Risks & Symptoms
There are **no nutritional benefits** to feeding macadamia nuts to your dog; the risks far outweigh any perceived gain. If your Scottie ingests macadamia nuts, you may notice the following symptoms:

*   ** Backend Weakness:** Difficulty walking or "wobbly" hind legs.
*   **Vomiting & Diarrhea:** Gastrointestinal distress is common.
*   **Lethargy:** Extreme tiredness or depression.
*   **Hyperthermia:** An increase in body temperature.
*   **Tremors:** In severe cases, muscle tremors or seizures may occur.

*Symptoms typically appear within 12 hours of ingestion.*

---

### What to Do If Your Dog Ate Macadamia Nuts
If you suspect your Scottish Terrier has consumed macadamia nuts (or a product containing them, like cookies or granola), take the following steps immediately:

1.  **Remove the Source:** Ensure they cannot eat any more of the nuts.
2.  **Call Your Vet:** Contact your veterinarian or an emergency animal clinic immediately.
3.  **Provide Details:** Be ready to tell the vet **how many** nuts were eaten and **how long ago** the ingestion occurred.
4.  **Monitor Closely:** Watch for the symptoms listed above while you are in transit to the clinic.

**Pro Tip:** To keep your Scottie safe, store all nuts in airtight, high-shelf containers where your curious terrier cannot reach them!
