# Can a Scottish Terrier Eat Chocolate?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

Scottish Terriers, like all dog breeds, **cannot** eat chocolate. Chocolate is toxic to dogs and can lead to serious health complications or even death.

---

### Why is Chocolate Dangerous for Scottish Terriers?
The primary culprit is a compound called **theobromine**, a methylxanthine found in cocoa plants. While humans metabolize theobromine quickly, dogs process it very slowly. 

Additionally, chocolate contains **caffeine**, which further stimulates the central nervous system and cardiovascular system, putting immense strain on your dog's heart and brain.

### Potential Risks & Symptoms
Depending on the type of chocolate (dark and baking chocolate are the most dangerous) and the size of your Scottish Terrier, the risks include:

*   **Mild to Moderate Toxicity:** 
    *   Extreme hyperactivity or restlessness
    *   Vomiting and diarrhea
    *   Increased thirst and urination
*   **Severe Toxicity:**
    *   Rapid or irregular heart rate (Tachycardia)
    *   Muscle tremors or seizures
    *   Coma or heart failure

**Note:** Because Scottish Terriers are small-to-medium dogs, even a small amount of high-concentration chocolate can be dangerous.

---

### What to Do If Your Scottish Terrier Ate Chocolate
If your dog has ingested chocolate, **act quickly**. Do not wait for symptoms to appear.

1.  **Identify the Chocolate:** Determine the type (Dark, Milk, White) and the approximate amount consumed.
2.  **Call Your Vet Immediately:** Contact your veterinarian or a Pet Poison Control hotline.
3.  **Provide Details:** Be ready to tell the vet your dog's weight and the exact time of ingestion.
4.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting** unless explicitly instructed to do so by a medical professional.

### Safe, Dog-Friendly Alternatives
If you want to treat your "Scottie" to something special, swap the chocolate for these vet-approved snacks:
*   **Fresh Blueberries:** Rich in antioxidants.
*   **Apple Slices:** (Remove seeds and core first).
*   **Plain Pumpkin:** Great for digestion.
*   **Carrot Sticks:** A crunchy, low-calorie option.
