# Can a Labrador Retriever Eat Raisins?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

**Absolutely not. You should never feed raisins (or grapes) to a Labrador Retriever or any other dog breed.**

---

### Why Raisins Are Dangerous for Dogs
While the exact toxic component in raisins and grapes is still being studied (recent research points toward **tartaric acid**), the result is consistently dangerous. Raisins are highly toxic to dogs and can lead to **acute kidney failure**. 

Because Labradors are notorious "scavengers" known for eating almost anything they find on the floor or counter, owners must be extra vigilant about keeping raisin-containing snacks out of reach.

---

### The Risks: What Happens to Your Lab?
There are no nutritional benefits to raisins for dogs; there are only severe risks. Even a small amount can be toxic, and sensitivity varies by dog.

**Potential symptoms of raisin poisoning include:**
*   **Vomiting and Diarrhea:** Usually occurs within a few hours of ingestion.
*   **Lethargy:** Unusual tiredness or weakness.
*   **Loss of Appetite:** Refusing their favorite treats or meals.
*   **Decreased Urination:** A primary sign that the kidneys are beginning to fail.
*   **Dehydration:** Dry gums and skin.

---

### What to Do If Your Lab Ate Raisins
If your Labrador has ingested raisins, **this is a veterinary emergency.** Do not wait for symptoms to appear, as kidney damage can happen quickly.

#### Immediate Steps:
1.  **Call Your Vet Immediately:** Or contact an emergency animal clinic.
2.  **Contact Pet Poison Helpline:** Have your dog's weight and the approximate amount of raisins consumed ready.
3.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting** unless specifically instructed to do so by a professional.
4.  **Prepare for Treatment:** Your vet may administer activated charcoal to prevent further absorption or provide IV fluids to protect the kidneys.

### Safe, Dog-Friendly Alternatives
If you want to give your Lab a sweet, healthy treat, swap raisins for these vet-approved options:
*   **Blueberries:** Packed with antioxidants and safe in moderation.
*   **Apple Slices:** (Remove the seeds and core first).
*   **Watermelon:** (Remove the seeds and rind).
*   **Carrot Sticks:** Great for chewing and low in calories.
