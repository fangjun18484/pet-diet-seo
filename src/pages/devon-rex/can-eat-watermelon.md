# Can a Devon Rex Eat Watermelon? 🍉

**Verdict: YES** (In moderation)

## Why Watermelon is Safe for Your Devon Rex
Watermelon is non-toxic to cats, including the spirited and curly-coated Devon Rex. Since it consists mostly of water, it is a refreshing, low-calorie snack that is generally safe for healthy cats to enjoy as an occasional treat.

---

## Potential Benefits & Risks

### ✅ The Benefits
*   **Hydration Boost:** Because cats often struggle to drink enough water, watermelon is an excellent way to increase fluid intake.
*   **Vitamin Content:** It contains small amounts of vitamins A and C, which support overall health.
*   **Low Calorie:** It is a leaner alternative to commercial treats, provided your cat doesn't overeat it.

### ⚠️ The Risks
*   **Sugar Content:** Watermelon contains natural sugars. For Devon Rexes with **diabetes** or those prone to obesity, watermelon should be avoided.
*   **Digestive Upset:** Too much fruit can lead to diarrhea or stomach upset due to the fiber and sugar.
*   **Choking Hazards:** The seeds and the tough outer rind are dangerous.

---

## How to Safely Serve Watermelon to Your Cat

If you want to share a slice of summer with your Devon Rex, follow these strict preparation guidelines:

1.  **Remove ALL Seeds:** Ensure there are no black or white seeds. Seeds can cause intestinal blockages or choking.
2.  **Discard the Rind:** Never feed your cat the green skin or the white pith. The rind is difficult to digest and can cause gastrointestinal distress.
3.  **Cut into Tiny Cubes:** Serve small, bite-sized pieces (about the size of a blueberry) to prevent choking.
4.  **Moderation is Key:** Treat watermelon as a "once-in-a-while" snack. It should never replace a balanced diet of high-quality protein.

**Pro Tip:** Try freezing small pieces of seedless watermelon for a cooling treat during hot summer months!
