# Can a Devon Rex Eat Chocolate? 🍫

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

No, a Devon Rex cannot eat chocolate. Like all cats, Devon Rexes are highly sensitive to the compounds found in cocoa, and consuming chocolate can be toxic or even fatal.

---

### Why is Chocolate Toxic to Devon Rexes?
The danger lies in a chemical called **theobromine** (and to a lesser extent, caffeine). 

While humans can easily metabolize these stimulants, cats process them very slowly. This allows the toxins to build up in their system, overstimulating the central nervous system and the heart. Because Devon Rexes are small, even a tiny amount of dark chocolate or baking powder can lead to a dangerous concentration of theobromine in their bloodstream.

---

### Potential Risks & Symptoms
There are **zero benefits** to feeding chocolate to a cat. The risks are severe and can include:

*   **Gastrointestinal Distress:** Vomiting and diarrhea.
*   **Hyperactivity:** Unusual restlessness or agitation.
*   **Cardiovascular Issues:** Rapid heart rate (tachycardia) or irregular heartbeat.
*   **Neurological Problems:** Muscle tremors, disorientation, and seizures.
*   **Organ Failure:** In severe cases, chocolate toxicity can lead to coma or death.

**Note:** The darker the chocolate (e.g., cocoa powder, baking chocolate, or 85% dark chocolate), the higher the concentration of theobromine and the greater the risk.

---

### What to Do If Your Devon Rex Ate Chocolate
If your cat has ingested chocolate, **time is of the essence.** Do not wait for symptoms to appear.

1.  **Identify the Source:** Determine what kind of chocolate was eaten and approximately how much.
2.  **Check the Ingredients:** Check if the chocolate contained other toxins, such as **xylitol** (an artificial sweetener) or raisins/grapes.
3.  **Call Your Vet Immediately:** Contact your veterinarian or an emergency animal clinic right away.
4.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting:** Never attempt to make your cat vomit unless specifically instructed to do so by a licensed professional.
5.  **Monitor Behavior:** Keep a close eye on your cat for any signs of shaking or lethargy while you are en route to the clinic.

**Safe Alternatives:** If you want to treat your Devon Rex, stick to cat-safe treats or a small piece of plain, cooked chicken or fish.
