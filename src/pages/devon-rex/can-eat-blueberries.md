# Can a Devon Rex Eat Blueberries?

**Verdict: ✅ YES**

## The Quick Answer
Yes, a Devon Rex can safely eat blueberries. Blueberries are non-toxic to cats and can be a healthy, occasional treat for your curly-coated companion. However, because cats are **obligate carnivores**, fruit should only be a tiny fraction of their overall diet.

---

## Why Blueberries Are Safe (and Beneficial)
Blueberries are packed with nutrients that can support your cat's health. While cats cannot taste sweetness, they may be attracted to the texture or smell.

### Potential Benefits:
*   **Antioxidants:** Rich in anthocyanins, which help combat oxidative stress and support the immune system.
*   **Vitamins:** Contains small amounts of Vitamin C and K.
*   **Fiber:** Can aid in digestion if given in very small quantities.

### Potential Risks:
*   **Sugar Content:** Blueberries contain natural sugars. Too many can lead to weight gain or spike blood glucose levels, which is a concern for cats with diabetes.
*   **Choking Hazard:** Due to their round shape, a whole blueberry could potentially be a choking hazard for smaller cats.
*   **Digestive Upset:** Some cats have sensitive stomachs and may experience diarrhea or vomiting when introducing new foods.

---

## How to Safely Feed Blueberries to Your Devon Rex

If you want to share a few berries with your Devon Rex, follow these safety guidelines:

1.  **Wash Thoroughly:** Always rinse blueberries under cold water to remove pesticides or residues.
2.  **Remove Stems:** Ensure there are no leaves or stems attached.
3.  **Cut or Mash:** To prevent choking, slice the berry in half or mash it with a fork before serving.
4.  **Moderation is Key:** Limit treats to **1–2 berries** a few times a week. Treats should never exceed 10% of your cat's daily caloric intake.
5.  **Introduce Slowly:** Give one small piece first and monitor your cat for 24 hours to ensure they don't have an adverse reaction.

**⚠️ Pro Tip:** Never feed your cat blueberries that have been processed into jams, syrups, or baked goods, as these often contain added sugars or toxic sweeteners like **Xylitol**.
