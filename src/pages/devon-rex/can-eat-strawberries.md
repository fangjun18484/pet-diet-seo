# Can a Devon Rex Eat Strawberries? 🍓

**Verdict: YES (In Moderation)**

While your Devon Rex is an obligate carnivore and doesn't *need* fruit in its diet, strawberries are non-toxic and safe for them to eat as an occasional treat.

---

### Why Strawberries are Safe for Devon Rexes
Strawberries do not contain the toxic compounds found in other fruits (like grapes or raisins). Because Devon Rexes are known for their curious and playful personalities, they may be tempted to sneak a bite of your snack. As long as the strawberry is plain, it won't harm your cat.

### Potential Benefits & Risks

#### ✅ The Benefits
*   **Antioxidants:** Strawberries contain anthocyanins and Vitamin C, which support overall cellular health.
*   **Hydration:** With a high water content, they provide a tiny boost of hydration.
*   **Fiber:** A small amount of fiber can occasionally help with digestive regularity.

#### ⚠️ The Risks
*   **Sugar Content:** Cats cannot taste sweetness, but their bodies still process the sugar. Too many strawberries can lead to weight gain or spike blood glucose levels (especially dangerous for diabetic cats).
*   **Digestive Upset:** Some cats may experience diarrhea or vomiting if they are sensitive to fruit sugars.
*   **Lack of Nutritional Necessity:** Strawberries provide no essential nutrients that a cat cannot get from a high-quality protein-based diet.

---

### How to Safely Feed Strawberries to Your Cat

If you want to share a strawberry with your Devon Rex, follow these safety guidelines:

1.  **Wash Thoroughly:** Remove all pesticides and dirt by rinsing the berry under cold water.
2.  **Remove Stems and Leaves:** Give your cat only the red flesh; the green top is unnecessary and can be a choking hazard.
3.  **Cut into Tiny Pieces:** Slice the strawberry into small, bite-sized cubes to prevent choking.
4.  **The "Treat Rule":** Keep it rare. One or two small pieces once a week is plenty. Treats should never exceed 10% of your cat's daily caloric intake.
5.  **Avoid Processed Versions:** **Never** feed your cat strawberry jam, preserves, or strawberry-flavored yogurts, as these contain added sugars and artificial sweeteners (like Xylitol) that are toxic to cats.

**Pro Tip:** If this is your Devon Rex's first time trying strawberries, start with a tiny piece and monitor them for 24 hours for any signs of stomach upset.
