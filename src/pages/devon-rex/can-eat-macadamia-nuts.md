# Can a Devon Rex Eat Macadamia Nuts?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

---

### Why You Should Avoid Macadamia Nuts
Regardless of the breed, **Devon Rex cats should never eat macadamia nuts.** Macadamia nuts contain compounds that are toxic to pets. While much of the research focuses on dogs, these nuts are dangerous for cats as well, as they can trigger severe reactions in the nervous system and digestive tract.

Additionally, macadamia nuts are incredibly dense in fats, which can lead to secondary health complications in cats.

---

### The Risks: What Happens if They Eat One?

Feeding macadamia nuts to your Devon Rex poses several serious health risks:

*   **Macadamia Toxicity:** This can cause lethargy, weakness in the hind legs, vomiting, and disorientation.
*   **Pancreatitis:** Due to the extremely high fat content, macadamia nuts can cause inflammation of the pancreas, a painful and potentially life-threatening condition.
*   **Digestive Distress:** Diarrhea and stomach upset are common immediate reactions.
*   **Choking Hazard:** The size and hardness of the nut pose a physical risk to your cat's throat and digestive tract.

---

### What to Do If Your Devon Rex Ate a Macadamia Nut

If your cat has ingested a macadamia nut, **do not wait for symptoms to appear.** Take the following steps immediately:

1.  **Remove the Source:** Ensure no more nuts are accessible to your pet.
2.  **Call Your Vet:** Contact your veterinarian or an emergency animal clinic right away.
3.  **Note the Amount:** Try to determine exactly how many nuts were eaten and when it happened; this helps the vet determine the treatment plan.
4.  **Monitor for Warning Signs:** Watch for trembling, vomiting, or sudden lethargy.

**Pro Tip:** Since Devon Rex cats are known for their curious and mischievous personalities, keep all nuts, seeds, and human snacks stored in airtight, cat-proof containers!
