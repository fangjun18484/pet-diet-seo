# Can Border Collies Eat Blueberries?

**Verdict: ✅ YES**

## Why Blueberries are Safe for Border Collies
Blueberries are non-toxic and nutrient-dense, making them an excellent snack for dogs of all breeds. For a high-energy, intelligent breed like the Border Collie, blueberries provide a healthy alternative to processed treats without adding unnecessary fillers or harmful ingredients.

## The Benefits of Blueberries for Your Dog
Adding a handful of blueberries to your Border Collie's diet offers several health advantages:

*   **Rich in Antioxidants:** Blueberries are packed with anthocyanins, which help combat oxidative stress and support overall cellular health.
*   **Cognitive Support:** Border Collies are "workaholics." The antioxidants in blueberries can help support brain function and cognitive longevity.
*   **Immune Boost:** High levels of Vitamin C and K help strengthen the immune system and support healthy blood clotting.
*   **Low Calorie:** They are a great low-calorie treat for keeping your active dog lean and agile.

## Potential Risks to Consider
While blueberries are safe, keep these few precautions in mind:

*   **Sugar Content:** Like all fruits, blueberries contain natural sugars. Overfeeding can lead to weight gain or upset the stomach of dogs with diabetes.
*   **Choking Hazard:** While rare for medium-sized Border Collies, always supervise your dog while they eat.
*   **Avoid "Human" Blueberry Products:** Never feed your dog blueberry muffins, pies, or dried blueberries sweetened with Xylitol (a toxic artificial sweetener) or added sugars.

## How to Safely Feed Blueberries to Your Border Collie

To ensure your dog enjoys their treat safely, follow these preparation tips:

1.  **Fresh & Raw:** Wash fresh blueberries thoroughly to remove any pesticides before serving.
2.  **Frozen Treats:** Frozen blueberries are a fantastic way to keep your dog cool during summer or provide a refreshing crunch.
3.  **Mix-ins:** Stir a small handful into their regular kibble for a nutritional boost.
4.  **Moderation is Key:** Follow the **10% rule**—treats (including fruit) should make up no more than 10% of your dog's daily caloric intake.

**Serving Suggestion:** For an average Border Collie, a small handful (roughly 5–10 berries) is a perfect portion size.
