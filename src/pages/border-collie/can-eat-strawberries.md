# Can Border Collies Eat Strawberries? 🍓

**Verdict: YES**

Border Collies can safely enjoy strawberries as an occasional treat. They are non-toxic and provide a healthy, low-calorie alternative to processed dog treats.

---

### Why Strawberries Are Safe
Strawberries are packed with nutrients that are beneficial for dogs. While Border Collies are high-energy working dogs with specific nutritional needs, a few berries won't disrupt their diet; instead, they offer a refreshing burst of hydration and vitamins.

### Potential Benefits & Risks

#### ✅ The Benefits
*   **Antioxidants:** Rich in anthocyanins and antioxidants that help combat free radicals and support overall cellular health.
*   **Vitamin C:** Supports a strong immune system, which is great for active Border Collies.
*   **Fiber:** Promotes healthy digestion and bowel movements.
*   **Low Calorie:** A great way to reward your dog without contributing to obesity.

#### ⚠️ The Risks
*   **Natural Sugars:** Strawberries contain fruit sugars (fructose). While safe, too many can lead to weight gain or blood sugar spikes, especially in dogs with diabetes.
*   **Digestive Upset:** Some dogs may experience mild stomach upset or diarrhea if they eat too many berries at once.
*   **Choking Hazard:** Whole strawberries can be a choking risk for smaller dogs or over-excited eaters.

---

### How to Safely Feed Strawberries to Your Border Collie

To ensure your dog enjoys their treat safely, follow these simple preparation steps:

1.  **Wash Thoroughly:** Rinse the berries under cold water to remove any pesticides or dirt.
2.  **Remove the Greens:** Cut off the green leafy stems and hulls, as these provide no nutritional value and can be harder to digest.
3.  **Slice for Safety:** Cut the strawberries into small, bite-sized pieces to prevent choking.
4.  **Practice Moderation:** Treat strawberries as a "treat," not a meal. They should make up **no more than 10%** of your dog's daily caloric intake.
5.  **Introduce Slowly:** If it's your Border Collie's first time eating strawberries, give them one small piece and monitor for any allergic reactions or digestive issues.

**Pro Tip:** For a high-energy Border Collie, try freezing strawberry slices! It provides a cooling sensory experience and keeps them occupied longer.
