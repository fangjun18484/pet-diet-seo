# Can a Border Collie Eat Macadamia Nuts?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

## Why Macadamia Nuts Are Dangerous for Border Collies
Regardless of their intelligence or energy levels, **Border Collies cannot eat macadamia nuts.** These nuts contain a toxic compound (though the exact toxin is not fully identified) that affects a dog's nervous system. 

While some dogs may react more severely than others, macadamia nuts are universally recognized by veterinarians as dangerous and potentially lethal for all canine breeds.

## Potential Risks & Symptoms
There are **no nutritional benefits** to feeding macadamia nuts to your dog; the risks far outweigh any perceived gain. If your Border Collie consumes macadamia nuts, you may notice the following symptoms within 12 hours:

*   **Lethargy:** Unusual tiredness or weakness in the hind legs.
*   **Vomiting:** Frequent nausea or stomach upset.
*   **Hyperthermia:** An increase in body temperature.
*   **Tremors:** Muscle shaking or loss of coordination (ataxia).
*   **Depression:** A noticeable drop in their usual high-energy personality.

## What to Do If Your Border Collie Ate Macadamia Nuts
If you suspect your dog has ingested macadamia nuts, **act quickly.** Because Border Collies are active and curious, they may have eaten a large quantity before you noticed.

1.  **Contact Your Vet Immediately:** Call your veterinarian or an emergency animal clinic.
2.  **Call Pet Poison Helpline:** If your vet is unavailable, contact a certified pet poison control center.
3.  **Identify the Amount:** Try to determine exactly how many nuts were consumed and when.
4.  **Monitor Behavior:** Keep a close eye on their movement and energy levels until you reach professional help.
5.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting** unless specifically instructed to do so by a veterinary professional.

### Safe Alternatives for Your Border Collie
If you want to give your Border Collie a healthy, crunchy treat, try these dog-safe options instead:
*   **Blueberries** (Rich in antioxidants)
*   **Carrot Sticks** (Great for dental health)
*   **Apple Slices** (Seedless and skinless)
*   **Plain Pumpkin** (Great for digestion)
