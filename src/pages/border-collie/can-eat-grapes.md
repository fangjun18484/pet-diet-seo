# Can a Border Collie Eat Grapes? 🍇

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

**Absolutely not. You should never feed grapes or raisins to a Border Collie (or any dog breed).**

---

### Why Grapes Are Dangerous for Border Collies
While many fruits are healthy treats, grapes and raisins are highly toxic to dogs. Even a small amount can trigger **acute kidney failure**. 

Recent research suggests that **tartaric acid** found in grapes is the primary culprit. Because every dog reacts differently, there is no "safe" dose; some dogs may show no symptoms after a few grapes, while others can experience total kidney collapse from a single one.

---

### Potential Risks & Symptoms
Because Border Collies are high-energy and curious, they may try to sneak a grape off the counter. If ingested, keep a close watch for these red flags:

*   **Gastrointestinal Distress:** Vomiting, diarrhea, or loss of appetite.
*   **Lethargy:** Unusual tiredness or weakness.
*   **Dehydration:** Increased thirst or a sudden decrease in urination (a sign of kidney failure).
*   **Neurological Issues:** Disorientation or incoordination.

---

### 🚨 What to Do If Your Border Collie Ate Grapes
If your dog has ingested grapes or raisins, **treat this as a medical emergency.** Time is of the essence to prevent permanent kidney damage.

1.  **Call Your Vet Immediately:** Contact your veterinarian or the nearest emergency animal clinic.
2.  **Provide Details:** Tell the vet roughly how many grapes were eaten and how long ago it happened.
3.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting** unless specifically instructed to do so by a professional.
4.  **Seek Immediate Care:** Your vet may administer activated charcoal or induce vomiting safely to remove the toxins before they reach the bloodstream.

---

### Safe, Healthy Alternatives for Your Border Collie
If you want to give your active Border Collie a fruity reward, swap the grapes for these dog-safe options:

*   **Blueberries:** Packed with antioxidants and similar in size to grapes.
*   **Apple Slices:** (Remove the seeds and core first).
*   **Watermelon:** (Remove the seeds and rind).
*   **Banana:** Great for a quick energy boost.
