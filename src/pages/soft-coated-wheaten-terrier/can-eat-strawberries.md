# Can a Soft Coated Wheaten Terrier Eat Strawberries?

**Verdict: ✅ YES**

## Why Strawberries are Safe for Wheaten Terriers
Strawberries are non-toxic and safe for dogs of all breeds, including the Soft Coated Wheaten Terrier. They are a low-calorie treat that provides a refreshing burst of flavor without the harmful ingredients found in many processed dog treats.

## The Benefits & Potential Risks

### 🍓 The Benefits
Adding a few strawberries to your Wheaten's diet can provide several nutritional boosts:
*   **Antioxidants:** Strawberries are rich in antioxidants, which help combat free radicals and support overall cellular health.
*   **Vitamin C:** This supports a healthy immune system.
*   **Fiber:** Great for digestive health and keeping your dog feeling full.
*   **Low Calorie:** An excellent alternative for dogs prone to weight gain.

### ⚠️ The Risks
While safe, there are a few precautions to keep in mind:
*   **Natural Sugars:** Strawberries contain fructose. While safe in moderation, too many can lead to weight gain or spike blood sugar levels in dogs with diabetes.
*   **Digestive Upset:** Some dogs may experience mild stomach upset or diarrhea if they are introduced to new fruits too quickly.
*   **Allergies:** Though rare, some dogs can be allergic to strawberries. Always monitor your dog after the first bite.

## How to Safely Serve Strawberries to Your Dog

To ensure your Soft Coated Wheaten Terrier enjoys their treat safely, follow these preparation steps:

1.  **Wash Thoroughly:** Rinse the berries under cold water to remove any pesticides or dirt.
2.  **Remove the Greens:** Pluck off the green stems and leaves, as these can be a choking hazard or cause digestive irritation.
3.  **Slice Into Bite-Sized Pieces:** Wheaten Terriers are medium-sized, but slicing strawberries into smaller chunks prevents choking and makes them easier to digest.
4.  **Practice Moderation:** Treat strawberries as a **treat, not a meal**. They should make up no more than 10% of your dog's daily caloric intake.
5.  **Avoid Added Sugars:** Never feed your dog strawberries from a syrup, jam, or dessert, as these often contain artificial sweeteners (like Xylitol) which are toxic to dogs.

**Pro Tip:** Try freezing strawberry slices for a cooling summer treat that keeps your Wheaten occupied and refreshed!
