# Can a Soft Coated Wheaten Terrier Eat Blueberries?

**Verdict: YES** ✅

## Why Blueberries Are Safe for Wheaten Terriers
Blueberries are non-toxic, low-calorie fruits that are safe for dogs of all breeds, including the Soft Coated Wheaten Terrier. They are nutrient-dense "superfoods" that provide a healthy alternative to processed commercial treats.

## Potential Benefits & Risks

### The Benefits 🌟
*   **Antioxidant Powerhouse:** Blueberries are rich in anthocyanins, which help combat free radicals and support overall cellular health.
*   **Immune Support:** Packed with Vitamin C and Vitamin K, they help keep your dog's immune system strong.
*   **Digestive Health:** The natural fiber in blueberries supports healthy digestion and bowel movements.
*   **Low Calorie:** Ideal for maintaining a healthy weight in active terriers.

### The Risks ⚠️
*   **Sugar Content:** While natural, blueberries contain fructose. Overfeeding can lead to weight gain or blood sugar spikes, which is a concern for dogs with diabetes.
*   **Digestive Upset:** Introducing too many berries too quickly may cause mild diarrhea or stomach upset in some sensitive dogs.
*   **Choking Hazard:** While rare, very small puppies may face a choking risk if they gulp large amounts of berries.

## How to Safely Feed Blueberries to Your Dog

To ensure your Soft Coated Wheaten Terrier enjoys this treat safely, follow these guidelines:

1.  **Moderation is Key:** Blueberries should be treated as a snack, not a meal. Follow the **10% rule**: treats should make up no more than 10% of your dog's daily caloric intake.
2.  **Fresh or Frozen:** Feed them raw and fresh or frozen. Both are excellent options.
3.  **Avoid Additives:** **Never** feed your dog blueberries from a pie, jam, or syrup. These contain added sugars and potentially toxic sweeteners like Xylitol.
4.  **Wash Thoroughly:** Rinse fresh blueberries to remove any pesticide residue.
5.  **Start Slow:** Give your dog 1–2 berries first to ensure they don't have an adverse reaction before offering a small handful.
