# Can a Soft Coated Wheaten Terrier Eat Tomatoes?

**Verdict: YES (But only if they are fully ripe)**

While a Soft Coated Wheaten Terrier can enjoy a slice of tomato, there is a very important distinction between the red fruit and the rest of the plant.

## The Quick Explanation
Ripe, red tomatoes are safe for dogs and can be a healthy treat in moderation. However, **green tomatoes, tomato leaves, and stems are toxic.** They contain a substance called **solanine**, which can cause gastrointestinal distress or more severe poisoning if ingested in large quantities.

---

## Potential Benefits & Risks

### The Benefits (Ripe Tomatoes Only)
When fed in small amounts, ripe tomatoes provide:
*   **Lycopene:** A powerful antioxidant that supports heart health.
*   **Vitamin C & K:** Essential for immune function and blood clotting.
*   **Potassium:** Helps maintain healthy nerve and muscle function.
*   **Hydration:** High water content makes them a refreshing snack.

### The Risks (Green Parts & Overfeeding)
*   **Solanine Poisoning:** Found in green tomatoes and the vine. Symptoms include lethargy, vomiting, and diarrhea.
*   **Acidity:** Tomatoes are acidic. Some Wheaten Terriers may have sensitive stomachs, and too many tomatoes can lead to an upset tummy or acid reflux.
*   **Added Ingredients:** Never feed your dog tomatoes from a can or jar (like ketchup or pasta sauce), as these often contain garlic or onion powder, which are toxic to dogs.

---

## How to Safely Feed Tomatoes to Your Wheaten Terrier

If you want to share a snack with your pup, follow these safety guidelines:

1.  **Ensure it is Bright Red:** Only feed tomatoes that are fully ripe. If it looks green or yellowish, keep it away from your dog.
2.  **Remove the Greens:** Strip away all leaves, stems, and vines before giving the fruit to your dog.
3.  **Cut into Small Pieces:** To prevent choking, dice the tomato into bite-sized chunks.
4.  **Moderation is Key:** Treat tomatoes as a "topper" or occasional treat, not a meal replacement. A few slices are plenty.
5.  **Plain Only:** No salt, pepper, oil, or seasonings.

### What to do if your dog eats a green tomato?
If your Soft Coated Wheaten Terrier accidentally munches on a tomato plant or a green tomato, monitor them closely for signs of toxicity. **Contact your veterinarian immediately** if you notice:
*   Extreme lethargy
*   Frequent vomiting
*   Diarrhea
*   Lack of coordination
