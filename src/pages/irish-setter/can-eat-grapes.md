# Can Irish Setters Eat Grapes? 🍇

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

Irish Setters, like all dog breeds, **should never eat grapes or raisins.** Even a small amount can be toxic and potentially fatal.

---

### Why Grapes are Dangerous for Irish Setters
While some fruits are healthy treats, grapes and raisins contain concentrated toxins (specifically tartaric acid) that can cause **acute kidney failure** in dogs. 

Unlike some foods that are toxic only in large quantities, grapes can trigger a severe reaction in some dogs regardless of the dose. Because Irish Setters are high-energy and often curious "scavengers," they are at a higher risk of accidentally consuming these fruits if left on countertops or in trash bins.

---

### Potential Risks & Symptoms
If your Irish Setter ingests grapes, you may notice the following symptoms of toxicity:

*   **Gastrointestinal Distress:** Vomiting, diarrhea, or loss of appetite.
*   **Lethargy:** Unusual tiredness or weakness.
*   **Dehydration:** Increased thirst or, conversely, a decrease in urination (a sign of kidney failure).
*   **Neurological Issues:** In severe cases, tremors or disorientation.

**Note:** There are no nutritional benefits to feeding grapes to your dog; the risks far outweigh any perceived "vitamin" gain.

---

### 🚨 What to Do If Your Dog Ate Grapes
If you suspect your Irish Setter has eaten a grape or raisin, **act immediately.** Do not wait for symptoms to appear, as kidney damage can happen quickly.

1.  **Call Your Veterinarian Immediately:** Contact your local vet or an emergency animal clinic.
2.  **Contact Pet Poison Control:** Call the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center or a similar service.
3.  **Provide Details:** Be ready to tell the vet how many grapes were eaten, the approximate time of ingestion, and your dog's current weight.
4.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting** unless specifically instructed to do so by a medical professional.

### Safe Fruit Alternatives for Irish Setters
Want to give your red-coated companion a healthy snack? Try these dog-safe options instead:
*   **Apple slices** (no seeds or core)
*   **Blueberries**
*   **Watermelon** (seedless and rind-free)
*   **Banana slices**
