# Can a Scottish Fold Eat Apples? 🍎

**Verdict: YES** (In moderation)

### Why Can Scottish Folds Eat Apples?
Apples are non-toxic to cats. While Scottish Folds—like all cats—are **obligate carnivores** (meaning they require meat to survive), they can safely consume small amounts of certain fruits as occasional treats. Apples provide a crunchy texture and a hint of sweetness that many cats enjoy without causing harm.

---

### 🌟 Potential Benefits & ⚠️ Risks

#### The Benefits
*   **Fiber:** A small amount of apple can help support digestive health.
*   **Vitamin C:** While cats synthesize their own Vitamin C, a little extra doesn't hurt.
*   **Hydration:** Apples have a high water content, contributing to overall hydration.

#### The Risks
*   **Cyanide Toxicity:** Apple **seeds and stems** contain amygdalin, which releases cyanide when chewed. These are toxic to cats.
*   **Sugar Content:** Apples contain natural sugars (fructose). Overfeeding can lead to weight gain or blood sugar spikes, which is a concern for cats prone to diabetes.
*   **Digestive Upset:** Some cats may experience diarrhea or an upset stomach if they eat too much fruit.

---

### How to Safely Feed Apples to Your Scottish Fold

If you want to give your Scottish Fold a taste of apple, follow these safety steps:

1.  **Wash Thoroughly:** Remove any pesticides or wax from the skin.
2.  **Core and De-seed:** Completely remove the core, seeds, and stem. **Never** let your cat chew on the center of the apple.
3.  **Peel (Optional):** While the skin is safe, peeling the apple makes it easier for some cats to digest.
4.  **Cut into Tiny Pieces:** Slice the apple into small, bite-sized cubes (about the size of a pea) to prevent choking.
5.  **Moderation is Key:** Limit treats to one or two small pieces. Apples should make up less than 10% of your cat's daily caloric intake.

**Pro Tip:** Avoid feeding your cat processed apple products, such as apple pie or sweetened applesauce, as these contain added sugars and fats that can be harmful.
