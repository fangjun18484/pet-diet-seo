# Can a Somali Cat Eat Tomatoes?

**Verdict: YES (But only if they are fully ripe!)**

While Somali cats are adventurous and curious, their dietary needs are very specific. While a small piece of a **ripe, red tomato** is generally safe, other parts of the tomato plant can be dangerous.

---

### Why the Distinction Matters
Cats are obligate carnivores, meaning their bodies are designed to process meat, not plants. While a ripe tomato isn't toxic, the **green parts** of the plant—including the stems, leaves, and unripened green tomatoes—contain a substance called **solanine**. 

Solanine is a glycoalkaloid that is toxic to cats and can cause serious health issues if ingested.

---

### Potential Benefits & Risks

#### ⚠️ The Risks (The Danger Zone)
*   **Solanine Poisoning:** Ingesting green tomatoes or leaves can lead to vomiting, diarrhea, and lethargy.
*   **Digestive Upset:** Some cats have sensitive stomachs; the acidity in tomatoes may cause gastric irritation.
*   **Added Ingredients:** Many "tomato products" (ketchup, pasta sauce) contain garlic or onion powder, both of which are **highly toxic** to cats.

#### ✅ The Benefits (Minimal)
*   **Antioxidants:** Ripe tomatoes contain lycopene and Vitamin C.
*   **Hydration:** Due to high water content, a small piece can provide a tiny bit of extra hydration.
*   *Note: Your Somali will get all necessary nutrients from a high-quality cat food; tomatoes are purely an optional treat.*

---

### How to Safely Feed Tomatoes to Your Somali

If you want to give your Somali a taste of tomato, follow these strict safety guidelines:

1.  **Ripe Only:** Ensure the tomato is deep red and fully ripe. Never feed green or yellowish tomatoes.
2.  **Flesh Only:** Remove all stems, leaves, and the green core.
3.  **Plain and Pure:** Do not add salt, pepper, butter, or oil. Ensure it is not part of a seasoned dish.
4.  **Tiny Portions:** Offer a piece no larger than a thumbnail. This should be a rare treat, not a meal replacement.
5.  **Monitor:** Watch your cat for any signs of allergic reaction or stomach upset after the first taste.

**What to do if your cat eats a green tomato:**
If your Somali has chewed on a tomato plant or eaten an unripened green tomato, **contact your veterinarian immediately**, as solanine poisoning requires professional medical attention.
