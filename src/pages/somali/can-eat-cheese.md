# Can a Somali Cat Eat Cheese? 🧀

**Verdict: YES (In Moderation)**

While Somali cats can eat cheese, it should be treated as an occasional snack rather than a dietary staple. Most adult cats are actually lactose intolerant, meaning their bodies struggle to digest the sugars found in dairy.

---

### Why the Caution?
Somalis are active, intelligent cats, but like all felines, they are **obligate carnivores**. Their digestive systems are designed to process animal proteins and fats, not dairy products. While a small cube of cheese won't be toxic, too much can lead to gastrointestinal distress.

### Potential Benefits & Risks

#### 🟢 The Benefits
*   **Protein Boost:** Cheese provides a small amount of high-quality protein.
*   **Calcium:** It offers a dose of calcium for bone health.
*   **Palatability:** Most Somalis love the strong scent and taste of cheese, making it a great high-value reward for training.

#### 🔴 The Risks
*   **Lactose Intolerance:** The most common risk. This can cause diarrhea, gas, and stomach pain.
*   **High Caloric Density:** Cheese is calorie-dense and can lead to obesity if fed frequently.
*   **Sodium Content:** Some cheeses are high in salt, which can put pressure on a cat's kidneys over time.
*   **Toxic Additives:** Some flavored cheeses contain garlic or onion powder, which are **toxic** to cats.

---

### How to Safely Feed Cheese to Your Somali

If you want to share a treat with your Somali, follow these expert guidelines:

1.  **Choose Hard Cheeses:** Opt for hard cheeses like **Cheddar, Parmesan, or Swiss**. These have lower lactose levels than soft cheeses (like Mozzarella or Brie).
2.  **Avoid Flavorings:** Ensure the cheese is plain. Never feed cheese containing herbs, garlic, onion, or excessive spices.
3.  **Keep Portions Tiny:** A piece the size of a small die (about 1 cm) is plenty. Cheese should make up less than 10% of your cat's daily calories.
4.  **Monitor the Reaction:** After the first piece, watch for signs of an upset stomach or itching (allergic reaction).

**Pro Tip:** If your Somali shows signs of lactose intolerance (loose stools), skip the cheese entirely and opt for freeze-dried chicken or salmon treats instead!
