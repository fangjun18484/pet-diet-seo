# Can Somali Cats Eat Grapes? The Essential Safety Guide

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

**Somali cats cannot eat grapes.** Whether fresh, dried (raisins), or in juice form, grapes are highly toxic to cats and can lead to severe health complications.

---

## Why Grapes are Dangerous for Somali Cats
While the exact toxin in grapes is not fully understood, veterinary science has linked the consumption of grapes and raisins to **acute kidney failure** in felines. 

Somali cats, despite their active and curious nature, are just as susceptible to this toxicity as any other cat breed. Even a single grape can be enough to trigger a systemic reaction in some cats, making it a high-risk food that should never be introduced to their diet.

---

## Potential Risks and Symptoms
If a Somali cat consumes grapes, the primary risk is **sudden renal (kidney) failure**. Because the kidneys are responsible for filtering waste from the blood, failure can lead to a rapid buildup of toxins in the body.

**Watch for these warning signs:**
*   **Vomiting and Diarrhea:** Often the first signs of distress.
*   **Lethargy:** Unusual tiredness or lack of interest in play.
*   **Loss of Appetite:** Refusing their favorite wet or dry food.
*   **Dehydration:** Dry gums or skin.
*   **Changes in Urination:** Either a significant decrease in urine output or excessive urination.

---

## What to Do If Your Somali Cat Ate a Grape
If your cat has accidentally ingested a grape or raisin, **treat this as a veterinary emergency.** Do not wait for symptoms to appear, as early intervention is critical for kidney preservation.

### Immediate Action Plan:
1.  **Call Your Vet Immediately:** Contact your primary veterinarian or the nearest emergency animal clinic.
2.  **Identify the Amount:** Try to determine exactly how many grapes were eaten and how long ago.
3.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting:** Never attempt to make your cat vomit at home unless explicitly instructed to do so by a professional.
4.  **Seek Treatment:** The vet may administer activated charcoal to prevent further absorption or provide IV fluids to support kidney function.

### Safe Alternatives for Your Somali
If you want to give your Somali cat a healthy treat, opt for cat-safe fruits in very small quantities, such as:
*   **Blueberries** (in moderation)
*   **Cantaloupe** (seedless)
*   **Apple slices** (without seeds or core)
