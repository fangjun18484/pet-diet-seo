# Can a Havanese Eat Grapes? 🍇

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

**Absolutely not. You should never feed grapes or raisins to a Havanese, or any other dog breed.**

---

## Why Grapes are Dangerous for Havanese
While many fruits are healthy treats, grapes and raisins are highly toxic to dogs. Even a small amount can trigger **acute kidney failure**. 

Recent veterinary research suggests that **tartaric acid** found in grapes is the likely culprit. Because Havanese are small dogs, their lower body mass means that even a single grape can potentially cause a toxic reaction.

---

## Potential Risks & Symptoms
Feeding your Havanese grapes doesn't just cause a stomach ache; it can be fatal. The primary risk is sudden renal (kidney) failure.

**Watch for these warning signs of grape toxicity:**
*   **Vomiting and Diarrhea:** Often occurs shortly after ingestion.
*   **Lethargy:** Unusual tiredness or lack of energy.
*   **Loss of Appetite:** Refusing their favorite treats or meals.
*   **Changes in Urination:** Decreased urine output (a sign the kidneys are failing).
*   **Dehydration:** Dry gums and skin.

---

## What to Do If Your Havanese Ate a Grape
If your dog accidentally ingested a grape or raisin, **treat this as a medical emergency.**

1.  **Call Your Vet Immediately:** Do not wait for symptoms to appear. Early intervention is key to preventing permanent kidney damage.
2.  **Contact Animal Poison Control:** If your vet is unavailable, call a pet poison hotline.
3.  **Gather Information:** Try to determine exactly how many grapes were eaten and how long ago it happened.
4.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting** unless specifically instructed to do so by a veterinary professional.

---

## Safe, Healthy Alternatives for Your Havanese
If you want to give your Havanese a fruity snack, choose these dog-safe, kidney-friendly options instead:

*   **Blueberries:** Packed with antioxidants (perfect for small mouths!).
*   **Apple Slices:** Great for crunch (remove seeds and core first).
*   **Banana:** A wonderful source of potassium in moderation.
*   **Watermelon:** Hydrating and sweet (remove seeds and rind).
