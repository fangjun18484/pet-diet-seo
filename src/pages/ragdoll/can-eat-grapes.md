# Can Ragdoll Cats Eat Grapes?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

## Why Ragdolls Cannot Eat Grapes
Regardless of their breed, **grapes and raisins are highly toxic to cats**, including Ragdolls. While the exact toxin hasn't been fully identified by science, ingestion can lead to sudden and severe **acute kidney failure**. 

Because Ragdolls are often docile and curious, they may try to sneak a grape off your kitchen counter, but even a small amount can be dangerous.

---

## The Risks: What Happens if a Ragdoll Eats Grapes?
There are no benefits to feeding grapes to a cat; the risks far outweigh any perceived nutritional value. The primary danger is **renal toxicity**.

**Common symptoms of grape poisoning include:**
*   **Vomiting and Diarrhea:** Often the first sign of distress.
*   **Lethargy:** Unusual tiredness or weakness.
*   **Loss of Appetite:** Refusing their favorite wet food or treats.
*   **Dehydration:** Dry gums and skin.
*   **Decreased Urination:** A critical sign that the kidneys are beginning to fail.

---

## What to Do If Your Ragdoll Ate a Grape
If you suspect your Ragdoll has ingested a grape or raisin, **treat this as a medical emergency.** Do not wait for symptoms to appear, as kidney damage can happen rapidly.

1.  **Call Your Vet Immediately:** Contact your primary veterinarian or the nearest emergency animal clinic.
2.  **Provide Details:** Tell the vet how many grapes were eaten and how long ago the ingestion occurred.
3.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting:** Never attempt to make your cat vomit unless explicitly instructed to do so by a licensed professional.
4.  **Monitor closely:** Keep an eye on their water intake and bathroom habits until you reach the clinic.

---

## Safe, Cat-Friendly Alternatives
If you want to give your Ragdoll a healthy treat, skip the fruit bowl and try these vet-approved options:
*   **Cooked Pumpkin:** Great for digestion (plain, no sugar).
*   **Blueberries:** A safe, antioxidant-rich fruit (in moderation).
*   **Freeze-Dried Chicken:** A high-protein snack they will love.
*   **Cat Grass:** A safe way for them to chew on greens.
