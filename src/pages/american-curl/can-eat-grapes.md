# Can American Curls Eat Grapes? 🍇

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

## Why You Should Never Feed Grapes to Your American Curl
Regardless of the breed, **grapes and raisins are highly toxic to all cats**, including American Curls. While the exact toxin has not been fully identified by science, ingestion can lead to **acute kidney failure**, a life-threatening condition that can progress rapidly.

Because American Curls are known for being curious and active, it is vital to keep these fruits completely out of their reach.

---

## Potential Risks & Side Effects
Feeding your cat even a single grape can be dangerous. There is no "safe" amount. The primary risks include:

*   **Acute Renal Failure:** The most severe risk, where the kidneys suddenly stop functioning.
*   **Gastrointestinal Distress:** Vomiting and diarrhea shortly after ingestion.
*   **Lethargy:** A sudden drop in energy levels and unusual tiredness.
*   **Loss of Appetite:** Refusing food or water.
*   **Dehydration:** Often a secondary effect of kidney dysfunction.

---

## What to Do If Your American Curl Ate a Grape
If you suspect your cat has consumed a grape or raisin, **treat this as a medical emergency.** Do not wait for symptoms to appear, as kidney damage can occur before the cat looks "sick."

### Immediate Action Steps:
1.  **Call Your Vet Immediately:** Contact your primary veterinarian or the nearest emergency animal clinic.
2.  **Provide Details:** Tell the vet how many grapes were eaten, the variety (if known), and exactly how long ago the incident occurred.
3.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting:** Never attempt to make your cat vomit at home unless specifically instructed to do so by a licensed professional.
4.  **Monitor Closely:** Watch for signs of weakness or changes in urination patterns while you are en route to the clinic.

### Safe Alternatives for Your Cat
If you want to give your American Curl a healthy treat, skip the grapes and try these cat-safe options (in moderation):
*   **Blueberries** (in very small amounts)
*   **Cooked Pumpkin** (plain, no sugar)
*   **Small pieces of cooked chicken** (unseasoned)
