# Can an American Curl Eat Tomatoes?

**Verdict: NO (Avoid it)**

While a small piece of a fully ripe red tomato is unlikely to be fatal, it is **not recommended** to feed tomatoes to your American Curl. The risks far outweigh any potential nutritional benefits.

---

### Why You Should Avoid Tomatoes
The primary concern is a compound called **solanine**. 

Solanine is a toxic alkaloid found in the **green parts** of the tomato plant, including the stems, leaves, and unripened green tomatoes. While ripe red tomatoes contain significantly lower levels of solanine, cats are highly sensitive to toxins, and it is very easy to accidentally feed them a piece of skin or stem that contains dangerous levels of the compound.

### Potential Risks & Benefits

#### 🚩 The Risks (High)
If your American Curl consumes green tomatoes or the plant itself, they may experience **solanine poisoning**. Symptoms include:
*   **Gastrointestinal Distress:** Vomiting and diarrhea.
*   **Neurological Issues:** Lethargy, disorientation, or muscle weakness.
*   **Heart Rate Changes:** In severe cases, an irregular heartbeat.

#### 🟢 The Benefits (Negligible)
Some argue that tomatoes provide antioxidants like lycopene. However, cats are **obligate carnivores**; they cannot derive essential nutrients from tomatoes and can get all their necessary vitamins from a high-quality cat food or safer treats (like cooked carrots or pumpkin).

---

### What to Do If Your Cat Ate a Tomato

If your American Curl sneaked a bite of a ripe red tomato, they will likely be fine. However, if they chewed on a **tomato plant** or ate a **green tomato**, take the following steps:

1.  **Remove the Source:** Immediately take the plant or fruit away from your cat.
2.  **Monitor Closely:** Watch for signs of lethargy, vomiting, or stumbling.
3.  **Contact Your Vet:** Call your veterinarian or a pet poison control center immediately. Be ready to tell them:
    *   Which part of the plant was eaten.
    *   Approximately how much was consumed.
    *   How long ago it happened.
4.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting:** Never try to make your cat vomit unless specifically instructed to do so by a licensed professional.

### Safe Alternatives for Your American Curl
Want to give your cat a healthy snack? Try these vet-approved options instead:
*   **Boiled Chicken** (Plain, no seasoning)
*   **Cooked Pumpkin** (Pureed, no sugar)
*   **Blueberries** (In moderation)
*   **Cat grass** (Specifically grown for pets)
