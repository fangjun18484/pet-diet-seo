# Can an American Curl Eat Cheese?

**Verdict: YES (In moderation)**

While American Curls can eat cheese, it is not a necessary part of their diet. Most adult cats are lactose intolerant, meaning they lack the enzyme (lactase) needed to digest the sugars found in dairy.

---

## Why Some Cheese is Okay (And Some Isn't)
American Curls, like most domestic cats, are obligate carnivores. Their bodies are designed to process proteins and fats from meat, not carbohydrates or lactose from dairy. However, many cats can tolerate small amounts of specific cheeses without adverse reactions.

## Potential Benefits & Risks

### ⚠️ The Risks
*   **Lactose Intolerance:** The most common risk. This can lead to gastrointestinal distress, including bloating, gas, and diarrhea.
*   **High Caloric Density:** Cheese is rich in fats, which can lead to obesity if fed frequently.
*   **Sodium Content:** High salt levels in some cheeses can put unnecessary strain on a cat's kidneys.
*   **Toxic Additives:** Some flavored cheeses contain garlic or onion powder, both of which are **toxic** to cats.

### ✅ The Benefits
*   **Palatability:** For cats that tolerate dairy, cheese can be a high-value treat used for bonding or rewarding good behavior.
*   **Protein/Calcium:** In very small quantities, cheese provides a minor boost of protein and calcium.

---

## How to Safely Feed Cheese to Your American Curl

If you want to give your American Curl a taste of cheese, follow these safety guidelines:

1.  **Choose Hard Cheeses:** Opt for aged, hard cheeses like **Cheddar, Parmesan, or Swiss**. These have significantly lower lactose levels than soft cheeses (like Mozzarella or Brie).
2.  **Keep it Plain:** Never feed cheese that contains herbs, spices, or flavorings. Ensure there is no garlic or onion powder in the ingredients.
3.  **The "Thumbnail Rule":** A serving should be no larger than a thumbnail. Cheese should be a rare treat, not a meal replacement.
4.  **Monitor the Reaction:** After the first piece, monitor your cat for 24 hours for any signs of vomiting or loose stools.

### What to do if your cat eats too much cheese?
If your American Curl consumes a large amount of cheese and shows signs of digestive upset:
*   **Provide plenty of fresh water** to help flush out excess sodium.
*   **Withhold treats** for 24 hours to let the stomach settle.
*   **Consult your vet** if you notice persistent vomiting, lethargy, or severe diarrhea.
