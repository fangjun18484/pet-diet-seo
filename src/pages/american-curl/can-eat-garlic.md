# Can American Curls Eat Garlic? The Expert Verdict

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

American Curls, like all breeds of domestic cats, **should never eat garlic.** Garlic is toxic to cats and can lead to serious health complications.

---

## Why Garlic is Dangerous for American Curls
While garlic is a staple in human kitchens, it contains compounds called **thiosulfates**. Cats lack the specific enzyme needed to break down these compounds. 

When a cat consumes garlic, the thiosulfates cause oxidative damage to their red blood cells, leading to a condition known as **hemolytic anemia**. This means the red blood cells burst and are destroyed faster than the body can replace them, reducing the blood's ability to carry oxygen to vital organs.

---

## Potential Risks & Symptoms
Because garlic toxicity is cumulative, symptoms may not appear immediately after one bite; they often develop over several days.

### Critical Risks:
*   **Hemolytic Anemia:** The primary risk, where red blood cells are destroyed.
*   **Organ Stress:** Significant strain on the kidneys and liver as they attempt to process the toxins.

### Warning Signs to Watch For:
*   **Pale Gums:** A sign of decreased red blood cell count.
*   **Lethargy:** Unusual tiredness or weakness.
*   **Rapid Heart Rate:** The heart works harder to move limited oxygen.
*   **Dark-Colored Urine:** A result of hemoglobin being released into the bloodstream.
*   **Loss of Appetite:** General malaise and nausea.

---

## What to Do If Your American Curl Ate Garlic
If your cat has ingested garlic (fresh, dried, or powdered), **treat it as a medical emergency.**

1.  **Remove the Source:** Immediately take away any remaining garlic or garlic-seasoned food.
2.  **Call Your Vet:** Contact your veterinarian or an emergency animal clinic immediately.
3.  **Provide Details:** Tell the vet the **amount** consumed, the **form** (e.g., garlic powder is more concentrated and dangerous), and **when** it happened.
4.  **Monitor:** Keep a close eye on their breathing and gum color while transporting them to the clinic.

**Pro Tip:** Be careful with "human" treats or pre-made deli meats, as garlic and onion powders are hidden ingredients in many processed foods. Always check the label!
