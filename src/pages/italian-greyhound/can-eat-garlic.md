# Can Italian Greyhounds Eat Garlic?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

***

## Why You Should Avoid Feeding Garlic to Your Italian Greyhound
While garlic is a staple in human kitchens, it is toxic to dogs, including Italian Greyhounds. Garlic belongs to the ***Allium*** family (which also includes onions, shallots, and chives). 

These plants contain compounds called **N-propyl disulfide**, which cause oxidative damage to a dog's red blood cells. This can lead to a condition known as **hemolytic anemia**, where the body destroys its own red blood cells faster than it can produce them.

## The Risks: What Happens if They Eat Garlic?
Because Italian Greyhounds are a smaller breed, they may be more susceptible to toxicity from smaller quantities than a larger dog. 

### Potential Symptoms of Garlic Poisoning:
*   **Pale Gums:** A sign of anemia (lack of oxygen in the blood).
*   **Lethargy:** Unusual tiredness or weakness.
*   **Rapid Heart Rate:** The heart works harder to compensate for fewer red blood cells.
*   **Dark-Colored Urine:** A result of hemoglobin being released into the bloodstream.
*   **Loss of Appetite:** General nausea or digestive upset.

*Note: Symptoms may not appear immediately and can take several days to manifest.*

## What to Do If Your Italian Greyhound Ate Garlic
If your dog accidentally ingested garlic powder, raw garlic, or a dish containing garlic, follow these steps immediately:

1.  **Remove the Source:** Ensure they cannot eat any more of the food.
2.  **Assess the Amount:** Try to determine how much garlic was consumed and whether it was concentrated (garlic powder is more potent than fresh garlic).
3.  **Call Your Veterinarian:** Contact your vet or an emergency animal clinic right away. 
4.  **Do NOT Induce Vomiting:** Unless explicitly instructed to do so by a veterinary professional, do not try to make your dog vomit at home.
5.  **Monitor Behavior:** Keep a close eye on their energy levels and gum color until you can get professional medical help.

### Safe Alternatives for Flavor
If you want to add a bit of "zing" to your Italian Greyhound's food, stick to dog-safe herbs like:
*   **Parsley** (great for fresh breath!)
*   **Turmeric** (anti-inflammatory)
*   **Basil**
