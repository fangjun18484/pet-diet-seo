# Can Italian Greyhounds Eat Tomatoes? 🍅

**Verdict: YES (But only if they are ripe and red!)**

While a slice of tomato won't hurt most dogs, there are critical safety rules you must follow to keep your Italian Greyhound healthy.

---

## The Quick Explanation
Ripe, red tomatoes are generally safe for Italian Greyhounds. However, **green tomatoes and the green parts of the plant (stems and leaves)** contain a substance called **solanine**. Solanine is toxic to dogs and can cause gastrointestinal distress or more severe neurological issues.

Because Italian Greyhounds are small and often have sensitive digestive systems, moderation is key.

---

## 🟢 The Benefits: Why Give Tomatoes?
In small quantities, ripe tomatoes can provide a nutritional boost for your sighthound:
*   **Antioxidants:** Lycopene helps fight free radicals in the body.
*   **Vitamins:** Rich in Vitamin C and Potassium.
*   **Hydration:** High water content makes them a refreshing low-calorie snack.

## 🔴 The Risks: What to Watch For
Despite the benefits, there are three primary risks:
1.  **Solanine Toxicity:** As mentioned, green tomatoes or vines can lead to vomiting, diarrhea, and lethargy.
2.  **Acidity:** Tomatoes are acidic. Some Italian Greyhounds may experience an upset stomach or acid reflux if they eat too many.
3.  **Processed Ingredients:** **Never** feed your dog ketchup, tomato sauce, or salsa. These contain salt, garlic, onions, and sugar—many of which are toxic to dogs.

---

## How to Safely Feed Tomatoes to Your Italian Greyhound

If you want to share a snack with your pup, follow these guidelines:

1.  **Check the Color:** Ensure the tomato is fully ripe and deep red.
2.  **Remove the Greens:** Strip away all leaves, stems, and any green-tinted parts of the fruit.
3.  **Plain is Best:** Serve them raw or steamed. Do not add salt, pepper, oil, or butter.
4.  **Small Portions:** Italian Greyhounds are tiny! A single small slice or a few cherry tomato halves are plenty.
5.  **Introduce Slowly:** Give one small piece first to ensure your dog doesn't have a specific allergy or sensitivity to the acidity.

### 🆘 What to do if they ate a green tomato?
If your Italian Greyhound accidentally munches on a tomato plant or a green tomato, contact your veterinarian or an animal poison control center immediately, especially if you notice:
*   Excessive drooling
*   Vomiting or diarrhea
*   Lack of coordination (ataxia)
*   Extreme lethargy
