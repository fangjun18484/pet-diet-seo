# Can Italian Greyhounds Eat Macadamia Nuts?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

**Italian Greyhounds cannot eat macadamia nuts. They are highly toxic to all dog breeds, regardless of size.**

---

## Why are Macadamia Nuts Dangerous?
While the exact toxin in macadamia nuts hasn't been fully identified, they contain compounds that cause a systemic reaction in dogs. Because Italian Greyhounds are a small breed with low body mass, even a small amount of these nuts can lead to a concentrated dose of toxicity, making them particularly vulnerable to severe reactions.

## The Risks: What Happens if They Eat Them?
Macadamia nut poisoning typically manifests within 12 hours of ingestion. Because your Italian Greyhound has a fast metabolism and a delicate system, watch for these critical warning signs:

*   **Hind Leg Weakness:** One of the most common signs is difficulty walking or "wobbly" back legs.
*   **Vomiting and Diarrhea:** Acute gastrointestinal distress.
*   **Lethargy:** Extreme tiredness or depression.
*   **Hyperthermia:** An increase in body temperature (fever).
*   **Tremors:** In severe cases, muscle tremors or disorientation may occur.

**Are there any benefits?**
No. There are zero nutritional benefits to feeding macadamia nuts to dogs that outweigh the life-threatening risks.

---

## What to Do if Your Italian Greyhound Ate Macadamia Nuts

If your dog has ingested macadamia nuts—whether raw, roasted, or inside a cookie—**treat this as a medical emergency.**

### 1. Immediate Action
Do not attempt to induce vomiting at home unless specifically instructed to do so by a veterinary professional, as this can sometimes cause further complications.

### 2. Call the Vet
Contact your primary veterinarian or an emergency animal clinic immediately. Be prepared to tell them:
*   How many nuts were consumed.
*   When they were eaten.
*   The approximate weight of your dog.

### 3. Professional Treatment
Depending on the amount ingested, your vet may:
*   Administer medication to induce vomiting safely.
*   Provide intravenous (IV) fluids to flush the toxins from the system.
*   Provide supportive care to manage tremors or fever.

### Safe Alternatives for Your IG
If you want to give your Italian Greyhound a crunchy, healthy treat, try these dog-safe options instead:
*   **Blueberries** (Antioxidant-rich)
*   **Carrot sticks** (Low calorie and great for teeth)
*   **Apple slices** (Seedless and skinless)
