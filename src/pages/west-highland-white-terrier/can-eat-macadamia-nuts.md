# Can West Highland White Terriers Eat Macadamia Nuts?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

**West Highland White Terriers (Westies) cannot eat macadamia nuts. They are highly toxic to dogs of all breeds and sizes.**

---

## Why Are Macadamia Nuts Dangerous for Westies?
While the exact toxin in macadamia nuts hasn't been fully identified, they contain compounds that trigger a severe reaction in a dog's nervous system. Because Westies are small dogs, even a small amount of these nuts can lead to a significant toxic load relative to their body weight.

## Potential Risks and Symptoms
If your Westie consumes macadamia nuts, they may experience **macadamia nut toxicosis**. Symptoms typically appear within 12 hours and can include:

*   **Loss of Balance:** Staggering or walking as if intoxicated (ataxia).
*   **Lethargy:** Extreme tiredness or weakness.
*   **Vomiting:** Sudden nausea and gastrointestinal upset.
*   **Tremors:** Muscle twitching or shaking.
*   **Hyperthermia:** An increase in body temperature.

**Note:** These symptoms are usually temporary but can be life-threatening if a large quantity is ingested.

## What to Do If Your Westie Ate Macadamia Nuts
If you suspect your dog has eaten macadamia nuts, **treat this as a medical emergency.**

1.  **Contact Your Vet Immediately:** Call your veterinarian or the nearest emergency animal clinic.
2.  **Call Pet Poison Control:** You can also contact the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center.
3.  **Gather Information:** Try to determine exactly how many nuts were eaten and when it happened.
4.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting:** Unless specifically instructed to do so by a professional, do not attempt to make your dog vomit at home.

## Safe, Healthy Alternatives for Your Westie
If you want to give your Westie a crunchy, healthy snack, skip the nuts and try these dog-safe options:
*   **Blueberries:** Packed with antioxidants.
*   **Apple Slices:** (Remove the seeds and core).
*   **Carrot Sticks:** Great for chewing and low in calories.
*   **Plain Pumpkin:** Excellent for digestion.
