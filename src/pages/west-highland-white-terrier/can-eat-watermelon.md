# Can a West Highland White Terrier Eat Watermelon?

**Verdict: ✅ YES**

West Highland White Terriers (Westies) can safely enjoy watermelon as a refreshing treat. It is non-toxic and provides a hydrating snack, especially during hot summer months.

---

### Why Watermelon is Safe for Westies
Watermelon consists of approximately **92% water**, making it an excellent way to keep your dog hydrated. It is naturally low in calories and contains essential nutrients that are safe for canine consumption.

### Potential Benefits & Risks

#### The Benefits 🌟
*   **Hydration:** Excellent for active Westies to maintain fluid levels.
*   **Nutrient-Rich:** Contains Vitamins A, B6, and C.
*   **Low Calorie:** A great alternative to high-calorie processed treats, helping to prevent obesity.

#### The Risks ⚠️
*   **The Rind:** The hard outer skin is difficult to digest and can cause **intestinal blockages**, particularly in smaller breeds like the Westie.
*   **The Seeds:** While not toxic, seeds can cause choking or contribute to gastrointestinal obstructions.
*   **Sugar Content:** Watermelon contains natural sugars. While safe in moderation, it should be avoided if your dog is diabetic or overweight.

---

### How to Safely Serve Watermelon to Your Westie

To ensure your Westie enjoys their treat without any health risks, follow these preparation steps:

1.  **Remove the Rind:** Serve only the red, fleshy part of the fruit. Discard the green skin entirely.
2.  **De-seed the Fruit:** Remove all black and white seeds before giving the fruit to your dog.
3.  **Bite-Sized Pieces:** Cut the melon into small, manageable cubes to prevent choking.
4.  **Moderation is Key:** Treat watermelon as a "treat," not a meal replacement. It should make up no more than **10% of their daily caloric intake**.

**Pro Tip:** Try freezing watermelon cubes for a cooling, long-lasting summer treat that keeps your Westie occupied!
