# Can a West Highland White Terrier Eat Blueberries?

**Verdict: ✅ YES**

West Highland White Terriers (Westies) can safely enjoy blueberries. These tiny berries are non-toxic and can be a healthy addition to your dog's diet when given in moderation.

## Why Blueberries Are Safe for Westies
Blueberries are nutrient-dense "superfoods" that are generally well-tolerated by dogs. They do not contain the toxins found in grapes or raisins, making them a safe, low-calorie treat for small breeds like the Westie.

## Potential Benefits & Risks

### The Benefits 🌟
*   **Rich in Antioxidants:** Blueberries are packed with anthocyanins, which help combat free radicals and support overall cellular health.
*   **Immune Support:** They provide essential vitamins C and K, helping your Westie maintain a strong immune system.
*   **Digestive Health:** The fiber content in blueberries supports healthy digestion and regular bowel movements.
*   **Low Calorie:** Since Westies can be prone to obesity, blueberries offer a satisfying treat without excessive calories.

### The Risks ⚠️
*   **Sugar Content:** While natural, blueberries contain sugar. Dogs with diabetes should avoid them or consult a vet first.
*   **Choking Hazard:** Due to their size, very small Westies may risk choking if they gulp them down too quickly.
*   **Digestive Upset:** Introducing too many berries too quickly can lead to loose stools or an upset stomach.

## How to Safely Feed Blueberries to Your Westie

To ensure your Westie enjoys their treat safely, follow these simple guidelines:

1.  **Moderation is Key:** Treat blueberries as a "treat," not a meal. A few berries a day is plenty for a small dog.
2.  **Fresh or Frozen:** You can serve them fresh or frozen. Frozen blueberries are great for teething or as a cooling treat in the summer.
3.  **Avoid Additives:** **Never** feed your dog blueberries from a pie, muffin, or jam. Avoid any products containing added sugar, artificial sweeteners (like Xylitol), or chocolate.
4.  **Wash Thoroughly:** Always rinse fresh blueberries to remove any pesticide residue.
5.  **Introduce Slowly:** Give your dog one or two berries first to ensure they don't have an individual allergic reaction or digestive sensitivity.
