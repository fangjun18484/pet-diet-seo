# Can a West Highland White Terrier Eat Apples?

**Verdict: ✅ YES**

West Highland White Terriers (Westies) can safely enjoy apples as an occasional treat. Apples are non-toxic and provide a healthy, crunchy alternative to processed dog treats.

---

## Why Apples are Safe for Westies
Apples are packed with essential nutrients that align well with a dog's dietary needs. Since Westies are energetic dogs, a low-calorie snack like apple provides a satisfying crunch without adding unnecessary weight to their compact frames.

### 🍎 Potential Benefits
*   **Vitamin Boost:** Apples are rich in **Vitamin A and Vitamin C**, which support overall immune health.
*   **Digestive Health:** The natural fiber helps regulate digestion and promotes a healthy gut.
*   **Dental Hygiene:** Chewing on a crisp slice of apple can help clean teeth and freshen your Westie's breath.
*   **Low Calorie:** Ideal for keeping your Westie at a healthy weight.

### ⚠️ Potential Risks
While the flesh is safe, there are three critical concerns:
1.  **Apple Seeds:** Contain small amounts of **cyanide**. While a few seeds may not harm a dog, they should always be avoided.
2.  **The Core:** The tough core can be a **choking hazard** or cause an intestinal blockage, especially in smaller breeds like the Westie.
3.  **Sugar Content:** Apples contain natural sugars. Too many can lead to weight gain or spike blood sugar in dogs with diabetes.

---

## How to Safely Serve Apples to Your Westie

To ensure your West Highland White Terrier enjoys their treat safely, follow these preparation steps:

1.  **Wash Thoroughly:** Rinse the apple to remove any pesticides or wax.
2.  **Remove the Core and Seeds:** Carefully cut around the center to ensure **no seeds or stem** are included.
3.  **Slice into Bite-Sized Pieces:** Westies have smaller mouths; cut the apple into small, manageable cubes to prevent choking.
4.  **Moderation is Key:** Treat apples as a "treat," not a meal. They should make up no more than **10% of your dog's daily caloric intake**.

**Pro Tip:** Try freezing apple slices for a refreshing, cooling treat during the summer months!
