# Can West Highland White Terriers Eat Strawberries?

**Verdict: YES ✅**

West Highland White Terriers (Westies) can safely enjoy strawberries as an occasional treat. Strawberries are non-toxic to dogs and can provide a healthy, low-calorie snack when served in moderation.

---

## Why Strawberries Are Safe for Westies
Strawberries are naturally free from the toxins found in other fruits (like grapes or raisins) that can cause kidney failure in dogs. For a spirited breed like the Westie, strawberries offer a burst of flavor and nutrition without adding excessive weight, provided they are given as a treat and not a meal replacement.

## Potential Benefits & Risks

### 🌟 The Benefits
*   **Antioxidants:** Packed with anthocyanins and Vitamin C, which help support the immune system and fight oxidative stress.
*   **Fiber:** Great for digestive health and keeping your Westie's gut moving.
*   **Low Calorie:** A great alternative to processed, store-bought treats that often contain fillers and artificial dyes.

### ⚠️ The Risks
*   **Natural Sugars:** While healthy, strawberries contain fructose. Too many can lead to weight gain or upset the blood sugar levels of dogs with diabetes.
*   **Digestive Upset:** Some dogs may experience mild diarrhea or stomach upset if they are introduced to new fruits too quickly.
*   **Choking Hazard:** Large, whole berries can be a choking hazard for smaller dogs.

---

## How to Safely Serve Strawberries to Your Westie

To ensure your Westie enjoys their treat safely, follow these simple preparation steps:

1.  **Wash Thoroughly:** Rinse the berries under cold water to remove pesticides and dirt.
2.  **Remove the Greens:** Cut off the stem and leaves (the hull), as these provide no nutritional value and can be harder to digest.
3.  **Slice into Pieces:** Cut the strawberry into small, bite-sized chunks to prevent choking.
4.  **Practice Moderation:** Follow the **"10% Rule"**—treats should never make up more than 10% of your dog's daily caloric intake. For a Westie, 2–3 small pieces are usually sufficient.

### 💡 Pro Tip for Westie Owners
Westies are known for having sensitive skin and being prone to allergies. When introducing strawberries for the first time, **give only one small piece** and monitor your dog for 24 hours for any signs of an allergic reaction, such as itching, hives, or swelling.
