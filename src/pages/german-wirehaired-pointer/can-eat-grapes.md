# Can a German Wirehaired Pointer Eat Grapes?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

## Why Grapes are Dangerous for Your Dog
Regardless of the breed, **grapes and raisins are highly toxic to dogs**, including the German Wirehaired Pointer. While the exact toxin has been debated, recent research points to **tartaric acid** as the primary cause of toxicity. 

Unlike some foods that are only toxic in large quantities, grapes can cause severe reactions in some dogs even in small amounts.

## The Risks: What Happens if They Eat Grapes?
Grapes do not provide any nutritional benefit to your dog; instead, they pose a critical health risk. The primary danger is **acute kidney failure**.

**Common symptoms of grape poisoning include:**
*   **Vomiting and Diarrhea:** Usually occurring within a few hours of ingestion.
*   **Lethargy:** A sudden drop in energy levels.
*   **Loss of Appetite:** Refusing favorite treats or meals.
*   **Dehydration:** Increased thirst or, conversely, a complete stop in urination (a sign of kidney failure).

## What to Do If Your German Wirehaired Pointer Ate Grapes
Since German Wirehaired Pointers are high-energy sporting dogs, they are often curious and prone to scavenging. If your dog has ingested a grape or raisin, **treat this as a medical emergency.**

### Immediate Action Steps:
1.  **Call Your Vet Immediately:** Do not wait for symptoms to appear. Early intervention is key to preventing kidney damage.
2.  **Contact Pet Poison Control:** If your vet is unavailable, call a dedicated pet toxicity hotline.
3.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting Unless Instructed:** Only induce vomiting if your veterinarian explicitly tells you to do so over the phone.
4.  **Provide Information:** Be ready to tell the vet how many grapes were eaten, the approximate time of ingestion, and your dog's current weight.

## Safe Fruit Alternatives for Your GWP
If you want to give your active German Wirehaired Pointer a healthy, fruity treat, try these safe options instead:
*   **Blueberries:** Packed with antioxidants.
*   **Apple Slices:** (Remove the seeds and core).
*   **Banana:** Great for a quick energy boost.
*   **Watermelon:** (Remove the seeds and rind).
