# Can a Colorpoint Shorthair Eat Grapes?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

## Why You Should Never Feed Grapes to Your Cat
Regardless of the breed, **Colorpoint Shorthairs cannot eat grapes.** Grapes and raisins are highly toxic to cats. While the exact toxin is still being studied, they are known to cause sudden, acute kidney failure in feline companions. 

Because Colorpoint Shorthairs are genetically similar to other domestic shorthairs in their metabolic processes, they have no special resistance to these toxins.

## The Risks: What Happens if They Eat Grapes?
There are **zero nutritional benefits** to feeding your cat grapes; the risks far outweigh any perceived "treat" value. Even a single grape or raisin can be dangerous depending on the cat's size and sensitivity.

**Potential symptoms of grape poisoning include:**
*   **Vomiting and Diarrhea:** Often the first sign of distress.
*   **Lethargy:** Uncharacteristic tiredness or weakness.
*   **Loss of Appetite:** Refusing their favorite wet or dry food.
*   **Dehydration:** Increased thirst or, conversely, a complete stop in urine production (a sign of kidney failure).

## What to Do if Your Cat Ate a Grape
If your Colorpoint Shorthair has accidentally ingested a grape or raisin, **treat this as a medical emergency.**

1.  **Call Your Vet Immediately:** Do not wait for symptoms to appear. Early intervention is key to preventing permanent kidney damage.
2.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting:** Never attempt to make your cat vomit at home unless explicitly instructed to do so by a licensed veterinarian.
3.  **Identify the Amount:** Try to determine exactly how many grapes were eaten and how long ago it happened to help your vet determine the treatment plan.
4.  **Monitor Urine Output:** Keep a close eye on the litter box. A decrease in urination is a critical warning sign of renal failure.

### Safe Alternatives for Your Colorpoint Shorthair
If you want to give your cat a healthy snack, skip the fruit and try these vet-approved treats:
*   **Small pieces of cooked chicken** (plain, no seasoning).
*   **Commercial cat treats** formulated for kidney health.
*   **A pinch of catnip** for mental stimulation.
