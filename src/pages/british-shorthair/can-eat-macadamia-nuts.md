# Can a British Shorthair Eat Macadamia Nuts?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

---

## Why Macadamia Nuts are Dangerous for Cats
While your British Shorthair may be curious and food-motivated, **macadamia nuts are toxic to cats.** 

Unlike some nuts that are simply hard to digest, macadamia nuts contain compounds that can trigger a systemic reaction in a cat's body. Because British Shorthairs are prone to obesity and metabolic issues, adding a toxic substance to their diet can put additional strain on their liver and kidneys.

---

## Potential Risks & Symptoms
If a British Shorthair consumes macadamia nuts, they may experience **macadamia nut toxicosis**. While the severity varies depending on the amount eaten, keep a close eye out for these warning signs:

*   **Lethargy:** Unusual tiredness or weakness in the hind legs.
*   **Vomiting:** Sudden nausea or repeated vomiting.
*   **Hyperthermia:** An increase in body temperature.
*   **Muscle Tremors:** In severe cases, shaking or loss of coordination (ataxia).
*   **Digestive Distress:** Diarrhea or loss of appetite.

---

## What to Do If Your Cat Ate a Macadamia Nut
If you suspect your cat has ingested macadamia nuts, **do not wait for symptoms to appear.** Take the following steps immediately:

1.  **Contact Your Vet:** Call your veterinarian or an emergency pet poison clinic right away.
2.  **Identify the Quantity:** Try to determine exactly how many nuts were eaten and how long ago.
3.  **Prevent Further Access:** Remove any remaining nuts or nut-based snacks (like cookies or granola) from the area.
4.  **Monitor Closely:** Watch for the symptoms listed above while you are en route to the clinic.

### Safe Alternatives for Your British Shorthair
If you want to give your cat a special treat, skip the nuts and try these cat-safe options:
*   **Freeze-dried chicken or salmon**
*   **Small pieces of steamed pumpkin**
*   **Commercial cat treats formulated for British Shorthairs**
