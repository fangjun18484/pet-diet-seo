# Can a Bengal Cat Eat Peanut Butter?

**Verdict: YES (In moderation and with caution)**

While peanut butter is not toxic to cats, it is not a natural part of a Bengal's diet. Because Bengals are highly active and curious, they may try to sneak a taste, but you should only offer it as an occasional, tiny treat.

---

## Why is Peanut Butter Safe (But Not Essential)?
Peanuts are not poisonous to felines. However, cats are **obligate carnivores**, meaning their bodies are designed to process animal proteins and fats, not plant-based proteins and sugars found in nut butters. While a small lick won't hurt most Bengals, it provides very little nutritional value compared to meat.

---

## Potential Benefits & Risks

### The Benefits 🐾
*   **High Palatability:** Because of its strong scent and taste, a tiny dab of peanut butter can be useful for hiding a pill if your Bengal is stubborn.
*   **Healthy Fats:** In very small quantities, the fats in peanuts are harmless and provide a quick energy boost.

### The Risks ⚠️
*   **Xylitol Toxicity:** This is the biggest danger. Some "low-sugar" or "diet" peanut butters contain **Xylitol**, an artificial sweetener that is **highly toxic** to cats and dogs.
*   **Obesity:** Peanut butter is calorie-dense. Overfeeding can lead to rapid weight gain, which puts pressure on a Bengal's joints.
*   **Pancreatitis:** The high fat content can trigger inflammation of the pancreas in sensitive cats.
*   **Allergies:** While rare, some cats can have allergic reactions to peanuts.

---

## How to Safely Give Peanut Butter to Your Bengal

If you want to give your Bengal a taste, follow these strict safety guidelines:

1.  **Read the Label First:** Ensure the only ingredients are **peanuts and salt**. Avoid any brand containing Xylitol, sugar, honey, or palm oil.
2.  **Keep Portions Tiny:** A portion the size of a **pea** is plenty. Do not feed it as a meal replacement.
3.  **Frequency:** Limit treats to once or twice a month.
4.  **Monitor Reactions:** The first time you give it, watch for any signs of itching, vomiting, or diarrhea.

### What to do if your cat ate "Diet" Peanut Butter?
If you suspect your Bengal has ingested peanut butter containing **Xylitol**, contact your veterinarian or an emergency pet clinic immediately. Xylitol can cause a dangerous drop in blood sugar and potential liver failure.
