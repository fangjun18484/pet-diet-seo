# Can a Bengal Eat Avocado? The Quick Answer for Cat Owners

**Verdict: NO** ❌

While a small lick might not be fatal, you should **not** feed avocado to your Bengal cat. 

---

## Why Avocado is Risky for Bengals
Unlike humans, cats are obligate carnivores and cannot process the components of an avocado efficiently. The primary concern is a toxin called **persin**.

While persin is more dangerous to birds and horses, it can still cause gastrointestinal distress in cats. Additionally, the extremely high fat content in avocados can lead to more serious health complications for a Bengal's sensitive system.

---

## Potential Risks vs. Benefits

### ⚠️ The Risks
*   **Pancreatitis:** The high fat levels in avocados can trigger inflammation of the pancreas, a painful and potentially life-threatening condition.
*   **Digestive Upset:** Many cats experience vomiting and diarrhea after consuming avocado.
*   **Choking Hazard:** The large avocado pit is a significant choking hazard and can cause intestinal blockages if swallowed.
*   **Added Ingredients:** Store-bought guacamole often contains **onions and garlic**, both of which are highly toxic to cats and can cause anemia.

### ✅ The Benefits
**None.** There are no nutritional benefits to feeding avocado to a Bengal cat. All the nutrients found in avocados (like healthy fats and vitamins) can be found in cat-safe alternatives and high-quality feline diets.

---

## What to Do If Your Bengal Ate Avocado

If your cat sneaked a piece of avocado or a lick of guacamole, don't panic, but stay vigilant.

### 1. Assess the Amount
If it was a tiny piece of plain flesh, your cat will likely be fine. If they ate a large amount or consumed guacamole with onion/garlic, contact your vet immediately.

### 2. Monitor for Symptoms
Keep a close eye on your Bengal for the next 24 hours. Watch for:
*   Lethargy
*   Vomiting or diarrhea
*   Loss of appetite
*   Abdominal pain (hiding or aggression when touched)

### 3. When to Call the Vet
Call your veterinarian or an emergency pet clinic immediately if you notice **persistent vomiting** or if your cat appears **disoriented**.

---

### Safe Alternatives for Your Bengal
If you want to give your Bengal a healthy treat, try these cat-approved options instead:
*   **Cooked Salmon or Tuna** (in small amounts)
*   **Plain Boiled Chicken**
*   **Cat Grass** (for fiber and enrichment)
*   **Freeze-Dried Minnows**
