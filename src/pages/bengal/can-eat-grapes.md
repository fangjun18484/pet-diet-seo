# Can a Bengal Cat Eat Grapes? The Essential Safety Guide

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

Bengal cats, like all feline breeds, **should never eat grapes or raisins.** 

---

### Why are Grapes Dangerous for Bengals?
While the exact toxin in grapes has been debated, veterinary research shows that grapes and raisins can cause **acute kidney failure** in cats. Because Bengals are high-energy, active cats, maintaining optimal kidney function is crucial for their overall health and longevity. Even a small amount can be toxic, and sensitivity varies from cat to cat.

### Potential Risks & Symptoms
Feeding your Bengal a grape is not a "treat"—it is a medical risk. If ingested, you may notice the following symptoms of grape toxicity:

*   **Vomiting and Diarrhea:** Often the first sign of distress.
*   **Lethargy:** A sudden drop in your Bengal's usual high energy levels.
*   **Loss of Appetite:** Refusing their favorite wet or dry food.
*   **Dehydration:** Increased thirst or, conversely, a decrease in urine output (a sign of kidney failure).

---

### What to Do If Your Bengal Ate a Grape
If your cat has accidentally ingested a grape or raisin, **time is of the essence.** Follow these steps immediately:

1.  **Call Your Veterinarian:** Contact your vet or an emergency animal clinic right away.
2.  **Provide Details:** Be ready to tell the vet how many grapes were eaten and how long ago it happened.
3.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting:** Never attempt to make your cat vomit unless explicitly instructed to do so by a professional.
4.  **Monitor Closely:** Keep a close eye on their behavior and hydration levels while transporting them to the clinic.

### Safe, Bengal-Approved Alternatives
If you want to give your Bengal a healthy snack, skip the vineyard and try these cat-safe options instead:
*   **Blueberries:** A great antioxidant-rich alternative (in moderation).
*   **Cooked Pumpkin:** Excellent for digestion.
*   **Freeze-Dried Salmon:** Aligns with their carnivorous nature.
*   **Small Pieces of Cooked Chicken:** High-protein and safe.
