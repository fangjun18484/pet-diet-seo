# Can a Bengal Eat Apples? Everything You Need to Know

**Verdict: ✅ YES (In moderation)**

While Bengal cats are obligate carnivores—meaning their primary diet must consist of meat—they can safely eat small amounts of apple flesh as an occasional treat.

## Why Apples Are Safe for Bengals
The flesh of an apple is non-toxic to cats. While Bengals are known for their high energy and curious nature, their digestive systems can handle small portions of apple without issue. However, because cats lack "sweet" taste receptors, your Bengal may be more interested in the texture than the flavor.

## Potential Benefits & Risks

### The Benefits 🍎
*   **Fiber:** A small piece of apple can provide a bit of dietary fiber, which may help with digestion.
*   **Vitamin C:** Apples contain antioxidants and vitamins that support overall health.
*   **Hydration:** The high water content in apples provides a refreshing snack.

### The Risks ⚠️
*   **Cyanide Toxicity:** Apple **seeds, stems, and leaves** contain amygdalin, which releases cyanide when chewed. These are toxic to cats.
*   **Sugar Content:** Apples contain natural sugars (fructose). Excessive amounts can lead to weight gain or exacerbate diabetes in predisposed cats.
*   **Digestive Upset:** Some cats may experience diarrhea or an upset stomach if they eat too much fruit.

## How to Safely Feed Apples to Your Bengal

If you want to share a slice of apple with your Bengal, follow these safety guidelines:

1.  **Remove All Seeds and Core:** Ensure every single seed and the stem are completely removed.
2.  **Peel the Skin (Optional):** While the skin is non-toxic, some cats find it harder to digest. Peeling it reduces the risk of choking or stomach upset.
3.  **Cut into Tiny Pieces:** Offer small, bite-sized cubes to prevent choking.
4.  **Moderation is Key:** A treat should never exceed 10% of your cat's daily caloric intake. One or two small cubes are plenty.
5.  **Avoid Added Sugars:** Never feed your cat apple pie, apple sauce with added sugar, or processed apple treats.

**Pro Tip:** If your Bengal shows no interest in apples, don't force it! Many cats prefer meat-based treats or cat-safe greens like wheatgrass.
