# Can German Shepherds Eat Grapes? The Definitive Answer

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

**German Shepherds cannot eat grapes.** Whether they are red, green, seedless, or organic, grapes and raisins are highly toxic to dogs, regardless of the breed's size.

---

## Why Are Grapes Dangerous for German Shepherds?

While some foods cause mild stomach upset, grapes and raisins can cause **acute kidney failure** in dogs. 

Recent research suggests that **tartaric acid**—found in grapes—is the likely culprit. Because every dog reacts differently, there is no "safe" amount. Even a single grape can be fatal for some dogs, while others may tolerate more; however, the risk is far too high to ever attempt feeding them.

---

## The Risks: What Happens if Your GSD Eats a Grape?

If your German Shepherd consumes grapes, they are at risk of sudden renal failure. Keep a close eye on your dog for these warning signs:

*   **Vomiting and Diarrhea:** Often occurring within a few hours of ingestion.
*   **Lethargy:** Unusual tiredness or weakness.
*   **Loss of Appetite:** Refusing their favorite treats or meals.
*   **Changes in Urination:** A significant decrease in urine output (a sign that the kidneys are shutting down).
*   **Dehydration:** Dry gums and skin.

---

## What to Do if Your German Shepherd Ate Grapes

**This is a veterinary emergency.** If you suspect your dog has eaten grapes or raisins, take the following steps immediately:

1.  **Call Your Vet Immediately:** Do not wait for symptoms to appear. Early intervention is critical to preventing permanent kidney damage.
2.  **Contact Pet Poison Control:** If your vet is unavailable, call the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center.
3.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting Unless Instructed:** Only induce vomiting if your veterinarian specifically tells you to do so over the phone.
4.  **Provide Information:** Tell your vet how many grapes were eaten, the approximate time of ingestion, and your dog's current weight.
