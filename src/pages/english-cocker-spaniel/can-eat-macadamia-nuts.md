# Can an English Cocker Spaniel Eat Macadamia Nuts?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

---

## Why Macadamia Nuts Are Dangerous for Dogs
While many nuts are simply "unhealthy" due to high fat content, **macadamia nuts are toxic to dogs**, including English Cocker Spaniels. 

These nuts contain a specific toxin (though the exact substance is not fully understood) that affects a dog's nervous system. Because English Cocker Spaniels are often food-motivated and known for "scavenging," it is crucial to keep these nuts completely out of their reach.

## The Risks: What Happens if They Eat One?
Even a small amount of macadamia nuts can cause a reaction. Because of their size, a medium-sized breed like the English Cocker Spaniel may experience varying levels of toxicity depending on the quantity ingested.

**Potential symptoms include:**
*   **Rear Leg Weakness:** Difficulty walking or "wobbly" hind legs.
*   **Vomiting and Diarrhea:** General gastrointestinal distress.
*   **Lethargy:** Extreme tiredness or depression.
*   **Hyperthermia:** An increase in body temperature.
*   **Tremors:** In severe cases, muscle twitching or shaking.

## What to Do if Your English Cocker Spaniel Ate Macadamia Nuts
If you suspect your dog has ingested macadamia nuts, **treat it as a medical emergency.**

1.  **Call Your Vet Immediately:** Contact your veterinarian or a local emergency pet clinic right away.
2.  **Identify the Amount:** Try to determine how many nuts were eaten and when it happened. This helps the vet determine the risk level.
3.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting:** Never force your dog to vomit unless specifically instructed to do so by a veterinary professional.
4.  **Monitor Closely:** Watch for the symptoms listed above while you are en route to the clinic.

### Safe Alternatives for Your Pup
If you want to give your English Cocker Spaniel a crunchy, healthy treat, skip the macadamia nuts and try these dog-safe options instead:
*   **Fresh Blueberries** (Antioxidant-rich)
*   **Carrot Sticks** (Great for dental health)
*   **Apple Slices** (Seedless and skinless)
*   **Plain Cooked Pumpkin** (Great for digestion)
