# Can a Poodle Eat Grapes? Everything You Need to Know

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

**Poodles, like all dogs, should never eat grapes or raisins.**

---

## Why Are Grapes Dangerous for Poodles?
While many fruits are healthy treats, grapes and raisins are highly toxic to dogs. Even a small amount can trigger **acute kidney failure**. 

Recent research suggests that **tartaric acid** found in grapes is the likely culprit. Because every dog's sensitivity varies, there is no "safe" dose; some Poodles may react to a single grape, while others may not—but the risk of total kidney shutdown is far too high to gamble with.

---

## Potential Risks and Symptoms
If your Poodle ingests grapes, they are at risk of severe systemic toxicity. Keep a close eye out for these warning signs:

*   **Vomiting and Diarrhea:** Usually occurs shortly after ingestion.
*   **Lethargy:** Unusual tiredness or weakness.
*   **Loss of Appetite:** Refusing their favorite treats or meals.
*   **Dehydration:** Increased thirst or, conversely, a complete stop in urination (a sign of kidney failure).
*   **Abdominal Pain:** Sensitivity when touched in the stomach area.

---

## What to Do If Your Poodle Ate Grapes
**This is a veterinary emergency.** If you suspect your Poodle has eaten a grape or raisin, take the following steps immediately:

1.  **Contact Your Vet Immediately:** Call your veterinarian or the nearest emergency animal clinic.
2.  **Call Animal Poison Control:** If your vet is unavailable, contact the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center.
3.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting Unless Instructed:** Only induce vomiting if your vet specifically tells you to do so over the phone.
4.  **Provide Information:** Be ready to tell the vet how many grapes were eaten, the variety, and approximately how long ago it happened.

---

## Safe Fruit Alternatives for Poodles
Since your Poodle can't have grapes, try these vet-approved, kidney-friendly alternatives for a healthy snack:

*   **Blueberries:** Packed with antioxidants.
*   **Apple Slices:** (Remove the seeds and core first).
*   **Watermelon:** (Remove the seeds and rind).
*   **Banana:** Great in moderation.
