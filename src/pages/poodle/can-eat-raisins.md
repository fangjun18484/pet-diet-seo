# Can a Poodle Eat Raisins?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

## Why Raisins are Dangerous for Poodles
Regardless of the size or breed of your Poodle—whether it's a Toy, Miniature, or Standard—**raisins and grapes are highly toxic.** 

While the exact toxin is still being studied, recent research points to **tartaric acid** as the cause. Dogs cannot metabolize this substance, and even a small amount can lead to sudden, acute kidney failure. Because Poodles are highly active and curious, it is vital to keep these dried fruits far out of their reach.

---

## Potential Risks & Symptoms
Feeding a raisin to your Poodle isn't just a "stomach ache" risk; it is a medical emergency. The primary risk is **Acute Renal Failure (Kidney Failure).**

**Watch for these warning signs:**
*   **Vomiting:** Often occurs within a few hours of ingestion.
*   **Lethargy:** Unusual tiredness or weakness.
*   **Loss of Appetite:** Refusing their favorite treats or meals.
*   **Diarrhea:** Loose stools or gastrointestinal distress.
*   **Decreased Urination:** A critical sign that the kidneys are shutting down.

---

## What to Do If Your Poodle Ate Raisins
If your Poodle has ingested a raisin or a grape, **time is of the essence.** Do not wait for symptoms to appear, as kidney damage can happen quickly.

1.  **Call Your Vet Immediately:** Contact your primary veterinarian or the nearest emergency animal clinic.
2.  **Identify the Amount:** Try to determine exactly how many raisins your dog ate and how long ago.
3.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting Unless Instructed:** Only induce vomiting if your vet specifically tells you to do so over the phone.
4.  **Prepare for Treatment:** Your vet may administer activated charcoal to stop toxin absorption or put your Poodle on IV fluids to flush the kidneys.

---

## Safe Fruit Alternatives for Poodles
Since raisins are off-limits, treat your Poodle to these kidney-friendly, nutrient-dense snacks instead:

*   **Blueberries:** Packed with antioxidants.
*   **Apple Slices:** (Remove the seeds and core first).
*   **Banana:** A great source of potassium in moderation.
*   **Watermelon:** (Remove the seeds and rind).
