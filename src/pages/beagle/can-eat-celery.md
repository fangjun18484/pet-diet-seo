# Can a Beagle Eat Celery?

**Verdict: ✅ YES**

## Why Celery is Safe for Beagles
Celery is a non-toxic vegetable that is perfectly safe for dogs, including Beagles. Since Beagles are notorious for their appetite and can be prone to weight gain, celery serves as an excellent, low-calorie alternative to traditional store-bought treats.

---

## The Benefits & Risks

### 🌟 Potential Benefits
*   **Low Calorie:** Great for keeping your Beagle lean without sacrificing the "treat" experience.
*   **Hydration:** Celery has a high water content, helping your dog stay hydrated.
*   **Nutrient-Rich:** Provides essential vitamins A, C, and K, as well as potassium and folate.
*   **Dental Health:** The crunch can help gently clean teeth and freshen breath.

### ⚠️ Potential Risks
*   **Choking Hazard:** Celery is fibrous and stringy. Large chunks can get stuck in a dog's throat or cause intestinal blockages.
*   **Digestive Upset:** Introducing too much celery too quickly can lead to gas or loose stools.
*   **Sodium Content:** While natural celery is fine, avoid celery that has been salted or pickled.

---

## How to Safely Feed Celery to Your Beagle

To ensure your Beagle enjoys their snack safely, follow these preparation steps:

1.  **Wash Thoroughly:** Remove any dirt or pesticide residue.
2.  **Cut into Small Pieces:** Chop the celery into bite-sized, pea-sized pieces or thin slices. This eliminates the choking risk associated with long, stringy fibers.
3.  **Start Slow:** Give your Beagle one or two small pieces first to ensure they don't have a sensitive stomach.
4.  **Moderation is Key:** Celery should be a treat, not a meal replacement. It should make up no more than 10% of your dog's daily caloric intake.

**Pro Tip:** Try freezing small pieces of celery for a refreshing, cooling treat during the summer months!
