# Can a Collie Eat Blueberries?

**Verdict: ✅ YES**

## Why Blueberries Are Safe for Collies
Blueberries are non-toxic and safe for dogs of all breeds, including Collies. These "superfoods" are low in calories and high in essential nutrients, making them an excellent healthy alternative to processed store-bought treats.

## Potential Benefits & Risks

### The Benefits 🌟
Collies are active, intelligent dogs, and blueberries provide several health boosts:
*   **Rich in Antioxidants:** Anthocyanins help fight free radicals and support cognitive function (great for the high-intelligence Collie brain).
*   **Immune Support:** Packed with Vitamin C and K to keep their immune system strong.
*   **Digestive Health:** The fiber content aids in smooth digestion.
*   **Low Calorie:** A great way to reward your dog without contributing to weight gain.

### The Risks ⚠️
While safe, there are a few things to keep in mind:
*   **Sugar Content:** Like all fruits, blueberries contain natural sugars. Overfeeding can lead to weight gain or upset the glycemic balance in diabetic dogs.
*   **Choking Hazard:** While rare for a medium-to-large Collie, always supervise your dog while they eat.
*   **Allergies:** Though uncommon, some dogs may have a sensitivity to blueberries. Always introduce new foods slowly.

## How to Safely Feed Blueberries to Your Collie

To ensure your Collie enjoys their treat safely, follow these guidelines:

1.  **Wash Thoroughly:** Always rinse blueberries under cold water to remove any pesticides or residue.
2.  **Fresh or Frozen:** Both are acceptable. Frozen blueberries can be a refreshing treat during hot summer months.
3.  **Practice Moderation:** Blueberries should be treated as a **treat, not a meal**. A general rule of thumb is that treats should make up no more than 10% of your dog's daily caloric intake.
4.  **Avoid Additives:** Never feed your Collie blueberries from a pie, jam, or syrup, as these contain added sugars, artificial sweeteners (like Xylitol), or other toxic ingredients.

**Serving Suggestion:** Mix a small handful of blueberries into their regular kibble or freeze them into ice cubes for a stimulating summer snack!
