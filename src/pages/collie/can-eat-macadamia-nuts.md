# Can a Collie Eat Macadamia Nuts?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

## Why Macadamia Nuts Are Dangerous for Collies
Macadamia nuts are **toxic to all dogs**, including Collies. While the exact toxin has not been fully identified, these nuts contain compounds that affect a dog's central nervous system. Because Collies are active, sensitive breeds, any ingestion of these nuts can lead to rapid onset of toxicity.

## The Risks: What Happens if a Collie Eats Macadamia Nuts?
There are no nutritional benefits for a dog to eat macadamia nuts; the risks far outweigh any perceived gain. If your Collie consumes even a small amount, you may observe the following symptoms:

*   ** backend Weakness:** Difficulty walking or "wobbly" hind legs.
*   **Vomiting and Diarrhea:** Gastrointestinal distress is common.
*   **Lethargy:** Extreme tiredness or depression.
*   **Hyperthermia:** An increase in body temperature.
*   **Tremors:** In severe cases, muscle tremors or seizures may occur.

## What to Do If Your Collie Ate Macadamia Nuts
If you suspect your Collie has ingested macadamia nuts, **treat this as a medical emergency.**

1.  **Contact Your Vet Immediately:** Call your veterinarian or a pet poison control center right away.
2.  **Remove Access:** Ensure no more nuts are available for the dog to eat.
3.  **Gather Information:** Try to determine how many nuts were eaten and when the ingestion occurred.
4.  **Monitor Closely:** Watch for the symptoms listed above while you are en route to the clinic.

**Safe Alternatives:** If you want to give your Collie a crunchy treat, opt for **sliced carrots, apple slices (no seeds), or plain blueberries.**
