# Can a Bichon Frise Eat Grapes? 🍇

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

**A Bichon Frise cannot eat grapes. Grapes and raisins are highly toxic to all dogs, regardless of their breed or size.**

---

### Why Are Grapes Dangerous for Bichons?
While some foods are mildly irritating to dogs, grapes and raisins can be fatal. Recent research indicates that **tartaric acid** found in grapes is the likely culprit, causing sudden and acute **kidney failure** in dogs. 

Because Bichon Frises are small dogs, even a small amount of grapes can lead to a high concentration of toxins in their bloodstream, making them particularly vulnerable.

---

### Potential Risks & Symptoms
Feeding your Bichon Frise a grape—or letting them sneak one off the counter—can lead to severe health complications. Keep a close eye out for these warning signs:

*   **Vomiting and Diarrhea:** Often the first sign of toxicity.
*   **Lethargy:** Unusual tiredness or weakness.
*   **Loss of Appetite:** Refusing their favorite treats or meals.
*   **Dehydration:** Increased thirst or, conversely, a decrease in urine output (a sign of kidney failure).
*   **Weight Loss:** In cases of prolonged toxicity.

---

### What to Do If Your Bichon Ate a Grape
If you suspect your dog has ingested a grape or raisin, **treat it as a medical emergency.** Do not wait for symptoms to appear.

1.  **Call Your Veterinarian Immediately:** Contact your vet or the nearest emergency animal clinic.
2.  **Provide Details:** Tell the vet exactly how many grapes were eaten and how long ago it happened.
3.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting Unless Instructed:** Only induce vomiting if your vet specifically tells you to do so over the phone.
4.  **Monitor Closely:** Watch for any changes in behavior or physical health while on your way to the clinic.

---

### Safe, Healthy Alternatives for Your Bichon
Want to give your fluffy friend a fruity treat? Skip the grapes and try these dog-safe, kidney-friendly options:

*   **Blueberries:** Packed with antioxidants and great for small mouths.
*   **Apple Slices:** (Remove all seeds and the core).
*   **Watermelon:** (Remove seeds and rind).
*   **Banana:** A great source of potassium in moderation.
