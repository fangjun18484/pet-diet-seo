# Can a Miniature Schnauzer Eat Grapes?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

## Why Grapes are Dangerous for Miniature Schnauzers
Regardless of the breed, **grapes and raisins are highly toxic to dogs.** While some dogs may seem fine after eating one, others can experience sudden, severe reactions. In Miniature Schnauzers, as with all canines, grapes can cause **acute kidney failure**, which can be fatal if not treated immediately.

## The Risks: What Happens if They Eat Grapes?
There is no "safe" amount of grapes for a dog. The toxicity is not dose-dependent in the same way some other foods are; even a single grape can trigger a crisis in sensitive dogs.

**Potential symptoms of grape poisoning include:**
*   **Vomiting and Diarrhea:** Usually occurring within a few hours of ingestion.
*   **Lethargy:** Unusual tiredness or weakness.
*   **Loss of Appetite:** Refusing their favorite treats or meals.
*   **Dehydration:** Increased thirst or, conversely, a decrease in urine output (a sign of kidney failure).
*   **Joint Pain:** In some cases, dogs may appear stiff or reluctant to move.

## What to Do if Your Miniature Schnauzer Ate a Grape
If you suspect your dog has consumed a grape or raisin, **treat this as a medical emergency.**

1.  **Call Your Veterinarian Immediately:** Contact your vet or the nearest emergency animal clinic.
2.  **Provide Details:** Tell them how many grapes were eaten, the variety, and approximately how long ago it happened.
3.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting Unless Instructed:** Only induce vomiting if your vet specifically tells you to do so over the phone.
4.  **Seek Rapid Treatment:** Early intervention (such as activated charcoal or IV fluids) is critical to preventing permanent kidney damage.

## Safe Fruit Alternatives for Miniature Schnauzers
If you want to give your Miniature Schnauzer a healthy, fruity snack, try these vet-approved options instead:
*   **Blueberries:** Packed with antioxidants.
*   **Apple Slices:** (Remove the seeds and core first).
*   **Watermelon:** (Remove the seeds and rind).
*   **Banana:** In small moderation.
