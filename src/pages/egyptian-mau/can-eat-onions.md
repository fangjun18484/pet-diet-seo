# Can an Egyptian Mau Eat Onions?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

---

## Why Onions Are Dangerous for Egyptian Maus
Regardless of the breed, all cats—including the energetic Egyptian Mau—should **never** eat onions. 

Onions (along with garlic, shallots, and chives) contain compounds called **N-propyl disulfides**. While harmless to humans, these substances are toxic to felines. Cats lack the specific enzyme needed to break down these compounds, leading to a dangerous reaction in their bloodstream.

## The Risks: What Happens to Your Cat?
There are no benefits to feeding onions to a cat; the risks are severe and potentially fatal.

### 1. Hemolytic Anemia
The primary risk is the destruction of red blood cells. The toxins cause oxidative damage, causing the red blood cells to rupture. This prevents the blood from carrying enough oxygen to the organs.

### 2. Organ Failure
If a significant amount is ingested, the resulting anemia can lead to kidney failure or liver distress.

### 3. Common Symptoms of Onion Poisoning:
*   Lethargy and weakness
*   Pale gums (a sign of anemia)
*   Loss of appetite
*   Rapid heart rate or panting
*   Dark-colored urine (reddish or brown)

---

## What to Do If Your Egyptian Mau Ate Onions
If your cat has ingested raw, cooked, or powdered onion (including onion powder in human snacks), **treat this as a medical emergency.**

### Immediate Action Plan:
1.  **Remove the Source:** Ensure your cat cannot eat any more of the food.
2.  **Call Your Vet Immediately:** Contact your primary veterinarian or an emergency pet clinic.
3.  **Provide Details:** Be ready to tell the vet **how much** was eaten, **what form** it was in (e.g., a piece of raw onion vs. a processed treat), and **how long ago** it happened.
4.  **Monitor Symptoms:** Keep a close eye on their gum color and energy levels while transporting them to the clinic.

**Pro Tip:** Check your human food labels! Many broths, seasonings, and canned soups contain "onion powder," which is concentrated and highly toxic to cats.
