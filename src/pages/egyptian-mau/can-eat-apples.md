# Can an Egyptian Mau Eat Apples?

**Verdict: YES** (in moderation)

### Why Can Egyptian Maus Eat Apples?
Apples are non-toxic to cats, including the active and curious Egyptian Mau. While cats are obligate carnivores—meaning they don't *need* fruit in their diet—small amounts of apple can be a safe, crunchy treat that provides a bit of variety.

---

### 🍎 Potential Benefits & Risks

#### The Benefits
*   **Fiber:** Can aid in digestion for some cats.
*   **Vitamins:** Contains small amounts of Vitamin C and Vitamin A.
*   **Hydration:** Apples have a high water content, which is beneficial for feline kidney health.

#### The Risks
*   **Cyanide Toxicity:** Apple **seeds and stems** contain amygdalin, which releases cyanide when chewed. This is toxic to cats.
*   **High Sugar:** Apples contain natural sugars (fructose). Overfeeding can lead to weight gain or spike blood sugar levels in diabetic cats.
*   **Choking Hazard:** Large chunks of apple can be a choking risk for smaller cats.

---

### 🛠 How to Safely Feed Apples to Your Egyptian Mau

If you want to share a slice of apple with your Mau, follow these safety guidelines to ensure it remains a healthy snack:

1.  **Remove All Seeds and the Core:** Never feed your cat the center of the apple.
2.  **Peel the Skin (Optional):** While the skin is non-toxic, some cats find it harder to digest. Removing it reduces the risk of stomach upset.
3.  **Cut into Tiny Pieces:** Serve small, bite-sized cubes (about the size of a blueberry) to prevent choking.
4.  **Moderation is Key:** Apples should be treated as a "once-in-a-while" treat. They should make up no more than 10% of your cat's daily caloric intake.
5.  **Avoid Processed Apples:** Never feed your cat apple pie, canned apple sauce with added sugar, or apple-flavored candies.

**Pro Tip:** Since Egyptian Maus are high-energy cats, a small piece of apple can be a refreshing treat after a long play session!
