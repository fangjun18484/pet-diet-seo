# Can Egyptian Maus Eat Grapes?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

## Why Egyptian Maus Cannot Eat Grapes
Regardless of their breed, **Egyptian Maus—and all cats—must never eat grapes or raisins.** 

Grapes contain naturally occurring toxins (suspected to be tartaric acid) that can cause sudden, acute **kidney failure** in felines. Because cats have a unique metabolic system, they cannot process these compounds, leading to rapid organ dysfunction.

## The Risks: What Happens if They Do?
There are no benefits to feeding grapes to your cat; only severe health risks. Even a single grape or raisin can be toxic depending on the size of the cat and its individual sensitivity.

**Potential symptoms of grape poisoning include:**
*   **Vomiting:** Often occurs shortly after ingestion.
*   **Lethargy:** Extreme tiredness or weakness.
*   **Loss of Appetite:** Refusing food and water.
*   **Dehydration:** Drying of the gums and skin.
*   **Decreased Urination:** A primary sign that the kidneys are failing.

## What to Do If Your Egyptian Mau Ate a Grape
If your cat has ingested a grape or raisin, treat it as a **medical emergency**. Do not wait for symptoms to appear, as kidney damage can happen quickly.

1.  **Call Your Vet Immediately:** Contact your primary veterinarian or the nearest emergency animal clinic.
2.  **Provide Details:** Tell the vet exactly how many grapes were eaten and how long ago it happened.
3.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting:** Never attempt to make your cat vomit at home unless specifically instructed to do so by a professional, as this can cause aspiration.
4.  **Monitor Closely:** Watch for any signs of distress while transporting them to the clinic.

### Safe Alternatives for Your Mau
If you want to give your Egyptian Mau a healthy treat, stick to cat-safe options like:
*   **Small pieces of cooked chicken** (plain, no seasoning).
*   **Commercial cat treats** formulated for nutrition.
*   **Small bits of steamed pumpkin** (plain).
