# Can Egyptian Maus Eat Macadamia Nuts?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

## Why Macadamia Nuts Are Dangerous for Egyptian Maus
Despite the active and athletic nature of the Egyptian Mau, their digestive systems are not equipped to process the fats and toxins found in macadamia nuts. Macadamia nuts are **toxic to cats** (and dogs), regardless of the breed. 

Ingesting these nuts can lead to severe neurological and gastrointestinal distress. Because cats are obligate carnivores, their bodies are designed to process animal proteins, not the complex oils and compounds found in macadamia nuts.

## Potential Risks & Symptoms
If your Egyptian Mau consumes macadamia nuts, you may notice the following symptoms, typically appearing within 12 hours:

*   **Vomiting and Diarrhea:** The most common immediate reaction.
*   **Lethargy:** A sudden drop in energy levels and weakness in the hind legs.
*   **Hyperthermia:** An increase in body temperature.
*   **Tremors:** In severe cases, muscle tremors or disorientation may occur.

## What to Do If Your Cat Ate Macadamia Nuts
If you suspect your cat has ingested any amount of macadamia nuts, **do not wait for symptoms to appear.** Follow these steps immediately:

1.  **Remove the Source:** Ensure no more nuts are accessible to your pet.
2.  **Call Your Veterinarian:** Contact your vet or an emergency animal clinic immediately.
3.  **Provide Details:** Tell the vet approximately how many nuts were eaten and when it happened.
4.  **Monitor Closely:** Keep a close eye on their movement and breathing until you reach professional help.

### Safe Alternatives for Your Egyptian Mau
If you want to give your Mau a healthy treat, stick to cat-safe options such as:
*   Small pieces of cooked chicken or turkey (unseasoned).
*   Commercial cat treats formulated for their specific nutritional needs.
*   Small amounts of steamed pumpkin or carrots.
