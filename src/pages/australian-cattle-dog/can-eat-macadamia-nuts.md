# Can Australian Cattle Dogs Eat Macadamia Nuts?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

## Why You Must Avoid Macadamia Nuts
Regardless of their breed, including the hardy and energetic Australian Cattle Dog (ACD), **macadamia nuts are toxic to dogs.** 

While many nuts are simply hard to digest, macadamia nuts contain a specific toxin (though the exact compound is still being studied) that targets a dog's nervous system. Because Australian Cattle Dogs are high-energy working dogs, any substance that impairs their motor skills or neurological function is particularly dangerous.

## The Risks: What Happens if They Eat One?
Even a small amount of macadamia nuts can cause a reaction. Symptoms typically appear within 12 hours of ingestion and may include:

*   **Hind Leg Weakness:** Difficulty walking or "wobbly" movement (ataxia).
*   **Vomiting & Diarrhea:** Gastrointestinal distress is common.
*   **Lethargy:** A sudden drop in energy levels.
*   **Hyperthermia:** An increase in body temperature.
*   **Tremors:** In severe cases, muscle tremors or disorientation may occur.

**Are there any benefits?** 
No. There are no nutritional benefits to macadamia nuts for dogs that outweigh the significant toxic risks.

## What to Do if Your Australian Cattle Dog Ate Macadamia Nuts
If your ACD has scavenged a handful of nuts or a snack containing macadamia nuts, take the following steps immediately:

1.  **Remove Access:** Ensure no more nuts are available to your dog.
2.  **Call Your Vet:** Contact your veterinarian or an emergency pet clinic immediately.
3.  **Identify the Quantity:** Try to determine how many nuts were consumed and when.
4.  **Monitor Symptoms:** Watch for the signs of toxicity mentioned above.
5.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting:** Unless specifically instructed to do so by a veterinary professional.

### Safe Alternatives for Your ACD
If you want to give your Australian Cattle Dog a crunchy, healthy treat, try these dog-safe options instead:
*   **Carrot sticks** (Great for chewing)
*   **Apple slices** (Without seeds)
*   **Plain blueberries**
*   **Cucumber slices**
