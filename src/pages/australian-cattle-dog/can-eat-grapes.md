# Can Australian Cattle Dogs Eat Grapes? 🚫

**Verdict: NO.** 

Australian Cattle Dogs, like all dog breeds, **must never eat grapes or raisins.** These fruits are highly toxic to dogs and can lead to severe, life-threatening complications.

---

## Why Grapes are Dangerous for Your ACD
While the exact toxin in grapes was historically debated, recent research points to **tartaric acid** as the primary cause of toxicity. Unlike some foods that are only dangerous in large quantities, grapes can cause **acute kidney failure** in dogs, regardless of the amount consumed or the variety of the grape.

Because Australian Cattle Dogs are high-energy, active working dogs, maintaining optimal kidney and liver function is vital for their overall health and longevity.

---

## The Risks: What Happens if Your Dog Eats Grapes?
There are no benefits to feeding grapes to a dog; there are only severe risks. If your ACD ingests grapes or raisins, you may notice the following symptoms:

*   **Gastrointestinal Distress:** Vomiting, diarrhea, and loss of appetite.
*   **Lethargy:** Unusual tiredness or weakness.
*   **Dehydration:** Increased thirst or a sudden decrease in urination.
*   **Kidney Failure:** In severe cases, the kidneys shut down entirely, which can be fatal if not treated immediately.

---

## What to Do if Your Australian Cattle Dog Ate Grapes
If your dog has accidentally ingested a grape or a raisin, **treat this as a medical emergency.**

1.  **Call Your Vet Immediately:** Contact your veterinarian or the nearest emergency animal clinic.
2.  **Provide Details:** Tell the vet approximately how many grapes were eaten, the variety, and how long ago it happened.
3.  **Do Not Induce Vomiting Unless Instructed:** Only induce vomiting if your vet specifically tells you to do so over the phone.
4.  **Seek Immediate Treatment:** Your vet may administer activated charcoal or IV fluids to help flush the toxins from your dog's system and protect the kidneys.

---

## Safe, Healthy Alternatives for Your ACD
If you want to give your Australian Cattle Dog a fruity treat, skip the grapes and try these vet-approved, kidney-safe options:

*   **Blueberries:** Packed with antioxidants and great for brain health.
*   **Apple Slices:** (Remove the seeds and core first).
*   **Watermelon:** (Remove the seeds and rind).
*   **Banana:** A great source of potassium in moderation.
