# Can a Pekingese Eat Strawberries?

**Verdict: ✅ YES**

Pekingese dogs can safely eat strawberries. These vibrant red berries are non-toxic and can be a healthy, low-calorie treat for your royal companion when fed in moderation.

---

## Why Strawberries Are Safe for Pekingese
Strawberries are naturally free of the toxins found in grapes or raisins, making them a dog-friendly fruit. While they are safe, it is important to remember that treats—including fruit—should only make up about **10% of your dog's daily caloric intake**.

## Potential Benefits & Risks

### The Benefits 🍓
*   **Antioxidants:** Strawberries are rich in antioxidants that support overall health and combat free radicals.
*   **Vitamin C:** Helps boost the immune system.
*   **Fiber:** Promotes healthy digestion.
*   **Low Calorie:** A great alternative to processed store-bought treats for weight management.

### The Risks ⚠️
*   **Natural Sugars:** Strawberries contain fructose. While safe, too many can lead to weight gain or blood sugar spikes, which is a concern for dogs with diabetes.
*   **Digestive Upset:** Some dogs may experience mild stomach upset if they are introduced to new fruits too quickly.
*   **Choking Hazard:** Because Pekingese are a small breed with a specific facial structure, large chunks of fruit can pose a choking risk.

---

## How to Safely Feed Strawberries to Your Pekingese

To ensure your Pekingese enjoys their treat safely, follow these preparation steps:

1.  **Wash Thoroughly:** Rinse the berries under cool water to remove any pesticides or dirt.
2.  **Remove the Greens:** Strip away the stem and all green leaves, as these can be difficult to digest.
3.  **Cut into Tiny Pieces:** Since Pekingese have small mouths, dice the strawberry into **small, bite-sized pieces** to prevent choking.
4.  **Start Slow:** Give your dog one small piece first to ensure they don't have an allergic reaction or a sensitive stomach.
5.  **Moderation is Key:** Limit treats to 1–2 small pieces a day.

### 🚫 What to Avoid
**Never** feed your Pekingese strawberries that have been processed with:
*   Added sugar (syrups)
*   Artificial sweeteners (especially **Xylitol**, which is toxic to dogs)
*   Whipped cream or chocolate coatings
