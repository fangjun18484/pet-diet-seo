# Can a Pekingese Eat Macadamia Nuts?

**Verdict: ❌ NO**

**Do not feed macadamia nuts to your Pekingese.** Macadamia nuts are toxic to dogs, regardless of their breed or size. Even a small amount can cause significant health issues for your pet.

---

### Why are Macadamia Nuts Dangerous for Pekingese?
While many nuts are simply high in fat, macadamia nuts contain a specific toxin (though the exact compound is not fully understood) that affects a dog's nervous system. Because Pekingese are small dogs, their lower body mass makes them even more susceptible to the effects of toxicity than larger breeds.

### Potential Risks & Symptoms
If your Pekingese ingests macadamia nuts, you may notice symptoms appearing within **12 hours**. Watch for the following:

*   **Loss of Balance:** Your dog may appear "drunk" or stumble while walking (ataxia).
*   **Lethargy:** Extreme tiredness or sudden weakness.
*   **Vomiting:** Frequent nausea or stomach upset.
*   **Hyperthermia:** An increase in body temperature.
*   **Tremors:** In severe cases, muscle tremors or seizures may occur.

---

### What to Do If Your Pekingese Ate Macadamia Nuts
Since there is no "safe" way to prepare macadamia nuts for dogs, you must act quickly if an accidental ingestion occurs.

#### 🚩 Immediate Action Plan:
1.  **Remove Access:** Immediately take the nuts away from your dog to prevent further consumption.
2.  **Call Your Vet:** Contact your veterinarian or an emergency animal clinic immediately.
3.  **Identify the Amount:** Try to determine exactly how many nuts were eaten and when. This helps the vet determine the level of toxicity.
4.  **Monitor Closely:** Keep a close eye on your dog for any of the symptoms listed above while you are en route to the clinic.

**Pro Tip:** To keep your Pekingese safe, store all nuts in airtight, high-shelf containers where your curious pup cannot reach them!
